**Central idea.** Write $F_Z(z)$ as an iterated integral over the joint density $f_X(x)f_Y(y)$, then differentiate under the integral sign (cf. the convolution formula (5.2.3)).

**Solution.** (a) $F_Z(z) = P(X - Y \leq z) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} P(X \leq z + y) f_Y(y) \, dy = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} F_X(z+y) f_Y(y) \, dy$. Differentiating,
$$f_Z(z) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f_X(z+y) f_Y(y) \, dy = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f_X(x) f_Y(x-z) \, dx$$
(substituting $x = z + y$).
(b) For $z$ fixed, $P(XY \leq z) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} P(X \leq z/y) f_Y(y) \, dy$ requires care with the sign of $y$; differentiating the joint integral $\iint_{xy \leq z} f_X(x)f_Y(y)\,dx\,dy$ with $u = xy$ gives
$$f_Z(z) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{1}{|y|} f_X(z/y) f_Y(y) \, dy = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{1}{|x|} f_X(x) f_Y(z/x) \, dx.$$
(c) Similarly, with $u = x/y$,
$$f_Z(z) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} |y|\, f_X(zy) f_Y(y) \, dy.$$
(These are the standard difference/product/ratio convolution formulas; (5.2.3) is the special case $Z = X + Y$.)
