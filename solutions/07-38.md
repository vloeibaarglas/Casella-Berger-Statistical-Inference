**Central idea.** The CRLB is attained iff the score is affine in $\delta-g(\theta)$; read off $g$ from the score's linear structure.
**Solution.** (a) Yes: $\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta}\log L=\frac{n}{\theta}+\sum\log X_i=-n\big(\delta-\frac1\theta\big)$ with $\delta=-\frac1n\sum\log X_i$. Since $-\log X_1\sim\mathrm{exponential}(\theta)$, $E\delta=1/\theta=:g(\theta)$, $\mathrm{Var}(\delta)=\frac{1}{n\theta^2}=\frac{[g'(\theta)]^2}{nI(\theta)}$ (as $I(\theta)=1/\theta^2$): the CRLB is attained for $g(\theta)=1/\theta$.
(b) Yes: $\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta}\log L=\frac{n}{\theta\log\theta}-\frac{n}{\theta-1}+\frac{\sum x_i}{\theta}=\frac{n}{\theta}\big(\bar x-g(\theta)\big)$ with $g(\theta)=\frac{\theta}{\theta-1}-\frac{1}{\log\theta}$. Indeed $E_\theta X_1=\frac{\log\theta}{\theta-1}\int_0^1x\theta^xdx=\frac{\theta}{\theta-1}-\frac{1}{\log\theta}=g(\theta)$, so $\bar X$ is unbiased for $g(\theta)$ and, the score being affine in $\bar X-g(\theta)$, attains the CRLB.
