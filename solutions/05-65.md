**Central idea.** Conditionally on the $Y$'s, $P(X^*\leq x) = \frac{\frac1m\sum w_i\mathbf{1}_{\{Y_i \leq x\}}}{\frac1m\sum w_j}$; numerator $\to_p \int_{-\infty}^x f = F(x)$, denominator $\to_p 1$, so $X^*\to_d f$ — and $r$ such resamples are asymptotically iid from $f$.

**Solution.** Conditionally on $Y_1,\ldots,Y_m$,
$$P(X^*\leq x \mid Y_1,\ldots,Y_m) = \sum_{i=1}^{m} q_i\mathbf{1}_{\{Y_i \leq x\}} = \frac{\frac1m\sum_{i=1}^{m}w_i\mathbf{1}_{\{Y_i \leq x\}}}{\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^{m}w_j}, \quad w_i = \frac{f(Y_i)}{g(Y_i)}.$$
By the WLLN, the numerator $\to_p E_g[w(Y)\mathbf{1}_{\{Y \leq x\}}] = \int_{-\infty}^{x}f(t)\,dt = F(x)$ and the denominator $\to_p E_g[w(Y)] = 1$. Hence $P(X^*\leq x \mid Y\text{'s}) \to_p F(x)$, i.e., $X^*\to_d f$. Drawing $X_1^*,\ldots,X_r^*$ iid from this conditional discrete law, they are conditionally independent given the $Y$'s with marginals $\to_d f$ — approximately an iid sample from $f$ for large $m$.
