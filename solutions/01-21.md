**Central idea.** No matching pair means the $2r$ shoes come from $2r$ distinct pairs — choose the pairs, then choose left/right from each.

**Solution.** Total: $\binom{2n}{2r}$. Favorable: choose which $2r$ pairs contribute ($\binom{n}{2r}$), then for each pair choose one of its 2 shoes ($2^{2r}$). Hence $P = \binom{n}{2r}2^{2r}/\binom{2n}{2r}$.
