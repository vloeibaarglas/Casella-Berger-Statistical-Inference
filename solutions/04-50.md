**Central idea.** Condition on $X$: $\mathrm{E}[XY] = \mathrm{E}[X\cdot\mathrm{E}(Y|X)] = \rho\mathrm{E}[X^2]$; and $\mathrm{E}[X^2Y^2] = \mathrm{E}[X^2(\rho^2X^2+1-\rho^2)]$ using $Y|X=x \sim n(\rho x,1-\rho^2)$.

**Solution.** Here $X,Y$ are standard normal marginally with $Y|X=x \sim n(\rho x,1-\rho^2)$. $\mathrm{E}[XY] = \mathrm{E}[X\cdot\rho X] = \rho$, so $\mathrm{Corr}(X,Y) = \rho$. $\mathrm{E}[X^2Y^2] = \mathrm{E}[X^2\mathrm{E}(Y^2|X)] = \mathrm{E}[X^2(\rho^2X^2+1-\rho^2)] = 3\rho^2+(1-\rho^2) = 1+2\rho^2$ (using $\mathrm{E}[X^4]=3$). With $\mathrm{E}[X^2]=\mathrm{E}[Y^2]=1$, $\mathrm{Var}(X^2)=2$: $\mathrm{Corr}(X^2,Y^2) = \frac{1+2\rho^2-1}{2} = \rho^2$.
