**Central idea.** Disjointness would force $P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) > 1$, contradicting the axioms.

**Solution.** $P(B) = 1 - 1/4 = 3/4$. If $A$ and $B$ were disjoint, $P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) = 1/3 + 3/4 = 13/12 > 1$, impossible since probabilities are at most 1. Hence $A$ and $B$ cannot be disjoint.
