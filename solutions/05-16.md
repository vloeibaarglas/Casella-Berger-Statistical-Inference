**Central idea.** Standardize first: $Z_i = (X_i - i)/i \sim$ iid $n(0,1)$; then use the definitions $\chi^2_p = \sum Z_i^2$, $t_p = Z/\sqrt{\chi^2_p/p}$, $F_{p,q} = (\chi^2_p/p)/(\chi^2_q/q)$.

**Solution.** Put $Z_i = (X_i - i)/i$; the $Z_i$ are iid $n(0,1)$.
(a) $Z_1^2 + Z_2^2 + Z_3^2 = \sum_{i=1}^{3}\left(\frac{X_i - i}{i}\right)^2 \sim \chi^2_3$.
(b) $\dfrac{Z_1}{\sqrt{(Z_2^2+Z_3^2)/2}} \sim t_2$ (numerator $n(0,1)$ independent of denominator $\sqrt{\chi^2_2/2}$).
(c) $\dfrac{Z_1^2/1}{(Z_2^2+Z_3^2)/2} = \dfrac{2(X_1-1)^2}{\big(\frac{X_2-2}{2}\big)^2 + \big(\frac{X_3-3}{3}\big)^2} \sim F_{1,2}$.
