**Central idea.** Add and subtract $\mathrm{E}(Y|X)$ inside the square; the cross term vanishes by iterated expectation, leaving a sum of a $g$-free term and a nonnegative term minimized at $g = \mathrm{E}(Y|X)$.

**Solution.** (a) $\mathrm{E}(Y-g(X))^2 = \mathrm{E}[(Y-\mathrm{E}(Y|X)) + (\mathrm{E}(Y|X)-g(X))]^2 = \mathrm{E}(Y-\mathrm{E}(Y|X))^2 + \mathrm{E}(\mathrm{E}(Y|X)-g(X))^2$, because the cross term is $2\,\mathrm{E}[(\mathrm{E}(Y|X)-g(X))\cdot\mathrm{E}[Y-\mathrm{E}(Y|X)|X]] = 0$. The second term is $\ge 0$ and equals $0$ for $g(x) = \mathrm{E}(Y|X=x)$, which attains the minimum.
(b) Equation (2.2.4), $\min_a \mathrm{E}(Y-a)^2 = \mathrm{Var}\,Y$, is the case where $X$ is degenerate: $g$ ranges over constants $a$ and $\mathrm{E}(Y|X) = \mathrm{E}Y$.
