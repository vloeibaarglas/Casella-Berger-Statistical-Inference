**Central idea.** Cauchy–Schwarz gives $(\sum x_i)^2\le n\sum x_i^2$, so the "ratio of sums" estimator has variance at least as large as the MLE's.
**Solution.** (a) $E[\sum Y_i/\sum x_i]=\beta\sum x_i/\sum x_i=\beta$.
(b) $\mathrm{Var}(\sum Y_i/\sum x_i)=n\sigma^2/(\sum x_i)^2$, versus $\mathrm{Var}(\hat\beta_{\mathrm{MLE}})=\sigma^2/\sum x_i^2$. By Cauchy–Schwarz, $(\sum x_i)^2\le n\sum x_i^2$, so $n\sigma^2/(\sum x_i)^2\ge\sigma^2/\sum x_i^2$, with equality iff all $x_i$ are equal. The MLE has uniformly smaller variance.
