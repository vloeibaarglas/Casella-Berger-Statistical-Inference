**Central idea.** $\mathrm{Var}(\bar X)$ sums all pairwise covariances; persistent correlation leaves an $O(1)$ term, while geometrically decaying correlation contributes only $O(1/n)$.

**Solution.** (a) $\mathrm{Var}(\bar X) = \frac{1}{n^2}\big[n\sigma^2+n(n-1)\rho\sigma^2\big] = \frac{\sigma^2}{n}+\frac{n-1}{n}\rho\sigma^2\to\rho\sigma^2\ne 0$.
(b) *As printed the formula has a sign error (verified against direct summation): the "$+$" before $\frac{1-\rho^n}{1-\rho}$ should be "$-$".* Indeed $\sum_{i<j}\rho^{|i-j|} = \sum_{k=1}^{n-1}(n-k)\rho^k = \frac{\rho\big[(n-1)-n\rho+\rho^n\big]}{(1-\rho)^2} = \frac{\rho}{1-\rho}\left(n-\frac{1-\rho^n}{1-\rho}\right)$ (check $n=2$: both sides equal $\rho$; the printed "$+$" version gives $\rho(3-2\rho-\rho^2)/(1-\rho)^2\ne\rho$). The correct formula is
$$\mathrm{Var}(\bar X) = \frac{\sigma^2}{n} + \frac{2\sigma^2}{n^2}\frac{\rho}{1-\rho}\left(n-\frac{1-\rho^n}{1-\rho}\right),$$
which is $O(1/n)\to 0$ since the bracket is $O(n)$.
(c) Iterating, $X_i = \rho^{i-1}X_1+\sum_{j=1}^{i-1}\rho^{i-1-j}\delta_j$. In stationarity $\mathrm{Var}(X_1)=\rho^2\mathrm{Var}(X_1)+1$, i.e. $\mathrm{Var}(X_1)=1/(1-\rho^2)=\sigma^2$, and the $\delta_j$ are independent of $X_1$, so $\mathrm{Cov}(X_1,X_i)=\rho^{i-1}\sigma^2$, giving $\mathrm{Corr}(X_1,X_i)=\rho^{i-1}$.
