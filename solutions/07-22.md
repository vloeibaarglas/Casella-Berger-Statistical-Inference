**Central idea.** $\bar X\mid\theta\sim\mathrm{n}(\theta,\sigma^2/n)$ and $\theta\sim\mathrm{n}(\mu,\tau^2)$: multiply, complete the square in $\theta$ — the normal–normal conjugate update.
**Solution.** (a) $f(\bar x,\theta)=f(\bar x\mid\theta)\pi(\theta)=\mathrm{n}(\bar x\mid\theta,\sigma^2/n)\cdot\mathrm{n}(\theta\mid\mu,\tau^2)$.
(b) $\bar X=\theta+(\bar X-\theta)$ with $\theta\sim\mathrm{n}(\mu,\tau^2)$ independent of $\bar X-\theta\sim\mathrm{n}(0,\sigma^2/n)$; hence $m$ is $\mathrm{n}(\mu,\sigma^2/n+\tau^2)$.
(c) $\pi(\theta\mid\bar x)\propto\exp\{-\frac{n}{2\sigma^2}(\bar x-\theta)^2-\frac{1}{2\tau^2}(\theta-\mu)^2\}$; completing the square in $\theta$ gives a normal with precision $n/\sigma^2+1/\tau^2$ and mean $\frac{\tau^2}{\tau^2+\sigma^2/n}\bar x+\frac{\sigma^2/n}{\tau^2+\sigma^2/n}\mu$, i.e. (7.2.10).
