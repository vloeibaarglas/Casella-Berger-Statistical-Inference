**Central idea.** The likelihood is constant on a rectangle-shaped support; the support's four edges are exactly the four extreme order statistics.

**Solution.** The joint pdf is
$$\frac{1}{[(\theta_3-\theta_1)(\theta_4-\theta_2)]^n}\, I(\theta_1 < x_{(1)},\ x_{(n)} < \theta_3)\, I(\theta_2 < y_{(1)},\ y_{(n)} < \theta_4),$$
where $x_{(1)} = \min_i x_i$, etc. This is $g\big(\min_i X_i,\ \min_i Y_i,\ \max_i X_i,\ \max_i Y_i \mid \theta_1,\ldots,\theta_4\big)\cdot 1$, so $\big(\min_i X_i,\ \min_i Y_i,\ \max_i X_i,\ \max_i Y_i\big)$ is a four-dimensional sufficient statistic.
