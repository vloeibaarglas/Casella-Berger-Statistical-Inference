**Central idea.** $\{X=k\}$ is the first $k$ flips agreeing and flip $k+1$ breaking the run — a two-sided geometric law.

**Solution.** $P(X=k)=p^k(1-p)+(1-p)^kp$, $k=1,2,\dots$ (a run of $k$ heads broken by a tail, or $k$ tails broken by a head). This sums to $(1-p)\frac{p}{1-p}+p\frac{1-p}{p}=1$. Using $\sum_{k\ge1}kr^k=r/(1-r)^2$,
$$EX=(1-p)\frac{p}{(1-p)^2}+p\frac{1-p}{p^2}=\frac{p}{1-p}+\frac{1-p}{p}.$$
