**Central idea.** Least squares on the transformed scale is minimized when $h(a)$ is the ordinary mean of the $h(x_i)$; for an exponential family the likelihood equation is $\sum h(x_i)=nH'(\theta)$, i.e. the same generalized mean.
**Solution.** (a) $\frac{d}{da}\sum[h(x_i)-h(a)]^2=-2h'(a)\sum[h(x_i)-h(a)]=0$ gives (for $h'\ne 0$) $h(a)=\frac1n\sum h(x_i)$, i.e. $a=h^{-1}(\frac1n\sum h(x_i))$ (a minimum by convexity in $h(a)$).
(b) $h(x)=x$: $a=\bar x$. $h(x)=\log x$: $a=\exp(\frac1n\sum\log x_i)=(\prod x_i)^{1/n}$, the geometric mean. $h(x)=1/x$: $a=[\frac1n\sum x_i^{-1}]^{-1}$, the harmonic mean.
(c) The Box–Cox transform is (a monotone function of) $x^\lambda$; (a) gives $a=[\frac1n\sum x_i^\lambda]^{1/\lambda}$, the generalized mean with $h(x)=x^\lambda$.
(d) $\log X_i\sim\mathrm{n}(\mu,\sigma^2)$, so $\hat\mu=\frac1n\sum\log X_i$; by equivariance the MLE of $e^\mu$ (the median) is $\exp(\hat\mu)=(\prod X_i)^{1/n}$, the geometric mean.
(e)(i) $\log L=\theta\sum h(x_i)-nH(\theta)+\sum\log g(x_i)$; FOC: $\sum h(x_i)-nH'(\theta)=0$. Since $H'=h$, $h(\hat\theta)=\frac1n\sum h(x_i)$, so $\hat\theta=h^{-1}(\frac1n\sum h(x_i))$ ($h$ increasing makes this the maximizer).
(ii) Normal $\mathrm{n}(\theta,1)$: $f=\exp\{\theta x-\theta^2/2\}g(x)$, $h(x)=x=H'(x)$; $\hat\theta=\bar x$ (arithmetic mean). Inverted gamma: $f=\exp\{-\theta(1/x)+\log\theta\}x^{-2}$, i.e. with sign change $h(x)=-1/x$, $H(\theta)=-\log\theta$, $H'(\theta)=-1/\theta=h(\theta)$; $\hat\theta=h^{-1}(-\frac1n\sum x_i^{-1})=n/\sum x_i^{-1}$ (harmonic mean).
