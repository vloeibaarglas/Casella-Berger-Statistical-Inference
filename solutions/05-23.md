**Central idea.** Condition on $X = x$: $Z \mid X=x$ has pdf $x(1-z)^{x-1}$; mixing over $x$ with weights $c/x!$ sums the exponential series in closed form.

**Solution.** Given $X = x$, $Z$ is the minimum of $x$ iid uniforms: $f_{Z\mid X}(z \mid x) = x(1-z)^{x-1}$, $0 < z < 1$. Hence
$$f_Z(z) = \sum_{x=1}^{\infty} x(1-z)^{x-1}\cdot\frac{c}{x!} = c\sum_{x=1}^{\infty}\frac{(1-z)^{x-1}}{(x-1)!} = c\,e^{\,1-z} = \frac{e^{1-z}}{e-1}, \qquad 0 < z < 1,$$
a truncated-exponential-type density (it integrates to 1 over $(0,1)$).
