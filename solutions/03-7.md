**Central idea.** $P(X \ge 2) = 1-e^{-\lambda}(1+\lambda)$ is increasing in $\lambda$; invert it at $0.99$.

**Solution.** $1-e^{-\lambda}(1+\lambda) > 0.99 \iff e^{-\lambda}(1+\lambda) < 0.01$. The left side decreases in $\lambda$; solving $e^{-\lambda}(1+\lambda) = 0.01$ gives $\lambda \approx 6.64$ (at $\lambda = 6.6$ the left side is $0.01034$; at $\lambda = 6.64$ it is $0.00999$). The smallest mean ensuring the requirement is $\lambda \approx 6.64$.
