**Central idea.** The interval is calibrated for the mean $\theta$, not for a new observation: $X_{n+1} - \bar X$ has variance $1 + 1/n$, strictly larger than the $1/n$ the interval assumes.

**Solution.** $p = P_\theta(|X_{n+1} - \bar X| \le 1.96/\sqrt{n})$. Since $X_{n+1} - \bar X \sim n(0, 1 + 1/n)$,
$$p = P\left(|Z| \le \frac{1.96}{\sqrt{1+1/n}}\right) < P(|Z| \le 1.96) = .95,$$
so $p$ is less than $.95$.
