**Central idea.** The $\chi^2_{n-1}$ likelihood kernel $(\sigma^2)^{-(n-1)/2}\exp(-(n-1)S^2/(2\sigma^2))$ multiplies the IG prior kernel — conjugacy just adds exponents.
**Solution.** $\pi(\sigma^2\mid\mathbf{x})\propto(\sigma^2)^{-(n-1)/2}e^{-(n-1)S^2/(2\sigma^2)}\cdot(\sigma^2)^{-(\alpha+1)}e^{-1/(\beta\sigma^2)}=(\sigma^2)^{-(\alpha+(n-1)/2+1)}\exp\{-[\tfrac{(n-1)S^2}{2}+\tfrac1\beta]/\sigma^2\}$, i.e. $\mathrm{IG}(\alpha+\frac{n-1}{2},[\frac{(n-1)S^2}{2}+\frac1\beta]^{-1})$ in the stated parameterization (shape $\alpha'=\alpha+(n-1)/2$, "scale" $1/\beta'=\frac{(n-1)S^2}{2}+\frac1\beta$). Since $E[\sigma^2]=\beta'^{-1}/(\alpha'-1)$ for this parameterization, the Bayes estimator (posterior mean) is $\dfrac{\frac{(n-1)S^2}{2}+\frac1\beta}{\alpha+\frac{n-1}{2}-1}$.
