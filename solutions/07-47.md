**Central idea.** With $X_i\sim\mathrm{n}(\rho,\sigma^2)$ ($\sigma^2$ known), $\bar X$ is complete sufficient; unbias $\pi\bar X^2$ by subtracting $\pi\sigma^2/n$.
**Solution.** Write $X_i=\rho+\varepsilon_i$, $\varepsilon_i\sim\mathrm{n}(0,\sigma^2)$ iid, $\sigma^2$ known; the area is $\tau(\rho)=\pi\rho^2$. Since $E_\rho\bar X^2=\rho^2+\sigma^2/n$, $\delta=\pi(\bar X^2-\sigma^2/n)$ is unbiased for $\pi\rho^2$. $\bar X$ is complete sufficient for $\rho$, and $\delta$ is a function of $\bar X$, so by Lehmann–Scheffé $\delta$ is the best (UMVUE) unbiased estimator of the area.
