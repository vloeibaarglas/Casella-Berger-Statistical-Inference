**Central idea.** Split the incomplete-data log-likelihood into the EM objective $Q$ minus a Kullback–Leibler-type term; the M-step raises $Q$ while Jensen shows the KL term cannot rise — so the likelihood never decreases.
**Solution.** (a) From (7.2.19), $L(\theta\mid\mathbf{y},\mathbf{x})=L(\theta\mid\mathbf{y})\,k(\mathbf{x}\mid\theta,\mathbf{y})$. Taking logs and then $E[\cdot\mid\hat\theta^{(r)},\mathbf{y}]$ gives the decomposition (the $k$-term is the conditional entropy term). The M-step chooses $\hat\theta^{(r+1)}$ to maximize $Q(\theta\mid\hat\theta^{(r)},\mathbf{y})=E[\log L(\theta\mid\mathbf{y},\mathbf{X})\mid\hat\theta^{(r)},\mathbf{y}]$, hence the inequality; it is an equality iff $\hat\theta^{(r)}$ already maximizes $Q$ (e.g. at a fixed point/convergence).
(b) The difference of the two $k$-expectations is $E\big[\log\frac{k(\mathbf{X}\mid\hat\theta^{(r+1)},\mathbf{y})}{k(\mathbf{X}\mid\hat\theta^{(r)},\mathbf{y})}\bigm|\hat\theta^{(r)},\mathbf{y}\big]\le\log E\big[\frac{k(\mathbf{X}\mid\hat\theta^{(r+1)},\mathbf{y})}{k(\mathbf{X}\mid\hat\theta^{(r)},\mathbf{y})}\bigm|\hat\theta^{(r)},\mathbf{y}\big]=\log 1=0$ by Jensen (concavity of $\log$), using the hint with $f=k(\cdot\mid\hat\theta^{(r+1)},\mathbf{y})$ and $g=k(\cdot\mid\hat\theta^{(r)},\mathbf{y})$. Thus the $k$-term does not increase, while $Q$ does not decrease, so $\log L(\hat\theta^{(r+1)}\mid\mathbf{y})\ge\log L(\hat\theta^{(r)}\mid\mathbf{y})$: the EM sequence is monotone.
