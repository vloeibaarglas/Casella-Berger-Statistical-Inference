**Central idea.** With only two parameter values, compare the two likelihood values directly — the MLE is the one giving the larger likelihood.
**Solution.** For $\mathbf{x}\in(0,1)^n$, $L(0\mid\mathbf{x})=1$ and $L(1\mid\mathbf{x})=\prod_{i=1}^n(2\sqrt{x_i})^{-1}=2^{-n}(\prod x_i)^{-1/2}$. Hence $L(1\mid\mathbf{x})>L(0\mid\mathbf{x})$ iff $(\prod x_i)^{-1/2}>2^n$ iff $\prod_{i=1}^n x_i<4^{-n}$. So $\hat\theta=1$ if $\prod X_i<4^{-n}$ and $\hat\theta=0$ otherwise (either value may be taken on the null set where equality holds).
