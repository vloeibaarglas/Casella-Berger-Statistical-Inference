**Central idea.** The profile likelihood over $\theta \le \theta_0$ is flat at the unconstrained MLE when $\bar Y \le \theta_0$ and pinned at $\theta_0$ otherwise, so the likelihood ratio is a decreasing function of $\sum Y_i$ on the rejection side.

**Solution.** With $S = \sum Y_i$, $L(\theta \mid \mathbf y) = \theta^S(1-\theta)^{m-S}$. The unconstrained MLE is $\hat\theta = S/m$. The numerator of $\lambda(\mathbf y)$ maximizes over $\theta \le \theta_0$: if $S/m \le \theta_0$ the maximum is at $\hat\theta$ (so $\lambda = 1$); if $S/m > \theta_0$ the log-likelihood, concave in $\theta$, is maximized at the boundary $\theta_0$. Hence, for $S > m\theta_0$,
$$\lambda(\mathbf y) = \frac{\theta_0^S(1-\theta_0)^{m-S}}{(S/m)^S(1-S/m)^{m-S}},$$
and $\log \lambda = S\log\frac{\theta_0 m}{S} + (m-S)\log\frac{(1-\theta_0)m}{m-S}$, whose derivative in $S$ is $\log\frac{\theta_0(1-S/m)}{(1-\theta_0)(S/m)} < 0$ for $S > m\theta_0$. So $\lambda$ equals $1$ for $S \le m\theta_0$ and is strictly decreasing in $S$ for $S > m\theta_0$; thus $\{\lambda(\mathbf y) < c\} = \{S > b\}$ for a suitable $b$.
