**Central idea.** Standardize: $Z=(X-\mu)/\sigma$ has pdf $f^*(z)=\sigma f(\sigma z+\mu)$ with mean 0 and variance 1; it generates the location–scale family.

**Solution.** Let $Z=(X-\mu)/\sigma$; its pdf is $f^*(z)=\sigma f(\sigma z+\mu)$. Then $\int f^*=1$, $EZ=\int z\sigma f(\sigma z+\mu)dz=\frac{1}{\sigma}E[X-\mu]=0$, and Var$Z=\frac{1}{\sigma^2}\text{Var}\,X=1$. The location–scale family $\frac1\tau f^*\!\left(\frac{x-\nu}{\tau}\right)$ based on $f^*$ has standard pdf $f^*$ with mean 0 and variance 1.
