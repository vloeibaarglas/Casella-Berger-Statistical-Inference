**Central idea.** $\mathrm{E}[XY] = \mathrm{E}[X\,\mathrm{E}(Y|X)]$ by iterated expectation (given $X$, $X$ is constant) — this makes the residual $Y-\mathrm{E}(Y|X)$ orthogonal to $X$ and gives the variance decomposition.

**Solution.** (a) $\mathrm{E}[XY] = \mathrm{E}[\mathrm{E}(XY|X)] = \mathrm{E}[X\,\mathrm{E}(Y|X)]$, and $\mathrm{E}[\mathrm{E}(Y|X)] = \mathrm{E}Y$, so $\mathrm{E}[XY]-\mathrm{E}X\,\mathrm{E}Y = \mathrm{E}[X\,\mathrm{E}(Y|X)]-\mathrm{E}X\,\mathrm{E}[\mathrm{E}(Y|X)]$.
(b) $\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y-\mathrm{E}(Y|X)) = \mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)-\mathrm{Cov}(X,\mathrm{E}(Y|X)) = 0$ by (a).
(c) $\mathrm{E}[Y-\mathrm{E}(Y|X)] = 0$, so $\mathrm{Var}(Y-\mathrm{E}(Y|X)) = \mathrm{E}[(Y-\mathrm{E}(Y|X))^2] = \mathrm{E}[\mathrm{E}[(Y-\mathrm{E}(Y|X))^2|X]] = \mathrm{E}[\mathrm{Var}(Y|X)]$.
