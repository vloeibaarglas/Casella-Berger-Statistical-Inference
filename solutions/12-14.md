**Central idea.** Each curve is an S-shape through $(-\alpha/\beta,\,1/2)=(-1,\,1/2)$ with slope $\beta/4$ there; larger $\alpha=\beta$ steepens the transition.

**Solution.** With $\pi(x)=e^{\alpha+\beta x}/(1+e^{\alpha+\beta x})$, all three curves pass through $(-1,1/2)$ and increase from 0 to 1, getting steeper as $\alpha=\beta$ grows (slope at $x=-1$ is $\beta\pi(1-\pi)=\beta/4$):
