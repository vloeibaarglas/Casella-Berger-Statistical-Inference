**Central idea.** For small $p$, $pX_i\;\dot\sim\;$Gamma$(r,1)$ so $2p\sum X_i\;\dot\sim\;\chi^2_{2nr}$; shortest intervals put equal density (not equal tail) at the endpoints.

**Solution.** (a) By Exercise 2.38, as $p\to 0$, $pX_i\xrightarrow{d}$ Gamma$(r,1)$; hence $p\sum_{i=1}^n X_i \xrightarrow{d}$ Gamma$(nr,1)$ and $2p\sum X_i \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{2nr}$. Therefore $P\!\left(\chi^2_{2nr,1-\alpha/2}\le 2p\sum X_i\le\chi^2_{2nr,\alpha/2}\right)\approx 1-\alpha$; solving for $p$ gives the displayed interval.
(b) For $1-\alpha$ coverage with upper-$\gamma$ and upper-$(1-\alpha+\gamma)$ quantiles, the interval is $\big[\chi^2_{2nr,1-\alpha+\gamma}/(2\sum x),\,\chi^2_{2nr,\gamma}/(2\sum x)\big]$ with length $\propto \chi^2_{2nr,\gamma}-\chi^2_{2nr,1-\alpha+\gamma}$. Minimizing in $\gamma\in[0,\alpha]$: $\frac{d}{d\gamma}\chi^2_{2nr,\gamma} = 1/f(\chi^2_{2nr,\gamma})$ (quantile derivative), so the first-order condition is $f(\chi^2_{2nr,\gamma}) = f(\chi^2_{2nr,1-\alpha+\gamma})$ — equal $\chi^2_{2nr}$ density at the two endpoints (not equal tails). Solve numerically; e.g. $nr=10$, $\alpha=0.05$: $\gamma^*=0.0372$ (vs.\ equal-tail $0.025$), length $\propto 24.02$ vs.\ $24.58$.
