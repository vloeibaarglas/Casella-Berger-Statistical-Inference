**Central idea.** Enumerate all $6^6 = 46656$ ordered samples (the natural reading: sample size $=$ collection size), tally the average of each, and weight by multiplicity — the sampling distribution of the mean.

**Solution.** There are $6^6 = 46656$ equally likely ordered samples of size 6. Computing $\bar x$ for each gives 85 distinct averages from $1$ to $20$, roughly bell-shaped around the population mean $26/3 \approx 8.67$. The most likely average is $26/3$ itself (2520 samples, probability $0.0540$); e.g., $P(\bar X \le 4) \approx 0.0347$ and $P(\bar X \ge 14) \approx 0.0284$. A histogram plots these 85 average values on the horizontal axis against their probabilities (count$/46656$) as bar heights.
