**Central idea.** Bilinearity of covariance kills every cross term between distinct (uncorrelated) variables, leaving only the shared variable's variance.

**Solution.** $\mathrm{Cov}(X_1+X_2,X_2+X_3) = \mathrm{Cov}(X_2,X_2) = \sigma^2$ (all other pairs are uncorrelated). $\mathrm{Cov}(X_1+X_2,X_1-X_2) = \mathrm{Var}\,X_1-\mathrm{Var}\,X_2 = \sigma^2-\sigma^2 = 0$.
