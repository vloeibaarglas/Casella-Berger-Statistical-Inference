**Central idea.** This is a "Pareto-type" family with threshold $\theta$: the likelihood forces $\theta\le X_{(1)}$, and $E_\theta X_1=\infty$, so moments fail while the MLE is trivial.
**Solution.** (a) $L(\theta\mid\mathbf{x})=\theta^n(\prod x_i)^{-2}\mathbf{1}_{[\theta\le x_{(1)}]}$, which factors through $X_{(1)}$; hence $X_{(1)}$ is sufficient (indeed minimal sufficient).
(b) $L$ is strictly increasing in $\theta$ on $0<\theta\le x_{(1)}$, so $\hat\theta=X_{(1)}$.
(c) $E_\theta X_1=\int_\theta^\infty\theta x^{-1}\,dx=\infty$; the first population moment does not exist, so there is no method-of-moments estimator based on $\bar X$ (equating $\bar x=E_\theta X_1$ is impossible).
