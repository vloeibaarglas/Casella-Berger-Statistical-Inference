**Central idea.** Both power functions depend on the data only through the standardized effect $\sqrt{n}\,\mu/\sigma$; the one-sided power is $\Phi(\sqrt{n}\mu/\sigma - z_\alpha)$ and the two-sided power adds the reflected tail.

**Solution.** (a) The size-$\alpha$ LRT/UMP test rejects for $\bar X > z_\alpha\sigma/\sqrt{n}$. With $z_{.05} = 1.645$,
$$\gamma(\mu) = P_\mu\!\left(\bar X > z_\alpha\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}\right) = \Phi\!\left(\frac{\sqrt n\,\mu}{\sigma} - 1.645\right),$$
increasing from $0$ to $1$, equal to $.05$ at $\mu = 0$.
(b) The two-sided size-$\alpha$ test rejects for $|\bar X| > z_{\alpha/2}\sigma/\sqrt{n}$. With $z_{.025} = 1.96$,
$$\gamma(\mu) = \Phi\!\left(\frac{\sqrt n\,\mu}{\sigma} - 1.96\right) + \Phi\!\left(-\frac{\sqrt n\,\mu}{\sigma} - 1.96\right),$$
symmetric in $\mu$, equal to $.05$ at $\mu=0$ and $\to 1$ as $|\mu|\to\infty$. In both cases larger $n$ steepens the curve (power rises faster in $|\mu|/\sigma$).
