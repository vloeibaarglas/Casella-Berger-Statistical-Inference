**Central idea.** With common $\sigma^2$ the profile MLEs give $\lambda$ as a decreasing function of $|T|$; under $H_0$, $\bar X - \bar Y$ is normal independent of the pooled $\chi^2$.

**Solution.** (a) $\sup_{H_0} L$ uses $\hat\mu = \frac{n\bar X + m\bar Y}{n+m}$ (pooled mean) and $\hat\sigma^2_0 = \frac1{n+m}\big(\sum(X_i-\hat\mu)^2 + \sum(Y_i-\hat\mu)^2\big)$; $\sup L$ uses $\hat\mu_X = \bar X$, $\hat\mu_Y = \bar Y$, $\hat\sigma^2 = \frac1{n+m}\big(\sum(X_i-\bar X)^2 + \sum(Y_i-\bar Y)^2\big)$. So $\lambda = (\hat\sigma^2/\hat\sigma^2_0)^{(n+m)/2}$, and $\hat\sigma^2_0 = \hat\sigma^2 + \frac{nm}{(n+m)^2}(\bar X-\bar Y)^2$ (between-group sum of squares). Thus $\{\lambda < c\} = \left\{\frac{(\bar X-\bar Y)^2}{S_p^2(1/n+1/m)} > c'\right\} = \{|T| > c''\}$: reject for large $|T|$.
(b) Under $H_0$, $\bar X - \bar Y \sim n(0, \sigma^2(1/n+1/m))$ independent of $(n+m-2)S_p^2/\sigma^2 \sim \chi^2_{n+m-2}$ (sums of independent $\chi^2$'s, independent of the means). Hence $T \sim t_{n+m-2}$; reject $|T| > t_{n+m-2,\alpha/2}$ for level $\alpha$.
(c) Core: $n = 14$, $\bar x = 1249.86$, $s_X^2 = 591.4$; periphery: $m = 9$, $\bar y = 1261.33$, $s_Y^2 = 176.0$. $S_p^2 = \frac{13(591.4)+8(176.0)}{21} = 433.1$. $T = \frac{1249.86-1261.33}{\sqrt{433.1\,(1/14+1/9)}} = -1.291$ on $21$ df; two-sided $p = 2P(t_{21} > 1.291) \approx .211$. Fail to reject: no significant evidence the mean ages differ.
