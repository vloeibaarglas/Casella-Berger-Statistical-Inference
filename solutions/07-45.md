**Central idea.** $\mathrm{MSE}(aS^2)$ is quadratic in $a$ with minimizer $a^*=\sigma^4/(\sigma^4+\mathrm{Var}(S^2))$; under normality this is $(n-1)/(n+1)$, and heavier/lighter tails shift it down/up.
**Solution.** (a) $E[aS^2-\sigma^2]^2=\mathrm{Var}(aS^2)+[E(aS^2)-\sigma^2]^2=a^2\mathrm{Var}(S^2)+(a-1)^2\sigma^4$.
(b) Standard (Exercise 5.8b): expanding $S^2=\frac{1}{n-1}\sum(X_i-\bar X)^2$ and using independence gives $\mathrm{Var}(S^2)=\frac{1}{n}\big(\kappa-\frac{n-3}{n-1}\sigma^4\big)$.
(c) For $Z\sim\mathrm{n}(0,1)$, $E[Z^4]=3$ (Lemma 3.6.5), so $\kappa=3\sigma^4$ and $\mathrm{Var}(S^2)=2\sigma^4/(n-1)$. Minimizing $a^2\frac{2}{n-1}+(a-1)^2$ gives $a^*=\frac{n-1}{n+1}$.
(d) *Erratum: as printed, the formula $a=\frac{n-1}{(n+1)+\frac{(\kappa-1)(n-1)}{n}}$ cannot be correct.* Minimizing the quadratic in (a) gives $a^*=\sigma^4/(\sigma^4+\mathrm{Var}(S^2))$. With (b), and writing $K=\kappa/\sigma^4$ for the standardized kurtosis, this is
$$a^*=\frac{n-1}{(n+1)+\frac{(K-3)(n-1)}{n}}\qquad\Big(K=\frac{\kappa}{\sigma^4}\Big).$$
Proof of the erratum: (i) the printed $(\kappa-1)$ mixes the raw fourth moment $\kappa$ (units of $X^4$) with the dimensionless $1$ — dimensionally inconsistent with (b)'s definition of $\kappa$; (ii) under normality ($K=3$) the printed formula gives, e.g. for $n=2$, $a=\frac{1}{3+(3\sigma^4-1)/2}\ne\frac13$, contradicting the $\frac{n-1}{n+1}$ of part (c); (iii) for a uniform distribution ($K=1.8<3$) the printed formula can give $a<\frac{n-1}{n+1}$, contradicting (e)(ii) below. The intended formula has $(K-3)=(\kappa/\sigma^4-3)$ in place of $(\kappa-1)$, and (e)'s "$\kappa>3$"/"$\kappa<3$" likewise refer to the standardized kurtosis $K=\kappa/\sigma^4$.
(e) With $a^*=\frac{n-1}{(n+1)+(K-3)(n-1)/n}$: (i) if $K>3$ the denominator exceeds $n+1$, so $a^*<\frac{n-1}{n+1}$; (ii) if $K<3$ the denominator is smaller than $n+1$, so $a^*>\frac{n-1}{n+1}$, and $a^*<1\iff(3-K)(n-1)<2n$, which holds since $K\ge1$ (Jensen) gives $(3-K)(n-1)\le2(n-1)<2n$.
