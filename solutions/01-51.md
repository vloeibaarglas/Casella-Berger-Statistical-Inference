**Central idea.** Sampling without replacement from a finite population: hypergeometric; the cdf is a step function jumping by the pmf values.

**Solution.** $X \sim \text{Hypergeometric}(N=30, K=5, n=4)$:
$$P(X = x) = \frac{\binom{5}{x}\binom{25}{4-x}}{\binom{30}{4}}, \quad x = 0,1,2,3,4,$$
i.e. ($\binom{30}{4} = 27405$):

| $x$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $P(X=x)$ | 0.4616 | 0.4196 | 0.1095 | 0.0091 | 0.0002 |
| $F(x)$ | 0.4616 | 0.8812 | 0.9907 | 0.9998 | 1.0000 |

The cdf plot is a right-continuous step function: $0$ for $x<0$, then flat segments at the tabulated $F(x)$ values with jumps of height $P(X=x)$ at $x = 0,1,2,3,4$, reaching $1$ at $x=4$.
