**Central idea.** Minimize $\sigma^2\sum a_i^2$ subject to $\sum a_i=1$ — Lagrange gives equal weights.
**Solution.** (a) $E[\sum a_iX_i]=\mu\sum a_i=\mu$ iff $\sum a_i=1$.
(b) $\mathrm{Var}(\sum a_iX_i)=\sigma^2\sum a_i^2$; minimizing $\sum a_i^2$ under $\sum a_i=1$ (Lagrange: $2a_i=\lambda$) gives $a_i\equiv1/n$, i.e. $\bar X$, with variance $\sigma^2/n$ — the BLUE.
