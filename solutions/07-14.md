**Central idea.** Reparameterize by the rates $a=1/\lambda$, $b=1/\mu$: then $Z\sim\mathrm{exponential}(a+b)$ independently of $W\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(a/(a+b))$, and the log-likelihood separates.
**Solution.** From Exercise 4.26, $Z$ and $W$ are independent with $Z\sim\mathrm{exponential}$ of mean $(1/\lambda+1/\mu)^{-1}$ and $P(W=1)=\mu/(\lambda+\mu)$. With $a=1/\lambda$, $b=1/\mu$: $Z\sim\mathrm{exponential}(a+b)$ (rate) and $W\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(a/(a+b))$, independently. The log-likelihood is
$$\log L=n\log(a+b)-(a+b)\sum z_i+\Big(\sum w_i\Big)\log\frac{a}{a+b}+\Big(n-\sum w_i\Big)\log\frac{b}{a+b}$$
$$=-(a+b)\sum z_i+\Big(\sum w_i\Big)\log a+\Big(n-\sum w_i\Big)\log b.$$
Thus $\hat a=(\sum w_i)/(\sum z_i)=\bar w/\bar z$ and $\hat b=(n-\sum w_i)/(\sum z_i)=(1-\bar w)/\bar z$, i.e. $\hat\lambda=\bar Z/\bar W$ and $\hat\mu=\bar Z/(1-\bar W)$ (for $0<\bar w<1$; at the boundaries the corresponding MLE is $+\infty$).
