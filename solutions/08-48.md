**Central idea.** Under $H_0: p_1 = p_2 = p$, $(S_1,S_2)$ has a joint binomial pmf in which the $p$-terms factor through $S = S_1+S_2$ and cancel in the conditional distribution.

**Solution.** In Example 8.3.30, $S_1 \sim \text{binomial}(n_1,p_1)$, $S_2 \sim \text{binomial}(n_2,p_2)$ independent; under $H_0$, $p_1 = p_2 = p$. With $S = S_1+S_2$,
$$P(S_1 = s_1 \mid S = s) = \frac{P(S_1 = s_1, S_2 = s-s_1)}{P(S = s)} = \frac{\binom{n_1}{s_1}\binom{n_2}{s-s_1}p^s(1-p)^{n_1+n_2-s}}{\binom{n_1+n_2}{s}p^s(1-p)^{n_1+n_2-s}} = \frac{\binom{n_1}{s_1}\binom{n_2}{s-s_1}}{\binom{n_1+n_2}{s}},$$
the hypergeometric pmf (population $n_1+n_2$ with $n_1$ "successes", $s$ draws) — free of the nuisance parameter $p$.
