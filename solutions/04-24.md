**Central idea.** The transformation $(x,y) \mapsto (x+y, x/(x+y))$ has Jacobian $z_1$, and the gamma kernels regroup into a gamma$(r+s)$ piece times a beta$(r,s)$ piece.

**Solution.** With $x = z_1z_2$, $y = z_1(1-z_2)$, $|J| = z_1$:
$$f(z_1,z_2) = \frac{1}{\Gamma(r)\Gamma(s)}(z_1z_2)^{r-1}e^{-z_1z_2}(z_1(1-z_2))^{s-1}e^{-z_1(1-z_2)}z_1 = \frac{z_1^{r+s-1}e^{-z_1}}{\Gamma(r+s)}\cdot\frac{\Gamma(r+s)}{\Gamma(r)\Gamma(s)}z_2^{r-1}(1-z_2)^{s-1},$$
$z_1>0$, $0<z_2<1$. Hence $Z_1 \sim \text{gamma}(r+s,1)$, $Z_2 \sim \text{beta}(r,s)$, independent.
