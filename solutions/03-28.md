**Central idea.** Each pdf already separates into $h(x)\cdot c(\theta)\cdot\exp(w(\theta)\cdot t(x))$; read off the pieces.

**Solution.** (a) $\sigma$ known: $f=\underbrace{\frac{e^{-x^2/(2\sigma^2)}}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma}}_{h(x)}\underbrace{e^{-\mu^2/(2\sigma^2)}}_{c(\mu)}\exp(\underbrace{\mu/\sigma^2}_{w(\mu)}\underbrace{x}_{t(x)})$. $\mu$ known: $f=\underbrace{1}_{h(x)}\underbrace{\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma}}_{c(\sigma)}\exp(\underbrace{-1/(2\sigma^2)}_{w(\sigma)}\underbrace{(x-\mu)^2}_{t(x)})$. (b) $f=\underbrace{x^{-1}}_{h(x)}\underbrace{\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha)\beta^\alpha}}_{c(\alpha,\beta)}\exp(\alpha\log x-\frac1\beta x)$; with one parameter fixed, absorb its term into $h$ or $c$. (c) $f=\underbrace{\frac{1}{x(1-x)}}_{h(x)}\underbrace{\frac{1}{B(\alpha,\beta)}}_{c(\alpha,\beta)}\exp(\alpha\log x+\beta\log(1-x))$; similarly with one parameter fixed. (d) $f=\underbrace{\frac{1}{x!}}_{h(x)}\underbrace{e^{-\lambda}}_{c(\lambda)}\exp(\underbrace{\log\lambda}_{w(\lambda)}\underbrace{x}_{t(x)})$. (e) $f=\underbrace{\binom{r+x-1}{x}}_{h(x)}\underbrace{p^r}_{c(p)}\exp(\underbrace{\log(1-p)}_{w(p)}\underbrace{x}_{t(x)})$.
