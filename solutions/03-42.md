**Central idea.** Location shifts and scale stretches move every quantile in the same direction as the parameter.

**Solution.** (a) $F(x|\theta)=F_0(x-\theta)$: $\theta_1>\theta_2\Rightarrow x-\theta_1<x-\theta_2\Rightarrow F(x|\theta_1)\le F(x|\theta_2)$. (b) On $[0,\infty)$, $F(x|\theta)=F_0(x/\theta)$: for $x\ge0$, $\theta_1>\theta_2\Rightarrow x/\theta_1<x/\theta_2\Rightarrow F(x|\theta_1)\le F(x|\theta_2)$ (at $x=0$ both equal $F_0(0)$).
