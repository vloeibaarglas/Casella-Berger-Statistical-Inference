**Central idea.** Shift to $\{0,1,\ldots,m\}$ with $m = N_1-N_0$; the mean is the midpoint by symmetry, and the variance follows from $\sum_{k=0}^{m}k^2 = m(m+1)(2m+1)/6$.

**Solution.** $P(X = x) = 1/(m+1)$ on $m+1$ points, where $m = N_1-N_0$. By symmetry,
$$EX = \frac{1}{m+1}\sum_{k=0}^{m}(N_0+k) = N_0 + \frac{m}{2} = \frac{N_0+N_1}{2}.$$
Var$X$ = Var$(X-N_0)$, and $E[(X-N_0)^2] = \frac{1}{m+1}\cdot\frac{m(m+1)(2m+1)}{6} = \frac{m(2m+1)}{6}$, so
$$\text{Var}\,X = \frac{m(2m+1)}{6}-\frac{m^2}{4} = \frac{m(m+2)}{12} = \frac{(N_1-N_0+1)^2-1}{12}.$$
