**Central idea.** Differentiating a mixture cdf brings down the inner density; the resulting $\int_0^\infty y^{a-1}e^{-by}\,dy$ is a gamma integral that collapses to exactly the $t$ (resp. $F$) pdf.

**Solution.** (a) The integrand is $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi v}}\frac{1}{\Gamma(v/2)2^{v/2}}\,x^{(v+1)/2-1}\exp\!\big[-\tfrac{x}{2}(1+t^2/v)\big]$. Integrating over $x > 0$ (gamma integral):
$$\int_0^\infty = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi v}\,\Gamma(v/2)2^{v/2}}\cdot\Gamma\!\left(\tfrac{v+1}{2}\right)\left(\frac{2}{1+t^2/v}\right)^{(v+1)/2} = \frac{\Gamma((v+1)/2)}{\sqrt{\pi v}\,\Gamma(v/2)}\left(1+\frac{t^2}{v}\right)^{-(v+1)/2},$$
the $t_v$ pdf. (b) Differentiating in $t$: $v\,f_{F_{1,v}}(vt) = \int_0^\infty y\,f_{\chi^2_1}(ty)\,f_v(y)\,dy$. The integrand is $\frac{t^{-1/2}}{\sqrt{2\pi}\,\Gamma(v/2)2^{v/2}}\,y^{(v+1)/2-1}e^{-y(t+1)/2}$; integrating gives $v\,f_{F_{1,v}}(vt) = \frac{\Gamma((v+1)/2)}{\sqrt{\pi}\,\Gamma(v/2)}\,t^{-1/2}(1+t)^{-(v+1)/2}$. With $s = vt$,
$$f_{F_{1,v}}(s) = \frac{\Gamma((v+1)/2)}{\sqrt{\pi}\,\Gamma(v/2)}\,v^{-1/2}s^{-1/2}\left(1+\frac{s}{v}\right)^{-(v+1)/2},$$
the $F_{1,v}$ pdf. (c) The mixture identity holds because $F_{m,v} = \frac{\chi^2_m/m}{\chi^2_v/v}$, so $\{F_{m,v} \leq \frac{v}{m}t\} = \{\chi^2_m \leq t\chi^2_v\}$ and conditioning on $\chi^2_v = y$ gives the integral. Differentiating,
$$\tfrac{v}{m}f_{F_{m,v}}\!\left(\tfrac{v}{m}t\right) = \int_0^\infty y\,f_{\chi^2_m}(ty)\,f_v(y)\,dy = \frac{t^{m/2-1}\Gamma((m+v)/2)}{\Gamma(m/2)\Gamma(v/2)}(1+t)^{-(m+v)/2},$$
again a gamma integral; with $s = \frac{v}{m}t$ this is the $F_{m,v}$ pdf $\frac{\Gamma((m+v)/2)}{\Gamma(m/2)\Gamma(v/2)}\left(\frac{m}{v}\right)^{m/2}s^{m/2-1}\left(1+\frac{m}{v}s\right)^{-(m+v)/2}$.
