**Central idea.** The LRT acceptance region $\{p_0 : L(p_0)/L(\hat p) \ge k\}$ is a level set of the likelihood — by definition a highest-density region — and its boundary is transcendental, unlike the quadratic Wilson interval.

**Solution.** (a) With $x = \sum x_i$, $\lambda = p_0^x(1-p_0)^{n-x}/[(x/n)^x(1-x/n)^{n-x}]$ and
$$-2\log\lambda = 2\left[x\log\frac{x}{np_0} + (n-x)\log\frac{n-x}{n(1-p_0)}\right].$$
$C(x) = \{p_0 : -2\log\lambda \le c\}$ ($c$ from the exact null distribution, or $\chi^2_{1,\alpha}$ asymptotically).
(b) $C(x) = \{p_0 : p_0^x(1-p_0)^{n-x} \ge k'\}$ is exactly the HDR of the likelihood shape $p_0 \mapsto p_0^x(1-p_0)^{n-x}$. It is not (10.4.4), the Wilson score interval solving the quadratic $|\hat p - p|/\sqrt{p(1-p)/n} = z_{\alpha/2}$; the LRT boundary equation above is transcendental in $p_0$, so the endpoints differ.
