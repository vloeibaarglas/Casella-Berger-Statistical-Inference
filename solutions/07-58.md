**Central idea.** $|X|$ is sufficient ($f$ depends on $x$ only through $|x|$); $T$ wastes information by distinguishing $1$ from $-1$, and Rao–Blackwell recovers $|X|$.
**Solution.** (a) $L(\theta\mid x)=(\theta/2)^{|x|}(1-\theta)^{1-|x|}$: for $x=0$, maximized at $\hat\theta=0$; for $|x|=1$, at $\hat\theta=1$. So $\hat\theta=|X|=\mathbf{1}_{\{X\ne0\}}$.
(b) $E_\theta[T]=2P_\theta(X=1)=2\cdot\theta/2=\theta$.
(c) $|X|$ is sufficient and $T$ is not a function of it. $E[T\mid|X|]=\mathbf{1}_{\{|X|=1\}}=|X|$ (given $|X|=1$, $X=\pm1$ with equal probability, so $E[T\mid|X|=1]=\tfrac12\cdot2=1$). $\mathrm{Var}_\theta(T)=2\theta-\theta^2>\theta(1-\theta)=\mathrm{Var}_\theta(|X|)$ for $0<\theta\le1$; thus $|X|$ (the MLE, in fact the UMVUE) is strictly better.
