**Central idea.** Shortest $\Rightarrow$ $f(a) = f(b)$ (9.38); symmetry about the mode then forces $a,b$ equidistant from the mode, i.e. equal tails.

**Solution.** By 9.38/Theorem 9.3.2 the shortest interval satisfies $f(a) = f(b)$. Let $m$ be the mode (= center of symmetry). $a \ge m$ is impossible (then $f$ strictly decreasing gives $f(a) > f(b)$); similarly $b \le m$. So $a < m < b$, and $f(a) = f(2m-a) = f(b)$ with $f$ strictly decreasing on $[m,\infty)$ gives $b = 2m-a$. Hence $P(X \le a) = P(X \ge 2m-a) = P(X \ge b)$; the two tails are equal, each $\alpha/2$.
