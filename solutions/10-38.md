**Central idea.** $l'(\beta)=-n\alpha/\beta+\sum X_i/\beta^2$ and $I(\beta)=n\alpha/\beta^2$ give $S=\sqrt n(\bar X-\alpha\beta_0)/(\beta_0\sqrt\alpha)$.

**Solution.** $l'(\beta) = -n\alpha/\beta+\sum_{i=1}^n X_i/\beta^2$, $I(\beta_0)=n\alpha/\beta_0^2$ (from 10.36). Hence
$$S = \frac{l'(\beta_0)}{\sqrt{I(\beta_0)}} = \frac{-n\alpha/\beta_0+\sum X_i/\beta_0^2}{\sqrt{n\alpha}/\beta_0} = \frac{\sqrt n\,(\bar X-\alpha\beta_0)}{\beta_0\sqrt\alpha}\;\xrightarrow{d}\;n(0,1)\ \text{under }H_0.$$
