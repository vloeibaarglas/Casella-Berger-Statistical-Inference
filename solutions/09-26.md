**Central idea.** The gamma prior (scale convention, as in the text) is conjugate for the $\text{beta}(\theta,1)$ shape parameter: the posterior is $\text{gamma}(n+r,\ [1/\lambda - \sum\log x_i]^{-1})$.

**Solution.** $L(\theta\mid x) = \theta^n(\prod x_i)^{\theta-1}$; with $\pi(\theta) \propto \theta^{r-1}e^{-\theta/\lambda}$,
$$\pi(\theta\mid x) \propto \theta^{\,n+r-1}\exp\!\big(-\theta(1/\lambda - \textstyle\sum\log x_i)\big),$$
i.e. $\theta\mid x \sim \text{gamma}(n+r,\ [1/\lambda - \sum_{i=1}^n\log x_i]^{-1})$ (shape–scale). A $1-\alpha$ credible set is e.g. the equal-tail interval $[G^{-1}(\alpha/2),\ G^{-1}(1-\alpha/2)]$ for this gamma law.
