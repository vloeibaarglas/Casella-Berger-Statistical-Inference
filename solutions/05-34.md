**Central idea.** Linearity of expectation and $\mathrm{Var}(\bar{X}_n) = \sigma^2/n$; the $\sqrt{n}/\sigma$ scaling is exactly what standardizes these.

**Solution.** $E[\bar{X}_n] = \mu$, so $E[\sqrt{n}(\bar{X}_n-\mu)/\sigma] = 0$. And $\mathrm{Var}(\bar{X}_n) = \sigma^2/n$, so
$$\mathrm{Var}\frac{\sqrt{n}(\bar{X}_n-\mu)}{\sigma} = \frac{n}{\sigma^2}\cdot\frac{\sigma^2}{n} = 1.$$
The CLT's normalization therefore matches the first two moments of its $n(0,1)$ limit exactly, for every $n$.
