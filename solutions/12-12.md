**Central idea.** The score equation is a quadratic whose roots multiply to $-1/\lambda$; the map $\beta\mapsto-1/(\lambda\beta)$ swaps the two linear combinations defining $r_\lambda$ up to scalars of opposite sign, flipping the correlation's sign but not its square.

**Solution.** (a) Maximizing (12.2.14) in $\beta$ (with $\hat\alpha=\bar y-\beta\bar x$) minimizes $g(b)=(S_{yy}-2bS_{xy}+b^2S_{xx})/(1+\lambda b^2)$; $g'(b)=0$ iff $\lambda S_{xy}b^2+(S_{xx}-\lambda S_{yy})b-S_{xy}=0$. The product of its two roots is $-S_{xy}/(\lambda S_{xy})=-1/\lambda$. The "$+$" root is the MLE $\hat\beta$; the other is $(-1/\lambda)/\hat\beta=-1/(\lambda\hat\beta)$.

(b) $r_\lambda(\beta)$ is the sample correlation of $A=\beta\lambda Y+X$ and $B=Y-\beta X$. Under $\beta'=-1/(\lambda\beta)$ (for $\beta\ne 0$): $\beta'\lambda Y+X=-(Y-\beta X)/\beta$ and $Y-\beta'X=(\beta\lambda Y+X)/(\lambda\beta)$, i.e. the new pair is $(c_1B,c_2A)$ with $c_1c_2=-1/(\lambda\beta^2)<0$. Correlation is invariant under positive rescaling and flips sign under negative rescaling, so $r_\lambda(-1/(\lambda\beta))=-r_\lambda(\beta)$.

(c) $C_\lambda(\hat\beta)$ is defined by $(n-2)r^2_\lambda(\beta)/(1-r^2_\lambda(\beta))\le F_{1,n-2,\alpha}$, which depends on $\beta$ only through $r^2_\lambda(\beta)=r^2_\lambda(-1/(\lambda\beta))$ by (b).
