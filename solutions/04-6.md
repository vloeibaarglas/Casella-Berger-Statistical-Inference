**Central idea.** With $X,Y$ iid uniform$(0,1)$, A's wait is $W = \max(X-Y,0)$: a point mass at $0$ plus a continuous piece on $(0,1)$.

**Solution.** Let $X,Y$ be the arrival times (hours after 1 PM), iid uniform$(0,1)$, and $W = \max(X-Y,0)$. $P(W=0) = P(X \le Y) = 1/2$. For $0 < w \le 1$, $F_W(w) = P(X-Y \le w) = 1-(1-w)^2/2$. Hence $W$ is mixed: $P(W=0)=1/2$, and on $(0,1)$ it has pdf $f_W(w) = F_W'(w) = 1-w$. (In particular $\mathrm{E}W = \int_0^1 w(1-w)\,dw = 1/6$ hour $= 10$ minutes.)
