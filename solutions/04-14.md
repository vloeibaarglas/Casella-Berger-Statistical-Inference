**Central idea.** $X^2+Y^2 \sim \chi^2_2$ is exponential with mean 2; $X^2$ is the square of a standard normal, i.e. $\chi^2_1$ by definition.

**Solution.** (a) $X^2+Y^2 \sim \chi^2_2$, which is exponential with mean $2$: $P(X^2+Y^2<1) = 1-e^{-1/2}$. (b) By definition $\chi^2_1$ is the distribution of the square of an $n(0,1)$ variable, so $X^2 \sim \chi^2_1$. Then $P(X^2<1) = P(|X|<1) = 2\Phi(1)-1 \approx 0.6826$.
