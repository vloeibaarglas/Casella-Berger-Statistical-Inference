**Central idea.** Match the two normal-approximated error probabilities at a common cutoff: $.02n = 2z_{.01}\sqrt{.49\cdot.51\,n}$, giving $n \approx 13{,}520$.

**Solution.** Let $S = \sum X_i$ and reject iff $S > c$. By the CLT, $S \approx n(.49,.49\cdot.51)$ under $H_0$ and $\approx n(.51,.49\cdot.51)$ under $H_1$. Setting both error probabilities to $.01$,
$$c = .49n + z_{.01}\sqrt{.2499n} = .51n - z_{.01}\sqrt{.2499n}, \quad z_{.01} \approx 2.326.$$
Hence $.02n = 2(2.326)\sqrt{.2499n}$, i.e. $\sqrt n = 2(2.326)\sqrt{.2499}/.02 \approx 116.27$, so $n \approx 13{,}520$ (round up). The cutoff is $c \approx .49n + 2.326\sqrt{.2499n} \approx 6760$: reject $H_0$ if more than about $6760$ of the $13{,}520$ trials are successes.
