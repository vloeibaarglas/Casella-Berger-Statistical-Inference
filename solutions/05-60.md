**Central idea.** $(X,Y)$ is uniform over the rectangle $[a,b]\times[0,c]$; accepting iff the point falls under the curve $y = f(x)$ makes the accepted $X$'s density proportional to $f$.

**Solution.** (a) For $a \leq w \leq b$: $P(W \leq w) = P(X \leq w,\ Y < f(X)) = \int_a^w P(Y < f(t))\cdot\frac{dt}{b-a} = \int_a^w \frac{f(t)}{c(b-a)}\,dt$.
(b) $P(W \leq b) = \int_a^b f/[c(b-a)] = 1/[c(b-a)]$ (since $f$ is a pdf). Conditioning on acceptance $\{W \leq b\}$,
$$P(W \leq w \mid W \leq b) = \frac{\int_a^w f(t)\,dt/[c(b-a)]}{1/[c(b-a)]} = \int_a^w f(t)\,dt,$$
so accepted $W$'s have pdf $f$. Geometrically: accept the uniform rectangle point iff it lies under $y = f(x)$; the $x$-marginal of the sub-curve region has density $\propto f(x)$. Repeat (generate new $(X,Y)$) until $W \leq b$ — this is the accept-reject method, with acceptance probability $1/[c(b-a)]$.
