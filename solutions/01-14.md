**Central idea.** Each element independently is either in or out of a subset — $n$ binary choices.

**Solution.** A subset is determined by, for each of the $n$ elements, the choice "include" or "exclude": $2$ choices per element, $2 \times \cdots \times 2 = 2^n$ by the multiplication principle. (Equivalently, $\sum_{k=0}^n \binom{n}{k} = (1+1)^n = 2^n$.)
