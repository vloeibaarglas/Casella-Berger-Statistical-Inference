**Central idea.** Element-chasing: $x$ belongs to the left side iff it belongs to the right side; $A^c$ is "not in $A$".

**Solution.** (a) $x \in A \setminus B \iff x \in A$ and $x \notin B$. Also $x \in A \setminus (A \cap B) \iff x \in A$ and $x \notin A \cap B \iff x \in A$ and ($x \notin A$ or $x \notin B$) $\iff x \in A$ and $x \notin B$. And $x \in A \cap B^c \iff x \in A$ and $x \notin B$. All three coincide. (b) $B = B \cap S = B \cap (A \cup A^c) = (B \cap A) \cup (B \cap A^c)$ by distributivity; the union is disjoint. (c) Same as (a) with roles swapped. (d) By (c), $B \cap A^c = B \setminus A$, and $A \cup B = A \cup (B \setminus A)$ since every element of $B$ is either in $A$ or not.
