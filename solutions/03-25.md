**Central idea.** The conditional probability is a difference quotient of $F_T$; the limit is $f_T(t)/(1-F_T(t))$, which is $-\frac{d}{dt}\log(1-F_T(t))$ by the chain rule.

**Solution.** $P(t\le T<t+\delta\mid T\ge t)=\frac{F_T(t+\delta)-F_T(t)}{1-F_T(t)}$. Dividing by $\delta$ and letting $\delta\downarrow0$ gives $h_T(t)=\frac{f_T(t)}{1-F_T(t)}=-\frac{d}{dt}\log(1-F_T(t))$.
