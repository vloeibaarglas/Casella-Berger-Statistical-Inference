**Central idea.** $W+aU$ stays unbiased; choose $a$ to kill the covariance term and strictly reduce variance at $\theta_0$.
**Solution.** Theorem 7.3.20: $W$ is best unbiased iff it is uncorrelated with every unbiased estimator of zero. For fixed $a$, $W_a=W+aU$ is unbiased for $\tau(\theta)$, with $\mathrm{Var}_{\theta_0}(W_a)=\mathrm{Var}_{\theta_0}(W)+a^2\mathrm{Var}_{\theta_0}(U)+2a\,\mathrm{Cov}_{\theta_0}(W,U)$. Minimizing in $a$ gives $a^*=-\mathrm{Cov}_{\theta_0}(W,U)/\mathrm{Var}_{\theta_0}(U)$ (if $\mathrm{Var}_{\theta_0}(U)=0$ then $U=0$ a.s. and the covariance is $0$), with $\mathrm{Var}_{\theta_0}(W_{a^*})=\mathrm{Var}_{\theta_0}(W)-\mathrm{Cov}_{\theta_0}(W,U)^2/\mathrm{Var}_{\theta_0}(U)<\mathrm{Var}_{\theta_0}(W)$. Hence $W$ is beaten at $\theta_0$ and cannot be best unbiased.
