**Central idea.** $A_r = \exp(\log A_r)$ transfers monotonicity; differentiating $\log A_r = \frac1r\log(\frac1n\sum x_i^r)$ and recognizing the weights $a_i = x_i^r/\sum x_j^r$ turns the derivative's sign into "entropy $\le \log n$" — Jensen on the concave log.

**Solution.** (a) $A_1 = \frac1n\sum x_i$ (arithmetic); $A_{-1} = (\frac1n\sum x_i^{-1})^{-1} = n/\sum(1/x_i)$ (harmonic); $\log A_r = \frac{\log(\frac1n\sum x_i^r)}{r} \to \left.\frac{d}{dr}\log(\tfrac1n\sum x_i^r)\right|_{r=0} = \frac1n\sum\log x_i$ by L'Hôpital, so $A_0 = (\prod x_i)^{1/n}$ (geometric; take $x_i>0$).
(b)(i) $A_r = e^{\log A_r}$ and $e^t$ is increasing, so monotonicity of $\log A_r$ implies that of $A_r$. (ii) $\frac{d}{dr}\log A_r = \frac{d}{dr}\left[\frac1r\left(\log\sum x_i^r-\log n\right)\right] = -\frac{1}{r^2}\log\left(\frac1n\sum x_i^r\right)+\frac1r\frac{\sum x_i^r\log x_i}{\sum x_i^r}$, as claimed. (iii) With $a_i = x_i^r/\sum_j x_j^r$: $\log n-\sum a_i\log(1/a_i) = \log n+\sum a_i\log a_i = \log n+\sum a_i(r\log x_i-\log\sum x_j^r) = \frac{r\sum x_i^r\log x_i}{\sum x_i^r}-\log(\frac1n\sum x_i^r)$, the braced quantity. By Jensen, $\sum a_i\log(1/a_i) = \mathrm{E}\log(1/a) \le \log(\mathrm{E}[1/a]) = \log(\sum a_i/a_i) = \log n$, so the braces are $\ge0$, $\frac{d}{dr}\log A_r \ge0$, and $A_{-1}\le A_0\le A_1$ is the harmonic–geometric–arithmetic inequality.
