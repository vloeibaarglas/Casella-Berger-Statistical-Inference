**Central idea.** The probability integral transform: the cdf itself is the monotone map that uniformizes a continuous variable.

**Solution.** $F_X(x)=\int_1^x\frac{t-1}{2}\,dt=\frac{(x-1)^2}{4}$, $1<x<3$, is continuous and strictly increasing. Take $u(x)=F_X(x)=\frac{(x-1)^2}{4}$; then $Y=u(X)\sim\text{uniform}(0,1)$ by Theorem 2.1.10.
