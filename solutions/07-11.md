**Central idea.** $-\log X_i\sim\mathrm{exponential}(\theta)$, so the MLE is $n/\sum(-\log X_i)$ — an inverse-gamma-scaled statistic whose variance is explicitly $O(1/n)$.
**Solution.** (a) $\log L=n\log\theta+(\theta-1)\sum\log x_i$; $\partial/\partial\theta$: $n/\theta+\sum\log x_i=0$, so $\hat\theta=-n/\sum_{i=1}^n\log X_i$ (a maximum). Since $-\log X_i\sim\mathrm{exponential}(\theta)$ (rate $\theta$), $S:=\sum(-\log X_i)\sim\mathrm{gamma}(n,\theta)$, and $\hat\theta=n/S$ has $E\hat\theta=n\theta/(n-1)$, $\mathrm{Var}(\hat\theta)=n^2\theta^2/[(n-1)^2(n-2)]\to 0$ as $n\to\infty$.
(b) $E_\theta X_1=\theta/(\theta+1)$; solving $\bar x=\tilde\theta/(\tilde\theta+1)$ gives $\tilde\theta=\bar X/(1-\bar X)$.
