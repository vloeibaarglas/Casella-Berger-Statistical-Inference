**Central idea.** $M=\max|X_i|$ is complete sufficient (differentiate $\int_0^\theta g(m)m^{n-1}dm\equiv0$), so Lehmann–Scheffé applied to the unbiased $(n+1)M/n$ gives the UMVUE.
**Solution.** Let $M=\max_i|X_i|$; $P_\theta(M\le m)=(m/\theta)^n$, $0\le m\le\theta$, so $E_\theta M=n\theta/(n+1)$ and $\delta=\frac{n+1}{n}M$ is unbiased for $\theta$. $M$ is sufficient (the likelihood $\propto\theta^{-n}\mathbf{1}_{[m\le\theta]}$ factors through $M$) and complete: $E_\theta[g(M)]=0\ \forall\theta>0$ gives $\int_0^\theta g(m)m^{n-1}dm\equiv0$, whence $g(\theta)\theta^{n-1}=0$ a.e. by differentiation. By Lehmann–Scheffé, $\frac{n+1}{n}\max_i|X_i|$ is the best (UMVUE) unbiased estimator of $\theta$.
