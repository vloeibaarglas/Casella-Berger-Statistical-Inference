**Central idea.** Tower laws: $\mathrm{E}Y = \mathrm{E}[\mathrm{E}(Y|X)]$, $\mathrm{Var}\,Y = \mathrm{E}[\mathrm{Var}(Y|X)]+\mathrm{Var}(\mathrm{E}(Y|X))$; and $Y/X|X=x \sim n(1,1)$ is free of $x$, which characterizes independence.

**Solution.** (a) $\mathrm{E}Y = \mathrm{E}[X] = 1/2$. $\mathrm{Var}\,Y = \mathrm{E}[X^2]+\mathrm{Var}(X) = 1/3+1/12 = 5/12$. $\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y) = \mathrm{E}[X\cdot\mathrm{E}(Y|X)]-\mathrm{E}X\,\mathrm{E}Y = \mathrm{E}[X^2]-1/4 = 1/3-1/4 = 1/12$.
(b) Given $X=x>0$, $Y/X = (1/x)Y \sim n(1,1)$, independent of $x$. Hence $P(Y/X \le t|X=x) = \Phi(t-1)$ for all $x$, so $Y/X \sim n(1,1)$ independent of $X$.
