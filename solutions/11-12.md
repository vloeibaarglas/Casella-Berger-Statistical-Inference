**Central idea.** Each pairwise $t^2$ is an $F_{1,N-k}$ statistic; Exercise 5.8a rewrites the sum of all squared pairwise differences of the $k$ cell means as $2k$ times the treatment sum of squares.

**Solution.** (a) $\bar{Y}_{i\cdot} - \bar{Y}_{i'\cdot}$ is the contrast with $a_i = 1$, $a_{i'} = -1$. By 11.8–11.9, $(\bar{Y}_{i\cdot}-\bar{Y}_{i'\cdot})/(S_p\sqrt{2/n}) \sim t_{N-k}$ under $H_0$, so its square is the test statistic.
(b) $\sum_{i,i'} t^2_{ii'} = \frac{n}{2S_p^2}\sum_{i,i'}(\bar{Y}_{i\cdot}-\bar{Y}_{i'\cdot})^2$ (diagonal terms are 0). Apply Exercise 5.8a to the $k$ numbers $\bar{Y}_{1\cdot},\dots,\bar{Y}_{k\cdot}$: $\frac{1}{k-1}\sum_i(\bar{Y}_{i\cdot}-\bar{\bar{Y}})^2 = \frac{1}{2k(k-1)}\sum_{i,i'}(\bar{Y}_{i\cdot}-\bar{Y}_{i'\cdot})^2$. With equal $n$, $\mathrm{SSB} = n\sum_i(\bar{Y}_{i\cdot}-\bar{\bar{Y}})^2$, so $\sum_{i,i'}(\bar{Y}_{i\cdot}-\bar{Y}_{i'\cdot})^2 = 2k\cdot\mathrm{SSB}/n$. Thus $\frac{1}{k(k-1)}\sum_{i,i'}t^2_{ii'} = \frac{1}{k(k-1)}\cdot\frac{n}{2S_p^2}\cdot\frac{2k\,\mathrm{SSB}}{n} = \frac{\mathrm{SSB}/(k-1)}{S_p^2} = F$.
