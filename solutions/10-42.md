**Central idea.** $-2\log(L(\theta\mid x)/L(\hat\theta\mid x))\le\chi^2_{1,\alpha}$ is the same inequality as $L(\theta\mid x)/L(\hat\theta\mid x)\ge e^{-\chi^2_{1,\alpha}/2}$.

**Solution.** (10.4.5) is $\{\theta:-2\log\frac{L(\theta\mid x)}{L(\hat\theta\mid x)}\le\chi^2_{1,\alpha}\}$; (9.2.7) is $\{\theta:\frac{L(\theta\mid x)}{L(\hat\theta\mid x)}\ge c\}$. For fixed $\alpha$, $-2\log\frac{L(\theta\mid x)}{L(\hat\theta\mid x)}\le\chi^2_{1,\alpha}\iff \frac{L(\theta\mid x)}{L(\hat\theta\mid x)}\ge e^{-\chi^2_{1,\alpha}/2}$, so with $c=e^{-\chi^2_{1,\alpha}/2}$ the two sets coincide.
