**Central idea.** Imposing $\sigma^2_\delta=\lambda\sigma^2_\epsilon$ on the moment equations collapses them to the same quadratic for $\hat\beta$ as in the functional case; the variance identities then follow by substituting $\hat\beta$'s first-order condition.

**Solution.** (a) From (12.2.20): $\hat\xi=\bar x$, $\hat\alpha=\bar y-\hat\beta\bar x$, $\hat\sigma^2_\xi=S_{xy}/(n\hat\beta)$. Also $\hat\sigma^2_\epsilon=(S_{yy}-\hat\beta S_{xy})/n$ and, with $\hat\sigma^2_\delta=\lambda\hat\sigma^2_\epsilon$, $S_{xx}/n=\lambda(S_{yy}-\hat\beta S_{xy})/n+S_{xy}/(n\hat\beta)$, i.e. $S_{xx}=\lambda S_{yy}-\lambda\hat\beta S_{xy}+S_{xy}/\hat\beta$. Multiplying by $\hat\beta$: $\lambda S_{xy}\hat\beta^2+(S_{xx}-\lambda S_{yy})\hat\beta-S_{xy}=0$, the same quadratic as the functional case, so $\hat\beta$ is (12.2.16) and $\hat\sigma^2_\delta=\lambda(S_{yy}-\hat\beta S_{xy})/n$, $\hat\sigma^2_\xi=S_{xy}/(n\hat\beta)$, as in (12.2.21).

(b) With $\lambda=1$ and the sums from Exercise 12.4: $\hat\beta=14.10$, $\hat\alpha=\bar y-\hat\beta\bar x=12.075-14.10(1.1005)=-3.45$, $\hat\sigma^2_\epsilon=(S_{yy}-\hat\beta S_{xy})/n=0.285$, $\hat\sigma^2_\delta=0.285$, $\hat\sigma^2_\xi=S_{xy}/(n\hat\beta)=0.852$.

(c) Since $\hat\alpha=\bar y-\hat\beta\bar x$, $\sum_i(y_i-(\hat\alpha+\hat\beta x_i))^2=S_{yy}-2\hat\beta S_{xy}+\hat\beta^2S_{xx}\equiv Q$. The claim is $(S_{xx}-S_{xy}/\hat\beta)(1+\lambda\hat\beta^2)=\lambda Q$, i.e. $S_{xx}+\lambda\hat\beta^2S_{xx}-S_{xy}/\hat\beta-\lambda\hat\beta S_{xy}-\lambda S_{yy}+2\lambda\hat\beta S_{xy}-\lambda\hat\beta^2S_{xx}=0$, which simplifies to $[\lambda S_{xy}\hat\beta^2+(S_{xx}-\lambda S_{yy})\hat\beta-S_{xy}]/\hat\beta=0$, true by the quadratic in (a). Numerically both sides equal $2.2774$, and since $\hat\sigma^2_\delta(\text{structural})=(S_{xx}-S_{xy}/\hat\beta)/n$ while $\hat\sigma^2_\delta(\text{functional})=\frac{\lambda}{2n(1+\lambda\hat\beta^2)}Q$, this is exactly $\hat\sigma^2_\delta(\text{structural})=2\hat\sigma^2_\delta(\text{functional})$.

(d) From (12.2.21), $\hat\sigma^2_\delta=(1/n)(S_{xx}-S_{xy}/\hat\beta)$ and also $\hat\sigma^2_\delta=\lambda\hat\sigma^2_\epsilon=(\lambda/n)(S_{yy}-\hat\beta S_{xy})$; equating gives $S_{xx}-S_{xy}/\hat\beta=\lambda(S_{yy}-\hat\beta S_{xy})$.
