**Central idea.** Independence gives $\mathrm{Cov}(XY,Y) = \mu_X\sigma_Y^2$ and $\mathrm{Var}(XY) = \mathrm{E}[X^2]\mathrm{E}[Y^2]-(\mathrm{E}X\,\mathrm{E}Y)^2$; assemble the correlation.

**Solution.** $\mathrm{Cov}(XY,Y) = \mathrm{E}[XY^2]-\mathrm{E}[XY]\mathrm{E}[Y] = \mathrm{E}X\,\mathrm{E}[Y^2]-(\mathrm{E}X)(\mathrm{E}Y)^2 = \mu_X\sigma_Y^2$. $\mathrm{Var}(XY) = (\sigma_X^2+\mu_X^2)(\sigma_Y^2+\mu_Y^2)-\mu_X^2\mu_Y^2 = \sigma_X^2\sigma_Y^2+\sigma_X^2\mu_Y^2+\sigma_Y^2\mu_X^2$. Hence
$$\mathrm{Corr}(XY,Y) = \frac{\mu_X\sigma_Y}{\sqrt{\sigma_X^2\sigma_Y^2+\sigma_X^2\mu_Y^2+\sigma_Y^2\mu_X^2}}.$$
