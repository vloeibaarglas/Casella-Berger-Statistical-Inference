**Central idea.** Each observation constrains $\theta > |x_i/i - 1|$; the $n$ constraints combine into one involving only the max and min of $X_i/i$.

**Solution.** The joint pdf is $\big(\prod_{i=1}^n \frac{1}{2i}\big)\theta^{-n}\prod_{i=1}^n I(-i(\theta-1) < x_i < i(\theta+1))$. Now
$$-i(\theta-1) < x_i < i(\theta+1) \iff \theta > 1 - \frac{x_i}{i}\ \text{and}\ \theta > \frac{x_i}{i} - 1 \iff \theta > \left|\frac{x_i}{i}-1\right|,$$
so the indicator is $I\big(\theta > \max_i|x_i/i - 1|\big)$. Since $\max_i|x_i/i-1| = \max\big(\max_i(x_i/i)-1,\ 1-\min_i(x_i/i)\big)$, with $T_1 = \max_i(X_i/i)$ and $T_2 = \min_i(X_i/i)$ the joint pdf equals $g(T_1,T_2\mid\theta)\cdot\text{const}$. Thus $(T_1,T_2) = \big(\max_i(X_i/i),\ \min_i(X_i/i)\big)$ is a two-dimensional sufficient statistic for $\theta$.
