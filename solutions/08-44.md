**Central idea.** Test 3 is the two-sided $z$-test $|\bar X - \theta_0| > \sigma z_{\alpha/2}/\sqrt{n}$ (for $n(\theta,\sigma^2)$ with $\sigma$ known); its power $\beta_3(\theta) = P(|Z+\delta| > z_{\alpha/2})$ is minimized at $\delta = 0$ by symmetry and the unimodality of $\phi$.

**Solution.** Example 8.3.20's Test 3 rejects $H_0: \theta = \theta_0$ iff $\bar X > \theta_0 + \sigma z_{\alpha/2}/\sqrt{n}$ or $\bar X < \theta_0 - \sigma z_{\alpha/2}/\sqrt{n}$. Under $\theta_0$, $P(\text{reject}) = P(|Z| > z_{\alpha/2}) = \alpha$: level $\alpha$ (point null). The power is $\beta_3(\theta) = P_\theta(|Z+\delta| > z_{\alpha/2})$ with $\delta = \sqrt{n}(\theta-\theta_0)/\sigma$, $Z \sim n(0,1)$. $\beta_3$ is even in $\delta$; for $\delta > 0$,
$$\frac{d}{d\delta}\beta_3 = \phi(z_{\alpha/2}-\delta) - \phi(z_{\alpha/2}+\delta) > 0,$$
since $(z_{\alpha/2}+\delta)^2 - (z_{\alpha/2}-\delta)^2 = 4z_{\alpha/2}\delta > 0$ and $\phi$ decreases in $|x|$. Hence $\beta_3$ is minimized at $\delta = 0$ with value $\alpha$: $\beta_3(\theta) \ge \alpha$ for all $\theta \ne \theta_0$. Unbiased.
