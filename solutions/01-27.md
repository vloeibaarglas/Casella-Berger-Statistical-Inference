**Central idea.** Differentiate the binomial theorem, then substitute $x=1$ or $x=-1$. The scanned source has $k$, not $1/k$, in (c); the earlier erratum claim was a transcription error.

**Solution.** (a) $(1-1)^n=\sum_{k=0}^n(-1)^k\binom nk=0$.
(b) From $n(1+x)^{n-1}=\sum_{k=1}^n k\binom nkx^{k-1}$, put $x=1$ to obtain $n2^{n-1}$.
(c) From $nx(1+x)^{n-1}=\sum_{k=1}^n k\binom nkx^k$, put $x=-1$ and negate: $\sum_{k=1}^n(-1)^{k+1}k\binom nk=0$ for $n\ge2$.
The unrelated identity with $1/k$ equals $H_n$, but it is not the statement in the inspected textbook.
