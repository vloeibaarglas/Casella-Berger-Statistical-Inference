**Central idea.** Each p-value is $\sup_{H_0} P_\theta(\text{statistic} \ge \text{observed})$, attained at the boundary by MLR/stochastic ordering.

**Solution.** (a) $p = P_{\theta=1/2}(X \ge 7) = 2^{-10}\sum_{k=7}^{10}\binom{10}{k} = 176/1024 \approx .172$.
(b) $p = P_{\lambda=1}(X \ge 3) = 1 - e^{-1}(1+1+1/2) = 1 - 2.5e^{-1} \approx .0803$.
(c) $T = \sum X_i = 9 \sim \text{Poisson}(3\lambda)$; $p = P_{\lambda=1}(T \ge 9) = 1 - e^{-3}\sum_{k=0}^{8}3^k/k! \approx .0038$.
