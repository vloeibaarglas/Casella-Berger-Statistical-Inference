**Central idea.** This is the CLT for exponential$(1)$ (mean $=$ sd $= 1$); $n\bar{X}_n = \sum X_i \sim \text{Gamma}(n,1)$, so differentiating the CLT approximation in $x$ exposes $\Gamma(n)$.

**Solution.** (a) $EX_i = \mathrm{Var}(X_i) = 1$, so by the CLT $\frac{\bar{X}_n-1}{1/\sqrt{n}} \to_d n(0,1)$.
(b) Let $S_n = n\bar{X}_n \sim \text{Gamma}(n,1)$ with pdf $s^{n-1}e^{-s}/\Gamma(n)$. Then $P\left(\frac{\bar{X}_n-1}{1/\sqrt{n}} \leq x\right) = P(S_n \leq n + x\sqrt{n})$; differentiating in $x$,
$$\frac{d}{dx}P(S_n \leq n+x\sqrt{n}) = \sqrt{n}\,f_{S_n}(n+x\sqrt{n}) = \frac{\sqrt{n}}{\Gamma(n)}(x\sqrt{n}+n)^{n-1}e^{-(x\sqrt{n}+n)} \approx \frac{d}{dx}\Phi(x) = \frac{e^{-x^2/2}}{\sqrt{2\pi}}.$$
At $x = 0$: $\frac{\sqrt{n}}{\Gamma(n)}n^{n-1}e^{-n} \approx \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}$, i.e., $\Gamma(n) \approx \sqrt{2\pi}\,n^{n-1/2}e^{-n}$, and $n! = n\Gamma(n) \approx \sqrt{2\pi}\,n^{n+1/2}e^{-n}$ — Stirling's formula.
