**Central idea.** Profile the likelihood: for fixed $\sigma^2$ the likelihood is maximized at the least squares $(\hat\alpha,\hat\beta)$; optimizing the remaining $-\frac{n}{2}\log\sigma^2 - \mathrm{RSS}/(2\sigma^2)$ gives $\mathrm{RSS}/n$.

**Solution.** The log likelihood is $-\frac{n}{2}\log(2\pi\sigma^2) - \frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\sum_i(y_i-\alpha-\beta x_i)^2$. For fixed $\sigma^2$, maximizing over $(\alpha,\beta)$ minimizes $\sum_i(y_i-\alpha-\beta x_i)^2$, attained at the least squares estimators $(\hat\alpha,\hat\beta)$ with value $\mathrm{RSS}$. Maximizing $-\frac{n}{2}\log\sigma^2 - \mathrm{RSS}/(2\sigma^2)$ over $\sigma^2 > 0$: derivative $-\frac{n}{2\sigma^2} + \frac{\mathrm{RSS}}{2\sigma^4} = 0$ gives $\hat\sigma^2 = \mathrm{RSS}/n = \frac{1}{n}\sum_i(y_i-\hat\alpha-\hat\beta x_i)^2$ (second derivative negative there).
