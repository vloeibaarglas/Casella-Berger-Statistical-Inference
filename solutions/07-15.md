**Central idea.** Profile $\lambda$ out of the log-likelihood; the profile is minimized by $\bar X$ via $\sum(x_i-\mu)^2/(\mu^2x_i)=\sum x_i/\mu^2-2n/\mu+\sum x_i^{-1}$. For (b), the algebraic identity $\sum(x_i-\mu)^2/x_i=n(\hat\mu_n-\mu)^2/\hat\mu_n+\mu^2n/\hat\lambda_n$ makes the joint density factor after a triangular change of variables.
**Solution.** (a) $\log L=\frac{n}{2}\log\lambda-\frac{3}{2}\sum\log x_i-\frac{\lambda}{2\mu^2}\sum\frac{(x_i-\mu)^2}{x_i}+c$. From $\partial\log L/\partial\lambda=0$, $\hat\lambda(\mu)=n\mu^2/\sum(x_i-\mu)^2/x_i$. The profile log-likelihood is maximized by minimizing $S(\mu)/\mu^2=\sum(x_i-\mu)^2/(\mu^2x_i)=\mu^{-2}\sum x_i-2n\mu^{-1}+\sum x_i^{-1}$, whose derivative $-2\sum x_i/\mu^3+2n/\mu^2$ vanishes at $\hat\mu_n=\bar X$ (a minimum). Since $\sum(x_i-\bar x)^2/x_i=\bar x^2\sum(1/x_i-1/\bar x)$, $\hat\lambda_n=n\bar x^2/\sum(x_i-\bar x)^2/x_i=n/[\sum x_i^{-1}-1/\bar x]$.
(b)(i) Put $m=(x_1+x_2)/2=\hat\mu_2$ and $u=x_1^{-1}+x_2^{-1}-2/m=2/\hat\lambda_2$. The key identity is
$$\frac{(x_1-\mu)^2}{x_1}+\frac{(x_2-\mu)^2}{x_2}=\frac{2(m-\mu)^2}{m}+\mu^2u,$$
(since $(x-\mu)^2/x=x-2\mu+\mu^2/x$). Transform $(x_1,x_2)\to(m,p=x_1x_2)$ (Jacobian factor $2/\sqrt{m^2-p}$ for the two orderings) and then $(m,p)\to(m,l=\hat\lambda_2)$ via $p=m^2l/(m+l)$ (Jacobian $m^3/(m+l)^2$). Using $x_1x_2=m^2l/(m+l)$ and $u=2/l$, the joint density becomes
$$f(m,l)=\frac{\lambda}{\pi}m^{-3/2}l^{-3/2}\exp\left\{-\frac{\lambda(m-\mu)^2}{\mu^2m}-\frac{\lambda}{l}\right\},$$
which factors as $\mathrm{IG}(\mu,2\lambda)$ in $m$ times the density of $l$ under $2\lambda/l\sim\chi^2_1$ (check: $(\lambda/(\pi m^3))^{1/2}e^{-\lambda(m-\mu)^2/(\mu^2m)}\times(2\pi)^{-1/2}(2\lambda)^{1/2}l^{-3/2}e^{-\lambda/l}$). Hence $\hat\mu_2\sim\mathrm{IG}(\mu,2\lambda)\perp\!\!\!\perp 2\lambda/\hat\lambda_2\sim\chi^2_1$.
(ii) Write $m_k=\hat\mu_k$, $v_k=k/\hat\lambda_k=\sum_{i\le k}(x_i^{-1}-m_k^{-1})$. The recursive updates are $m_{k+1}=(km_k+x)/(k+1)$ and $(k+1)/l_{k+1}=k/l_k+k/m_k+1/x-(k+1)/m_{k+1}$. By the induction hypothesis and independence of $x$, $f(x,m_k,l_k)=\mathrm{IG}(x\mid\mu,\lambda)\,\mathrm{IG}(m_k\mid\mu,k\lambda)\,f_{\chi^2_{k-1}}(k\lambda/l_k)\,(k\lambda/l_k^2)$. The exponent decomposes as
$$-\frac{k\lambda(m_k-\mu)^2}{2\mu^2m_k}-\frac{\lambda(x-\mu)^2}{2\mu^2x}-\frac{k\lambda}{2l_k}=-\frac{(k+1)\lambda(m_{k+1}-\mu)^2}{2\mu^2m_{k+1}}-\frac{(k+1)\lambda}{2l_{k+1}}-[\text{terms free of }(\mu,\lambda)],$$
using $\sum_{i\le k+1}(x_i-\mu)^2/x_i=(k+1)(m_{k+1}-\mu)^2/m_{k+1}+\mu^2(k+1)/l_{k+1}$. The transformation $(m_k,l_k)\to(m_{k+1},l_{k+1})$ (at fixed $x$) is triangular with Jacobian combining with the $m^{-3/2}l^{-3/2}$-type prefactors to produce exactly the $\mathrm{IG}(\mu,(k+1)\lambda)$ and $\chi^2_k$ normalizing constants. Thus $f(x,m_{k+1},l_{k+1})$ factors as $[\mathrm{IG}(\mu,(k+1)\lambda)\text{ in }m_{k+1}]\times[\chi^2_k\text{ in }(k+1)\lambda/l_{k+1}]\times[\text{free of }(\mu,\lambda)]$, proving $\hat\mu_{k+1}\sim\mathrm{IG}(\mu,(k+1)\lambda)\perp\!\!\!\perp(k+1)\lambda/\hat\lambda_{k+1}\sim\chi^2_k$.
