**Central idea.** Multinomial counting over the gaps between successive order statistics (marginal); conditioning on one group fixes cut points, splitting the remaining sample into independent truncated subsamples in each gap (conditional).

**Solution.** (a) With $i_0 = 0$, $i_{l+1} = n+1$, $x_0 = -\infty$, $x_{l+1} = +\infty$: the marginal cdf is the multinomial sum
$$P(X_{(i_1)} \leq x_1, \ldots, X_{(i_l)} \leq x_l) = \sum_{\text{valid }(c_0,\ldots,c_l)} \frac{n!}{\prod c_k!}\prod_{k=0}^{l}\big[F_X(x_{k+1})-F_X(x_k)\big]^{c_k},$$
over count vectors with $\sum_{t \leq k} c_t \geq i_{k+1}-1$-type constraints; differentiating gives the joint pdf for $x_1 < \cdots < x_l$:
$$f(x_1,\ldots,x_l) = \frac{n!}{\prod_{k=0}^{l}(i_{k+1}-i_k-1)!}\,[F_X(x_1)]^{i_1-1}\prod_{k=2}^{l}\big[F_X(x_k)-F_X(x_{k-1})\big]^{i_k-i_{k-1}-1}[1-F_X(x_l)]^{n-i_l}\prod_{k=1}^{l}f_X(x_k).$$
(b) Given $X_{(j_1)} = y_1, \ldots, X_{(j_m)} = y_m$, the $y$'s partition the line into gaps; the $n-m$ unobserved $X$'s fall independently into these gaps, and within each gap they are iid from $F_X$ truncated to that gap. Each $X_{(i_t)}$ lying in a gap is then an order statistic of that gap's subsample from the truncated distribution. E.g., if all $j$'s precede all $i$'s, given $X_{(j_m)} = y$ the vector $(X_{(i_1)},\ldots,X_{(i_l)})$ has the joint order-statistic distribution (as in (a)) of a sample of size $n-j_m$ from cdf $[F_X(x)-F_X(y)]/[1-F_X(y)]$, $x > y$, with indices $i_t - j_m$. The conditional cdf is the corresponding multinomial sum with these truncated cell probabilities.
