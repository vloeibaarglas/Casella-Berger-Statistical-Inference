**Central idea.** Each is (up to an irrelevant constant factor) the integral $\int dy/\sqrt{v(y)}$ for the stated mean–variance relationship.

**Solution.** (a) Poisson: $v(\theta) = \theta$, so $\int dy/\sqrt{y} = 2\sqrt{y} \propto \sqrt{y}$.
(b) For the binomial count, $\theta = np$ and $v(\theta) = \theta(1 - \theta/n)$. Then $\int \frac{dy}{\sqrt{y(1-y/n)}} = \sqrt{n}\int \frac{dy}{\sqrt{y(n-y)}}$. With $y = n\sin^2 t$, $dy = 2n\sin t\cos t\,dt$ and $\sqrt{y(n-y)} = n\sin t\cos t$, giving $2\sqrt{n}\,t = 2\sqrt{n}\sin^{-1}(\sqrt{y/n}) \propto \sin^{-1}(\sqrt{y/n})$.
(c) $\int dy/\sqrt{Ky^2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{K}}\log y \propto \log y$.
