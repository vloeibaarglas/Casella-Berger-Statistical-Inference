**Central idea.** Transform the joint pdf of $(X_{(1)}, X_{(n)})$ via $(W, V) = (U/V, V)$; the Jacobian factor $v$ combines with $(v-u)^{n-2}$ to split into a function of $w$ times a function of $v$.

**Solution.** $f_{X_{(1)},X_{(n)}}(u,v) = n(n-1)(v-u)^{n-2}/\theta^n$, $0 < u < v < \theta$. With $w = u/v$, $v = v$ (Jacobian $|J| = v$),
$$f_{W,V}(w,v) = n(n-1)\frac{(v-wv)^{n-2}}{\theta^n}\cdot v = \underbrace{(n-1)(1-w)^{n-2}\mathbf{1}_{(0,1)}(w)}_{\text{Beta}(1,n-1)\text{ pdf}}\cdot\underbrace{\frac{nv^{\,n-1}}{\theta^n}\mathbf{1}_{(0,\theta)}(v)}_{\text{pdf of }X_{(n)}},$$
which factors. Hence $X_{(1)}/X_{(n)} \sim \text{Beta}(1,n-1)$ independent of $X_{(n)}$.
