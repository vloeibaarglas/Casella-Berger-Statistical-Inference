**Central idea.** The $\beta$-interval has length $\propto 1/a - 1/b$, not $b-a$; differentiating the coverage constraint gives $db/da = f(a)/f(b)$, and the length FOC becomes $f(a)a^2 = f(b)b^2$.

**Solution.** (a) Minimize $\phi(a) = 1/a - 1/b(a)$ subject to $\int_a^{b(a)} f_Y = 1-\alpha$. Differentiating the constraint: $-f(a) + f(b)b'(a) = 0$, so $b'(a) = f(a)/f(b)$. $\phi'(a) = -1/a^2 + b'(a)/b^2 = -1/a^2 + f(a)/(f(b)b^2) = 0 \iff f(a)a^2 = f(b)b^2$.
(b) $Y = X/\beta \sim \text{gamma}(k,1)$ is a pivot; $\{\beta : x/b \le \beta \le x/a\}$ has length $x(1/a-1/b)$. By (a), the shortest $1-\alpha$ interval uses $a,b$ with $\int_a^b f_Y = 1-\alpha$ and $f_Y(b)b^2 = f_Y(a)a^2$.
