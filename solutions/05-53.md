**Central idea.** A simulation sanity check: with 1000 draws the sample moments should land within a standard error or two of the theoretical mean/variance, and the histogram should track the pmf.

**Solution.** Using the algorithms of 5.52 (seed 20261004, 1000 draws each):

| Distribution | Sample mean (theoretical) | Sample variance (theoretical) |
|---|---|---|
| Binomial$(8,\tfrac23)$ | 5.374 (5.333) | 1.814 (1.778) |
| Hypergeometric$(10,8,4)$ | 3.176 (3.200) | 0.415 (0.427) |
| Neg. binomial$(5,\tfrac13)$ | 9.572 (10.000) | 26.203 (30.000) |

Theoretical values: binomial mean $8\cdot\frac23 = \frac{16}{3}$, var $8\cdot\frac23\cdot\frac13 = \frac{16}{9}$; hypergeometric mean $4\cdot\frac{8}{10} = 3.2$, var $4\cdot\frac{8}{10}\cdot\frac{2}{10}\cdot\frac{10-4}{10-1} = 0.4267$; negative binomial (failures before 5th success) mean $5\cdot\frac{2/3}{1/3} = 10$, var $5\cdot\frac{2/3}{(1/3)^2} = 30$. All sample moments agree with theory within simulation error (e.g., se of the binomial mean $\approx \sqrt{1.78/1000} \approx 0.042$), and the histograms (not shown) track the pmfs — the generators are working.
