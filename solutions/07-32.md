**Central idea.** This is sampling–importance–resampling: the self-normalized weights converge to the posterior, and conditioning the resample on the prior draws can only reduce variance.
**Solution.** (a) $P(\theta^*\le t\mid\theta_{1:m})=\sum_{i:\theta_i\le t}q_i=\frac{\frac1m\sum_i\mathbf{1}_{\{\theta_i\le t\}}L(\theta_i\mid\mathbf{x})}{\frac1m\sum_i L(\theta_i\mid\mathbf{x})}\to\frac{\int_{-\infty}^t L(\theta\mid\mathbf{x})\pi(\theta)\,d\theta}{\int L(\theta\mid\mathbf{x})\pi(\theta)\,d\theta}=\int_{-\infty}^t\pi(\theta\mid\mathbf{x})\,d\theta$ a.s. as $m\to\infty$, by the SLLN (numerator and denominator).
(b) Conditionally on $\theta_{1:m}$, the $\theta_j^*$ are iid from the discrete law $\{q_i\}$; as $m,r\to\infty$, $\frac1r\sum_j h(\theta_j^*)\to E[h(\theta)\mid\mathbf{x}]$ by the SLLN and part (a).
(c) Given $\theta_{1:m}$, $\theta_j^*$ equals $\theta_i$ with probability $q_i$, so $E[h(\theta_j^*)\mid\theta_{1:m}]=\sum_i q_ih(\theta_i)$, the self-normalized importance-sampling estimator. It has the same unconditional mean as $\frac1r\sum_j h(\theta_j^*)$ (tower property), and $\mathrm{Var}\big(\frac1r\sum_j h(\theta_j^*)\big)=\mathrm{Var}\big(E[\cdot\mid\theta_{1:m}]\big)+E\big[\mathrm{Var}(\cdot\mid\theta_{1:m})\big]\ge\mathrm{Var}\big(\sum_i q_ih(\theta_i)\big)$.
