**Central idea.** Factorial moments $E[X(X-1)]$ (discrete) and gamma/beta integrals (continuous) give $EX^2$; subtract $(EX)^2$.

**Solution.** (a) $E[X(X-1)] = \sum_{x\ge2}\frac{e^{-\lambda}\lambda^x}{(x-2)!} = \lambda^2$; $EX^2=\lambda^2+\lambda$; Var$X=\lambda$. (b) $x(x-1)\binom{r+x-1}{x} = r(r+1)\binom{r+x-1}{x-2}$, so $E[X(X-1)] = r(r+1)(1-p)^2p^{-2}\sum_{y\ge0}\binom{r+y+1}{y}p^{r+2}(1-p)^y = \frac{r(r+1)(1-p)^2}{p^2}$ (the sum is a negative binomial$(r+2,p)$ pmf). Then Var$X = \frac{r(r+1)(1-p)^2}{p^2}+\frac{r(1-p)}{p}-\frac{r^2(1-p)^2}{p^2} = \frac{r(1-p)}{p^2}$. (c) $EX=\alpha\beta$, $EX^2=\beta^2(\alpha+1)\alpha$ by (3.17); Var$X=\alpha\beta^2$. (d) $EX=\frac{B(\alpha+1,\beta)}{B(\alpha,\beta)}=\frac{\alpha}{\alpha+\beta}$; $EX^2=\frac{(\alpha+1)\alpha}{(\alpha+\beta+1)(\alpha+\beta)}$; Var$X=\frac{\alpha\beta}{(\alpha+\beta)^2(\alpha+\beta+1)}$. (e) $EX=\mu$ by symmetry; Var$X=E[(X-\mu)^2]=\int_0^\infty\frac{u^2}{\sigma}e^{-u/\sigma}du=2\sigma^2$.
