**Central idea.** Two iid uniform break points give three piece lengths; a triangle is possible iff every piece is shorter than half the stick, and each ordering of the breaks contributes the same triangular region.

**Solution.** Let the break points be $X,Y$ iid uniform$(0,1)$. By symmetry take $X<Y$ (probability $1/2$ each way); the pieces are $X$, $Y-X$, $1-Y$. They form a triangle iff each is $<1/2$, i.e. $X<1/2$, $Y-X<1/2$, $Y>1/2$: a triangle in $\{0<x<y<1\}$ with vertices $(0,1/2),(1/2,1/2),(1/2,1)$, area $1/8$. Doubling for $Y<X$ gives $1/4$.
