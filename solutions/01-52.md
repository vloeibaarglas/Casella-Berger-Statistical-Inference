**Central idea.** $g$ is the conditional density of $X$ given $X \geq x_0$ — just renormalize the tail to integrate to 1.

**Solution.** $g(x) \geq 0$ since $f \geq 0$ and $1 - F(x_0) > 0$. Moreover,
$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} g(x)\,dx = \frac{1}{1-F(x_0)}\int_{x_0}^{\infty} f(x)\,dx = \frac{1-F(x_0)}{1-F(x_0)} = 1.$$
