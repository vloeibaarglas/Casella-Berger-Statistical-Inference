**Central idea.** (a) Random-walk Metropolis with $n(0,1)$ steps targeting $t_v$; (b) accept-reject with Cauchy candidate ($M_v = \sup t_v/\text{Cauchy} < \infty$ since the $t_v$ density has tails $|x|^{-(v+1)}$, no heavier than Cauchy when $v\ge1$); (c) $T = Z/\sqrt{V/v}$ is exact. Simulation confirms: direct is exact, iid, and fastest.

**Solution.** (a) Target $\pi(x) \propto (1+x^2/v)^{-(v+1)/2}$. Start $X_0 \sim n(0,1)$; iterate: propose $X^*= X_t + Z$, $Z \sim n(0,1)$; accept with prob $\min(1, \pi(X^*)/\pi(X_t))$. After burn-in the chain is approximately $t_v$.
(b) This envelope argument needs $v\ge1$; for $0<v<1$ the density ratio diverges at infinity. Candidate $g(x) = \frac{1}{\pi(1+x^2)}$ (Cauchy). $M_v = \sup_x f_v(x)/g(x) < \infty$: e.g., $M_2 \approx 1.209$ (ratio $\propto (1+x^2)(1+x^2/2)^{-3/2}$, max at $|x| = 1$). Generate $C \sim$ Cauchy, $U \sim$ uniform$(0,1)$; accept $C$ if $U < f_v(C)/(M_vg(C))$; accepted draws are exactly $t_v$.
(c) Generate $Z \sim n(0,1)$ (Box–Muller) and $V \sim \chi^2_v$ (sum of $v$ squared normals, or $-2\sum\log U_i$ if $v$ even) independently; $T = Z/\sqrt{V/v} \sim t_v$ exactly.
(d) Samples of 100 (seed 11):

| $v$ | Direct (c) | Accept/Reject (b) | Metropolis (a) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | mean 0.33, var 6.35 (th: var $=\infty$) | mean 0.38, var 5.98, $M=1.21$, 1.17 draws/accept | mean $-0.02$, var 5.11, acc. 0.76 |
| 10 | mean 0.23, var 1.61 (th 1.25) | mean $-0.09$, var 1.64, $M=1.45$, 1.68 draws/accept | mean 0.06, var 1.25, acc. 0.72 |
| 25 | mean 0.11, var 1.10 (th 1.09) | mean $-0.08$, var 1.33, $M=1.49$, 1.38 draws/accept | mean 0.16, var 1.20, acc. 0.71 |

All three produce reasonable $t_v$ samples, but the **direct transformation (c)** is preferred: it is exact (iid draws, no burn-in, no autocorrelation) and fastest (100 draws in ~1ms vs. ~700ms for accept/reject with its Cauchy draws and $M$ evaluation, ~5ms for Metropolis plus burn-in). Accept/reject is exact but wasteful; Metropolis is only approximate and its draws are dependent — its advantage (no supremum needed) doesn't buy anything here since the supremum is easy.
