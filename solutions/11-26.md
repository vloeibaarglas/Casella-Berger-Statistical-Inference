**Central idea.** $S_{xx}$ is convex in each coordinate separately, so at the maximum every $x_i$ sits at an endpoint; among endpoint allocations, $j(n-j)(f-e)^2/n$ is maximized at $j = n/2$.

**Solution.** Fix all coordinates but $x_k$: $S_{xx} = \sum x_i^2 - (\sum x_i)^2/n$ is quadratic in $x_k$ with positive leading coefficient $(1-1/n) > 0$, hence convex in $x_k$ — its maximum over $[e,f]$ is at an endpoint. So at any maximizer, each $x_i \in \{e,f\}$. If $j$ of them equal $e$ (and $n-j$ equal $f$), then $\bar x = (je+(n-j)f)/n$ and
$$S_{xx} = j(e-\bar x)^2 + (n-j)(f-\bar x)^2 = \frac{j(n-j)}{n}(f-e)^2,$$
maximized over $j$ at $j = n/2$ ( $n$ even). Hence half at $e$, half at $f$ maximizes $S_{xx}$ (and minimizes $\mathrm{Var}\,b = \sigma^2/S_{xx}$).
