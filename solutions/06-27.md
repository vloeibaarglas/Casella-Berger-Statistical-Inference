**Central idea.** Expanding $(x-\mu)^2/(\mu^2x) = x/\mu^2 - 2/\mu + 1/x$ reveals a two-parameter exponential family in $(\sum X_i,\sum 1/X_i)$ with an open natural parameter space; for $n=2$ the $(S,U)\mapsto(\bar{X},W)$ Jacobian computation factors the joint density.

**Solution.** (a) Since $\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{\mu^2x} = \frac{x}{\mu^2}-\frac{2}{\mu}+\frac{1}{x}$,
$$\prod_{i=1}^n f(x_i\mid\mu,\lambda) = \left(\frac{\lambda}{2\pi}\right)^{n/2}\!\!\Big(\prod x_i^{-3/2}\Big)\exp\!\left(-\frac{\lambda}{2\mu^2}\sum x_i + \frac{n\lambda}{\mu} - \frac{\lambda}{2}\sum\frac{1}{x_i}\right),$$
a two-parameter exponential family in $\big(\sum X_i,\sum \frac{1}{X_i}\big)$ with natural parameters $\eta_1 = -\lambda/(2\mu^2)$, $\eta_2 = -\lambda/2$ ranging over the open set $(-\infty,0)^2$. $(\bar{X},T)$ is 1–1 with $\big(\sum X_i,\sum 1/X_i\big)$ (via $\sum 1/X_i = n/T + 1/\bar{X}$), hence sufficient by Theorem 6.2.10 and complete by Theorem 6.2.25.
(b) With the corrected $T^*$, put $S = X_1+X_2 = 2\bar{X}$ and $W = \lambda\big(\frac{1}{X_1}+\frac{1}{X_2}-\frac{4}{S}\big) = 2\lambda/T^*$. Writing $X_1 = SU$, $X_2 = S(1-U)$ (Jacobian $S$) and changing $U \to W$ (two symmetric branches), the joint density becomes
$$h(s,w) = \underbrace{\sqrt{\frac{2\lambda}{\pi}}\,s^{-3/2}\exp\!\left(-\frac{\lambda s}{2\mu^2}+\frac{2\lambda}{\mu}-\frac{2\lambda}{s}\right)}_{\text{density of }S = 2\bar{X}\ (\text{so }\bar{X} = S/2 \sim \mathrm{IG}(\mu,2\lambda))}\ \cdot\ \underbrace{\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}w^{-1/2}e^{-w/2}}_{\chi^2_1\ \text{density}},$$
using $\frac{1}{U(1-U)} = \frac{sw}{\lambda}+4$ and $|dU/dW| = \frac{s}{\lambda}\big/\big[(\frac{sw}{\lambda}+4)^{3/2}\sqrt{\frac{sw}{\lambda}}\big]$. Thus $\bar{X}\sim\text{IG}(\mu,2\lambda)$, $W = n\lambda/T^*\sim\chi^2_1 = \chi^2_{n-1}$, and they are independent.
