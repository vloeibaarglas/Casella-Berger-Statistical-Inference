**Central idea.** A rectangle probability is an alternating sum of four cdf values; the product form factors that sum.

**Solution.** $P(a\le X\le b,\ c\le Y\le d) = F_{X,Y}(b,d)-F_{X,Y}(a,d)-F_{X,Y}(b,c)+F_{X,Y}(a,c)$. Substituting $F_{X,Y}(x,y)=F_X(x)F_Y(y)$ gives $F_X(b)F_Y(d)-F_X(a)F_Y(d)-F_X(b)F_Y(c)+F_X(a)F_Y(c) = (F_X(b)-F_X(a))(F_Y(d)-F_Y(c)) = P(a\le X\le b)P(c\le Y\le d)$.
