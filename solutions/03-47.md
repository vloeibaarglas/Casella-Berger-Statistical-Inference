**Central idea.** Write the desired lower bound as $h(t)=(1+t^2)P(Z>t)-t\phi(t)\ge0$; $h$ is decreasing (by the upper bound of Example 3.6.3) and $h(t)\to0$.

**Solution.** It suffices to show $P(Z>t)\ge\frac{t}{1+t^2}\phi(t)$, then double for $|Z|$. Put $h(t)=(1+t^2)P(Z>t)-t\phi(t)$ for $t\ge0$. Then $h'(t)=2tP(Z>t)-(1+t^2)\phi(t)-\phi(t)+t^2\phi(t)=2\big(tP(Z>t)-\phi(t)\big)\le0$ by Example 3.6.3 ($P(Z>t)\le\phi(t)/t$). Also $h(t)\to0$: $0\le(1+t^2)P(Z>t)\le(1+t^2)\phi(t)/t\to0$ and $t\phi(t)\to0$. A decreasing function with limit 0 is nonnegative: $h(t)\ge0$, i.e. $P(Z>t)\ge\frac{t\phi(t)}{1+t^2}$. Doubling gives $P(|Z|\ge t)\ge\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\frac{t}{1+t^2}e^{-t^2/2}$.
