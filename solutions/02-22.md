**Central idea.** The substitution $u=x^2/\beta^2$ turns every moment into a gamma integral.

**Solution.** (a) $f\ge0$, and with $u=x^2/\beta^2$ ($x^2dx=\frac{\beta^3}{2}u^{1/2}du$),
$$\int_0^\infty x^2e^{-x^2/\beta^2}dx=\frac{\beta^3}{2}\Gamma(\tfrac32)=\frac{\beta^3\sqrt\pi}{4},$$
so $\int_0^\infty f\,dx=1$.
(b) $EX=\frac{4}{\beta^3\sqrt\pi}\int_0^\infty x^3e^{-x^2/\beta^2}dx=\frac{4}{\beta^3\sqrt\pi}\cdot\frac{\beta^4}{2}\Gamma(2)=\frac{2\beta}{\sqrt\pi}$. $EX^2=\frac{4}{\beta^3\sqrt\pi}\cdot\frac{\beta^5}{2}\Gamma(\tfrac52)=\frac{2\beta^2}{\sqrt\pi}\cdot\frac{3\sqrt\pi}{4}=\frac{3\beta^2}{2}$. Hence $\mathrm{Var}X=\frac{3\beta^2}{2}-\frac{4\beta^2}{\pi}=\beta^2\left(\frac32-\frac{4}{\pi}\right)$.
