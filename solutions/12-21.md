**Central idea.** One application of the delta method to $g(\pi)=\log(\pi/(1-\pi))$: $g'(\pi)=1/[\pi(1-\pi)]$ squares against $\mathrm{Var}\,\hat\pi=\pi(1-\pi)/n$.

**Solution.** $g(\pi)=\log(\pi/(1-\pi))$ has $g'(\pi)=1/\pi+1/(1-\pi)=1/[\pi(1-\pi)]$. By the delta method,
$$\mathrm{Var}\,W\approx [g'(\pi)]^2\mathrm{Var}\,\hat\pi=\frac{1}{\pi^2(1-\pi)^2}\cdot\frac{\pi(1-\pi)}{n}=\frac{1}{n\pi(1-\pi)},$$
and plugging in the MLE $\hat\pi$ gives the variance estimate $1/[n\hat\pi(1-\hat\pi)]$.
