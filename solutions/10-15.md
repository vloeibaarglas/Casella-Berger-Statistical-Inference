**Central idea.** For fixed data, bootstrap resamples are iid draws from the bootstrap distribution: sample variances converge to the population variance (SLLN), and averages of iid unbiased copies converge to their mean (WLLN).

**Solution.** (a) Conditional on the data, $\hat\theta_1^*,\ldots,\hat\theta_B^*$ are iid from the bootstrap distribution of $\hat\theta^*$. By the SLLN, $\frac{1}{B-1}\sum_{i=1}^B(\hat\theta_i^*-\bar{\hat\theta}^*)^2 \xrightarrow{a.s.} E^*[(\hat\theta^*-E^*\hat\theta^*)^2] = \mathrm{Var}^*(\hat\theta)$.
(b) For fixed $B$, each $\mathrm{Var}^*_{B_i}(\hat\theta)$ is the sample variance of $B$ iid bootstrap draws, hence the $\mathrm{Var}^*_{B_i}$ are iid across $i$ with $E[\mathrm{Var}^*_{B_i}(\hat\theta)]=\mathrm{Var}^*(\hat\theta)$ (the $B-1$ divisor makes it unbiased). By the WLLN, $\frac1m\sum_{i=1}^m\mathrm{Var}^*_{B_i}(\hat\theta)\xrightarrow{p}\mathrm{Var}^*(\hat\theta)$.
