**Central idea.** Split $|a_nW_n+b_n-\theta| \le |a_n||W_n-\theta| + |a_n-1||\theta| + |b_n|$; the deterministic pieces vanish, leaving only $|W_n-\theta|$.

**Solution.** For any $\eta>0$,
$$|U_n-\theta| = |a_n(W_n-\theta) + (a_n-1)\theta + b_n| \le |a_n||W_n-\theta| + |a_n-1||\theta| + |b_n|.$$
Since $a_n\to 1$, $|a_n|\le 2$ eventually; choose $N$ so that for $n\ge N$, $|a_n-1||\theta|<\eta$ and $|b_n|<\eta$. Then for $n\ge N$,
$$\{|U_n-\theta|>4\eta\} \subseteq \{2|W_n-\theta|>\!2\eta\} = \{|W_n-\theta|>\eta\},$$
so $P(|U_n-\theta|>4\eta) \le P(|W_n-\theta|>\eta)\to 0$ by consistency of $W_n$. Since $\eta>0$ is arbitrary, $U_n \xrightarrow{p}\theta$.
