**Central idea.** Induction on $n$, or the multiply-by-$(1-t)$ telescoping trick.

**Solution.** (1.5.4): $\sum_{k=1}^{n}t^{k-1} = \frac{1-t^n}{1-t}$ ($t \neq 1$). By induction: true for $n=1$; assuming it for $n$, $\sum_{k=1}^{n+1}t^{k-1} = \frac{1-t^n}{1-t} + t^n = \frac{1-t^{n+1}}{1-t}$. (Equivalently, $(1-t)\sum_{k=1}^{n}t^{k-1} = 1-t^n$ telescopes.)
