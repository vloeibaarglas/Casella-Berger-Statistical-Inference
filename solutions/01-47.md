**Central idea.** Each is nondecreasing (positive derivative), right-continuous, with limits $0$ and $1$ — the cdf checklist.

**Solution.** In each case $F$ is continuous (hence right-continuous) with $F'(x) \geq 0$. (a) $\tan^{-1}$ increases from $-\pi/2$ to $\pi/2$: $F: 0 \to 1$ (Cauchy). (b) $e^{-x}$ decreases $\infty \to 0$: $F: 0 \to 1$ (logistic). (c) $e^{-x}$ decreases, so $-e^{-x}$ increases and $e^{-e^{-x}}$ increases from $0$ to $1$ (Gumbel). (d) On $(0,\infty)$ (with $F = 0$ for $x \leq 0$): increasing from $0$ to $1$ (exponential). (e) From (1.5.6), $F_Y(y) = \frac{1-\varepsilon}{1+e^{-y}}$ for $y<0$ and $\varepsilon + \frac{1-\varepsilon}{1+e^{-y}}$ for $y \geq 0$: the logistic pieces increase, there is an upward jump of height $\varepsilon > 0$ at $y=0$ (right-continuous there), $\lim_{y\to-\infty}F_Y = 0$, $\lim_{y\to\infty}F_Y = \varepsilon + (1-\varepsilon) = 1$.
