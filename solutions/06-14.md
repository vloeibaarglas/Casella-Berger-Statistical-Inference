**Central idea.** A location shift moves the median and the mean by the same amount, so their difference is shift-invariant.

**Solution.** Write $X_i = Z_i + \theta$ with $Z_i$ iid from the base distribution (free of $\theta$). The sample median satisfies $M = M_Z + \theta$ and $\bar{X} = \bar{Z} + \theta$, so $M - \bar{X} = M_Z - \bar{Z}$, free of $\theta$. Hence $M - \bar{X}$ is ancillary (a special case of the general location-family result in Exercise 6.11(a)).
