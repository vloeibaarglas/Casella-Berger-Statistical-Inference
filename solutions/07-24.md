**Central idea.** Poisson–gamma conjugacy: add the total count to the shape and the sample size to the rate.
**Solution.** (a) $\pi(\lambda\mid\mathbf{x})\propto\lambda^{\sum x_i}e^{-n\lambda}\cdot\lambda^{\alpha-1}e^{-\lambda/\beta}=\lambda^{\alpha+n\bar x-1}\exp\{-\lambda(n+1/\beta)\}$, i.e. $\mathrm{gamma}(\alpha+n\bar x,\beta/(n\beta+1))$ (shape–scale).
(b) Posterior mean $\dfrac{\alpha\beta+n\beta\bar x}{n\beta+1}$ (a weighted average of the prior mean $\alpha\beta$ and $\bar x$) and posterior variance $(\alpha+n\bar x)\big(\frac{\beta}{\,n\beta+1\,}\big)^2$.
