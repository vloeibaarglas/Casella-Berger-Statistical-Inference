**Central idea.** L'Hôpital's rule gives continuity at $\lambda = 0$; inverting the Exercise 11.1(b) construction ($g' = 1/\sqrt{v}$) recovers the variance function each power stabilizes.

**Solution.** (a) For $\lambda \neq 0$, $(y^\lambda-1)/\lambda$ is continuous in $\lambda$. At $\lambda = 0$, l'Hôpital gives $\lim_{\lambda\to 0}(y^\lambda - 1)/\lambda = \lim_{\lambda\to 0} y^\lambda \log y = \log y$, which is the defined value — hence continuous.
(b) For $\lambda \neq 0$, $\frac{d}{dy}g^*_\lambda(y) = y^{\lambda-1}$; for $\lambda = 0$, $\frac{d}{dy}\log y = 1/y = y^{-1}$, the same formula. By 11.1(b), stabilization requires $[g'(\theta)]^2 v(\theta)$ constant, i.e. $v(\theta) \propto \theta^{2(1-\lambda)}$ (take $v(\theta) = \theta^{2(1-\lambda)}$). Check: $\lambda = 0$ gives $v(\theta) = \theta^2$, agreeing with 11.2(c).
