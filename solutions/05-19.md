**Central idea.** Couple the variables on one probability space so the inequality holds pointwise: $\chi^2_p \stackrel{d}{=} \chi^2_q + \chi^2_{p-q} \geq \chi^2_q$ a.s.; the same coupling with a shared denominator lifts stochastic monotonicity to $kF_{k,v}$ and then to the scaled cutoffs.

**Solution.** (a) By gamma additivity, $\chi^2_p \stackrel{d}{=} \chi^2_q + \chi^2_{p-q}$ with independent summands, and $\chi^2_{p-q} > 0$ a.s. Hence, on a common space, $\chi^2_p > \chi^2_q$ a.s., so $P(\chi^2_p > a) \geq P(\chi^2_q > a)$ for all $a$, with strict inequality e.g. for $a$ between typical values (indeed $P(\chi^2_p > a) - P(\chi^2_q > a) = P(\chi^2_q \leq a < \chi^2_q + \chi^2_{p-q}) > 0$ for every $a > 0$, since all densities are positive on $(0,\infty)$).
(b) $kF_{k,v} \stackrel{d}{=} \chi^2_k/(\chi^2_v/v)$. Couple nested numerators $\chi^2_1 \subset \chi^2_2 \subset \cdots$ (partial sums of iid $\chi^2_1$) with a single independent denominator $W \sim \chi^2_v$: then $kF_{k,v} \stackrel{d}{=} U_k/(W/v)$ is a.s. increasing in $k$, hence stochastically increasing.
(c) Let $c_k = kF_{\alpha,k,v}$, so $P(kF_{k,v} > c_k) = \alpha$. If $c_k \leq c_{k-1}$, then $\alpha = P(kF_{k,v} > c_k) \geq P(kF_{k,v} > c_{k-1}) > P((k-1)F_{k-1,v} > c_{k-1}) = \alpha$, where the strict inequality is the strict stochastic dominance from (b) at the fixed point $c_{k-1} > 0$ (same coupling argument as in (a): the increment is a.s. positive with density support $(0,\infty)$). Contradiction; thus $c_k > c_{k-1}$.
