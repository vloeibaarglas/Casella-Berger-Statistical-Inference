**Central idea.** $X \sim$ Binomial$(1000,1/2)$ counts theater-A choosers; "turn away" is $\{X > N\}$; invert the normal tail at $0.01$.

**Solution.** (a) $P(X > N) = \sum_{k=N+1}^{1000}\binom{1000}{k}2^{-1000} < 0.01$; the required $N$ is the smallest integer satisfying this. (b) With $\mu = 500$, $\sigma = \sqrt{250} \approx 15.81$: $P(X > N) \approx 1-\Phi((N+0.5-500)/15.81) < 0.01$ requires $(N-499.5)/15.81 \ge 2.326$, i.e. $N \ge 536.3$, so $N = 537$ (check: $1-\Phi(37.5/15.81) = 1-\Phi(2.372) \approx 0.0089 < 0.01$).
