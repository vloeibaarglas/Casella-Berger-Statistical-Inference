**Central idea.** The scale MLE is the sample minimum; profiling it out leaves a one-parameter problem in $T = \sum \log(X_i/\min X_i)$, and under $H_0$ the log-ratios are $n-1$ iid exponentials by the memoryless property, so $2T \sim \chi^2_{2(n-1)}$.

**Solution.** Write $m = \min_i X_i$. The likelihood is $L(\theta,v) = \theta^n v^{n\theta}(\prod x_i)^{-(\theta+1)} I(v \le m)$.
(a) For fixed $\theta$, $L$ increases in $v$, so $\hat v = m$. Profiling: $\ell(\theta) = n\log\theta + n\theta\log m - (\theta+1)\sum\log x_i$; $\ell'(\theta) = n/\theta + n\log m - \sum\log x_i = 0$ gives
$$\hat v = \min_i X_i, \qquad \hat\theta = \frac{n}{\sum_{i=1}^n \log(X_i/\hat v)} = \frac{n}{T}.$$
(b) Under $H_0$, $\hat v_0 = m$ as well, so with $T = \sum\log(x_i/m)$,
$$\log\lambda = -n\log\hat\theta + (\hat\theta-1)T = n\bigl[1 - u + \log u\bigr], \quad u = T/n,$$
using $\hat\theta = n/T$. The function $k(u) = 1-u+\log u \le 0$ equals $0$ only at $u=1$, strictly increasing on $(0,1)$ and strictly decreasing on $(1,\infty)$, with $k(u) \to -\infty$ at both ends. Hence $\{\log\lambda \le \log c\} = \{u \le c_1'\} \cup \{u \ge c_2'\}$, i.e. $\{T \le c_1\} \cup \{T \ge c_2\}$ with $0 < c_1 < c_2$ (and $T \ge 0$ always, $T = 0$ iff all $X_i$ are equal).
(c) Under $H_0$ ($\theta = 1$), $Y_i = \log(X_i/v)$ are iid exponential(1). Then $T = \sum_i (Y_i - \min_j Y_j)$: one term is $0$ and, by the memoryless property, the other $n-1$ terms are iid exponential(1), independent of $\min_j Y_j$ (this is the hint's conditional argument — the conditional distribution of the ratios given the minimum is free of the minimum). Thus $T \sim \text{Gamma}(n-1, 1)$ and $2T \sim \chi^2_{2(n-1)}$: $2(n-1)$ degrees of freedom.
