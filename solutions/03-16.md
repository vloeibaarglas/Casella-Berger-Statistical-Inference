**Central idea.** (a) is one integration by parts; (b) is the Gaussian integral in disguise ($u=\sqrt{x}$).

**Solution.** (a) $\Gamma(\alpha+1) = \int_0^\infty x^\alpha e^{-x}dx = \big[-x^\alpha e^{-x}\big]_0^\infty + \alpha\int_0^\infty x^{\alpha-1}e^{-x}dx = \alpha\Gamma(\alpha)$. (b) $\Gamma(\tfrac12) = \int_0^\infty x^{-1/2}e^{-x}dx$; with $u=\sqrt{x}$ ($dx = 2u\,du$), $= 2\int_0^\infty e^{-u^2}du = \int_{-\infty}^\infty e^{-u^2}du = \sqrt{\pi}$.
