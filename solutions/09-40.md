**Central idea.** Equal endpoint densities plus symmetry $\Rightarrow$ symmetric interval; apply to the likelihood (in $\theta$) and to the posterior.

**Solution.** From 9.39, $b = 2m - a$: the optimal interval is $[m-k, m+k]$ with $k = m-a$. (a) If $L(\theta\mid x)$ is symmetric about the MLE $\hat\theta$, the likelihood region (9.2.7) $\{\theta : L(\theta\mid x) \ge kL(\hat\theta\mid x)\} = \{\theta : L(\theta\mid x) \ge k'\}$ is symmetric about $\hat\theta$ (provided the cutoff $k'$ is on the likelihood scale, not parameter-distorted). (b) If $\pi(\theta\mid x)$ is symmetric about its mean (= mode), the HPD region $\{\theta : \pi(\theta\mid x) \ge k\}$ from Corollary 9.3.10 is symmetric about the posterior mean by the same argument.
