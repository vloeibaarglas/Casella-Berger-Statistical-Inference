**Central idea.** (a) is the CLT; (b) is the delta method with $g(t) = t(1-t)$, $g'(p) = 1-2p$; (c) is the second-order delta method since $g'(1/2) = 0$: $Y_n(1-Y_n) - 1/4 = -(Y_n-1/2)^2$.

**Solution.** (a) Immediate from the CLT: $E[Y_n] = p$, $\mathrm{Var}(Y_n) = p(1-p)/n$.
(b) With $g(t) = t(1-t)$, $g'(t) = 1-2t \neq 0$ at $t = p \neq 1/2$; the delta method (5.43) gives $\sqrt{n}(g(Y_n)-g(p)) \to_d n(0, [g'(p)]^2p(1-p)) = n(0,(1-2p)^2p(1-p))$.
(c) For $p = 1/2$, $g'(1/2) = 0$ kills the first-order term. Note $Y_n(1-Y_n) - \tfrac{1}{4} = -(Y_n-\tfrac{1}{2})^2$, so
$$n\left[Y_n(1-Y_n)-\tfrac{1}{4}\right] = -\big(\sqrt{n}(Y_n-\tfrac{1}{2})\big)^2 \to_d -Z^2,$$
where $Z \sim n(0,1/4)$ by (a); $-Z^2 \stackrel{d}{=} -\tfrac{1}{4}\chi^2_1$. Equivalently $4n[\tfrac14 - Y_n(1-Y_n)] = (2\sqrt{n}(Y_n-\tfrac12))^2 \to_d \chi^2_1$.
