**Central idea.** Substitute $y=g(x)$ in the integral defining $Eg(X)$; monotonicity makes the change of variables legal and produces exactly the pdf of $Y$.

**Solution.** For $g$ increasing, $Eg(X)=\int_{-\infty}^\infty g(x)f_X(x)dx$; with $y=g(x)$ ($dy=g'(x)dx$) this becomes $\int_{-\infty}^\infty y\,f_X(g^{-1}(y))(g^{-1})'(y)dy=\int y f_Y(y)dy=EY$ by the change-of-variables formula for $f_Y$ (Theorem 2.1.5). If $g$ is decreasing the limits swap and $|(g^{-1})'|$ absorbs the sign, giving the same conclusion.
