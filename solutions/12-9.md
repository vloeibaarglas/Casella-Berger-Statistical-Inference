**Central idea.** Nonnegativity of the three variance estimates forces $\hat\beta$ and $S_{xy}$ to share a sign and squeezes $|\hat\beta|$ between the two OLS slopes.

**Solution.** (a) From (12.2.20), $\hat\sigma^2_\xi=S_{xy}/(n\hat\beta)$, so $\hat\sigma^2_\delta=S_{xx}/n-\hat\sigma^2_\xi=(1/n)(S_{xx}-S_{xy}/\hat\beta)\ge 0$ iff $S_{xx}\ge S_{xy}/\hat\beta$, and $\hat\sigma^2_\epsilon=S_{yy}/n-\hat\beta^2\hat\sigma^2_\xi=(1/n)(S_{yy}-\hat\beta S_{xy})\ge 0$ iff $S_{yy}\ge\hat\beta S_{xy}$.

(b) Since $\hat\sigma^2_\xi=S_{xy}/(n\hat\beta)\ge 0$, $S_{xy}$ and $\hat\beta$ have the same sign, so $S_{xy}/\hat\beta=|S_{xy}|/|\hat\beta|$ and $\hat\beta S_{xy}=|\hat\beta||S_{xy}|$. Then $S_{xx}\ge |S_{xy}|/|\hat\beta|$ gives $|\hat\beta|\ge |S_{xy}|/S_{xx}$, and $S_{yy}\ge |\hat\beta||S_{xy}|$ gives $|\hat\beta|\le S_{yy}/|S_{xy}|$.
