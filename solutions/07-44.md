**Central idea.** $\bar X$ is complete sufficient, so Lehmann–Scheffé blesses $\bar X^2-1/n$; Stein's identity gives $E\bar X^4=\theta^4+6\theta^2/n+3/n^2$, whence the variance exceeds the CRLB $4\theta^2/n$ by $2/n^2$.
**Solution.** $\bar X$ is complete sufficient for $\theta$ and $E_\theta\bar X^2=\theta^2+1/n$, so by Lehmann–Scheffé $\bar X^2-1/n$ is the UMVUE of $\theta^2$. Let $W=\bar X\sim\mathrm{n}(\theta,1/n)$. Stein's identity, $E_\theta[(W-\theta)h(W)]=\frac1nE_\theta[h'(W)]$: with $h(w)=w^2$, $E[W^3]=\theta^3+3\theta/n$; with $h(w)=w^3$, $E[W^4]=\theta E[W^3]+3E[W^2]/n=\theta^4+6\theta^2/n+3/n^2$. Hence $\mathrm{Var}(\bar X^2-1/n)=E[W^4]-(E[W^2])^2=4\theta^2/n+2/n^2$. The CRLB for $\theta^2$ is $[2\theta]^2/(n\cdot1)=4\theta^2/n$; the UMVUE's variance is larger by $2/n^2$.
