**Central idea.** Jensen on the convex exponential: $\mathrm{E}X = \mathrm{E}[e^Z] \ge e^{\mathrm{E}Z} = 1$, strict unless $Z$ (hence $X$) is degenerate.

**Solution.** $\mathrm{E}X = \mathrm{E}[e^Z] \ge e^{\mathrm{E}Z} = e^0 = 1$ by Jensen's inequality ($e^t$ convex). So $\mathrm{E}X \ge 1$, with equality iff $P(Z=0)=1$, i.e. $X=1$ a.s.; otherwise $\mathrm{E}X > 1$.
