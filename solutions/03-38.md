**Central idea.** Standardize: $\{X>x_\alpha\}=\{Z>z_\alpha\}$ for $Z=(X-\mu)/\sigma$, which has pdf $f$.

**Solution.** $P(X>x_\alpha)=P\left(\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}>\frac{x_\alpha-\mu}{\sigma}\right)=P(Z>z_\alpha)=\int_{z_\alpha}^\infty f(z)dz=\alpha$, since $Z=(X-\mu)/\sigma$ has pdf $f$ and $(x_\alpha-\mu)/\sigma=z_\alpha$.
