**Central idea.** Condition on the mixture indicator and use total variance; the cdf is a mixture whose contaminating piece vanishes because $1-p_n\to 0$ — convergence in distribution with exploding variance.

**Solution.** Write $Y_n\mid W_n=1\sim n(0,1)$, $Y_n\mid W_n=0\sim n(0,\sigma_n^2)$ with $P(W_n=1)=p_n$.
(a) $EY_n = E[E[Y_n\mid W_n]] = 0$. By total variance, $\mathrm{Var}(Y_n) = E[\mathrm{Var}(Y_n\mid W_n)] + \mathrm{Var}(E[Y_n\mid W_n]) = p_n\cdot 1 + (1-p_n)\sigma_n^2 + 0$.
(b) $P(Y_n<a) = p_nP(Z<a) + (1-p_n)P(Z<a/\sigma_n)$. Since $a/\sigma_n\to 0$, $P(Z<a/\sigma_n)\to 1/2$, and $(1-p_n)\to 0$, the second term vanishes while $p_nP(Z<a)\to P(Z<a)$. Hence $Y_n\xrightarrow{d} n(0,1)$ even though $\mathrm{Var}(Y_n)=p_n+(1-p_n)\sigma_n^2\to\infty$: the asymptotic variance (1) and the limiting variance ($\infty$) disagree.
