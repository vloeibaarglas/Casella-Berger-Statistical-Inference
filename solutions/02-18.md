**Central idea.** Differentiate $g(a)=E|X-a|$ under the integral: $g'(a)=2F_X(a)-1$, whose zero is the median, and $g$ is convex.

**Solution.** Assume $E|X|<\infty$ and put $g(a)=\int_{-\infty}^a(a-x)f_X(x)dx+\int_a^\infty(x-a)f_X(x)dx$. By Leibniz' rule (the boundary terms vanish by continuity of $X$),
$$g'(a)=\int_{-\infty}^a f_X(x)dx-\int_a^\infty f_X(x)dx=2F_X(a)-1.$$
Hence $g'(a)=0\iff F_X(a)=1/2$, i.e. at the median $m$; $g''(a)=2f_X(a)\ge0$, so this critical point is the global minimum.
