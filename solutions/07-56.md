**Central idea.** Rao–Blackwell gives an unbiased function of $T$; completeness makes it the only one, hence best.
**Solution.** $\phi(T)$ is unbiased by the tower property. If $\psi(T)$ is any other unbiased estimator based on $T$, then $E_\theta[\phi(T)-\psi(T)]=0$ for all $\theta$, so completeness of $T$ forces $\phi(T)=\psi(T)$ a.s. Thus $\phi$ is the unique unbiased estimator that is a function of $T$; for any unbiased $h'$, Rao–Blackwell gives $\mathrm{Var}_\theta(h')\ge\mathrm{Var}_\theta(E[h'\mid T])=\mathrm{Var}_\theta(\phi)$, so $\phi$ is best unbiased (the Lehmann–Scheffé theorem).
