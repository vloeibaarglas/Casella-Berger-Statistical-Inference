**Central idea.** Reduce by sufficiency to $\bar X$, then complete the square separately on $\theta>0$ and $\theta<0$ — the posterior mean is a $\Phi/\phi$-weighted average of two shifted normals.
**Solution.** By sufficiency, $\pi(\theta\mid\mathbf{x})\propto\exp\{-\frac{n}{2\sigma^2}(\theta-\bar x)^2-\frac{|\theta|}{a}\}$. Hence, with $\sigma_n^2=\sigma^2/n$,
$$E[\theta\mid\mathbf{x}]=\frac{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\theta\exp\{-\frac{(\theta-\bar x)^2}{2\sigma_n^2}-\frac{|\theta|}{a}\}\,d\theta}{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\exp\{-\frac{(\theta-\bar x)^2}{2\sigma_n^2}-\frac{|\theta|}{a}\}\,d\theta}.$$
Completing the square on each half-line with $\mu_{\pm}=\bar x\mp\sigma_n^2/a$ gives, with $\phi,\Phi$ the standard normal pdf/cdf,
$$E[\theta\mid\mathbf{x}]=\frac{e^{-( \bar x^2-\mu_+^2)/(2\sigma_n^2)}[\mu_+\Phi(\mu_+/\sigma_n)+\sigma_n\phi(\mu_+/\sigma_n)]+e^{-( \bar x^2-\mu_-^2)/(2\sigma_n^2)}[\mu_-\Phi(-\mu_-/\sigma_n)-\sigma_n\phi(\mu_-/\sigma_n)]}{e^{-( \bar x^2-\mu_+^2)/(2\sigma_n^2)}\Phi(\mu_+/\sigma_n)+e^{-( \bar x^2-\mu_-^2)/(2\sigma_n^2)}\Phi(-\mu_-/\sigma_n)}.$$
