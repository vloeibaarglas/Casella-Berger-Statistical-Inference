**Central idea.** Center the (11.2.7) pivot at $\delta$: $(\sum a_i\bar{Y}_{i\cdot} - \sum a_i\theta_i)/(S_p\sqrt{\sum a_i^2/n_i}) \sim t_{N-k}$ is a pivot, independent of the unknown $\sum a_i\theta_i$.

**Solution.** By 11.8, $\sum a_i\bar{Y}_{i\cdot} \sim n(\sum a_i\theta_i, \sigma^2\sum a_i^2/n_i)$, and by 11.6(b) it is independent of $S_p^2$ with $(N-k)S_p^2/\sigma^2 \sim \chi^2_{N-k}$. Hence
$$T = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^k a_i\bar{Y}_{i\cdot} - \sum_{i=1}^k a_i\theta_i}{S_p\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^k a_i^2/n_i}} \sim t_{N-k}.$$
(a) Reject $H_0$ if $|T_0| > t_{N-k,\alpha/2}$ where $T_0 = (\sum a_i\bar{Y}_{i\cdot} - \delta)/(S_p\sqrt{\sum a_i^2/n_i})$.
(b) Reject $H_0$ if $T_0 > t_{N-k,\alpha}$.
