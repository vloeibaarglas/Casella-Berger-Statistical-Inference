**Central idea.** In the (3.4.7) parametrization $w_i(\eta)=\eta_i$, so $\partial w_i/\partial\eta_j=\delta_{ij}$ kills every term except the $j$th; for gamma read $c^*(\eta)$ off the pdf.

**Solution.** (a) With $w_i(\eta)=\eta_i$: $\frac{\partial w_i}{\partial\eta_j}=\delta_{ij}$ and $\frac{\partial^2w_i}{\partial\eta_j^2}=0$. Then (3.4.4) becomes $E_\eta t_j(X)=-\frac{\partial}{\partial\eta_j}\log c^*(\eta)$ and (3.4.5) becomes $\text{Var}_\eta(t_j(X))=-\frac{\partial^2}{\partial\eta_j^2}\log c^*(\eta)$. (b) $f(x)=\frac{1}{x}\cdot\frac{1}{\Gamma(\eta_1)(-\eta_2)^{\eta_1}}\exp(\eta_1\log x+\eta_2x)$ with $\eta_1=a$, $\eta_2=-1/b$, $t_2(x)=x$. $\log c^*(\eta)=-\log\Gamma(\eta_1)+\eta_1\log(-\eta_2)$: $-\frac{\partial}{\partial\eta_2}\log c^*=-\frac{\eta_1}{\eta_2}=ab$, so $EX=ab$; $-\frac{\partial^2}{\partial\eta_j^2}\log c^*=\frac{\eta_1}{\eta_2^2}=ab^2$, so Var$X=ab^2$.
