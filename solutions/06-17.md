**Central idea.** The joint pmf is $\theta^n(1-\theta)^{\sum x_i - n}$ — an exponential family in $\sum X_i$, whose distribution is negative binomial with an open natural parameter space.

**Solution.** $\prod_{i=1}^n \theta(1-\theta)^{x_i-1} = \theta^n(1-\theta)^{\sum x_i - n} = g(\sum x_i\mid\theta)\cdot 1$, so $\sum X_i$ is sufficient by factorization. $S = \sum X_i$ is the trial of the $n$th success: $P_\theta(S = s) = \binom{s-1}{n-1}\theta^n(1-\theta)^{s-n}$, $s = n,n+1,\ldots$ (negative binomial). The family is complete: if $\mathrm{E}_\theta[g(S)] = \theta^n\sum_{k\ge 0} g(k+n)\binom{k+n-1}{n-1}(1-\theta)^k = 0$ for all $\theta \in (0,1)$, the power series in $(1-\theta)$ is identically zero, so $g \equiv 0$. (Equivalently, it is a full exponential family with natural parameter $\log(1-\theta) \in (-\infty,0)$, open, so Theorem 6.2.25 applies.)
