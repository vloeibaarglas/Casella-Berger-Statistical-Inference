**Central idea.** Bonferroni alone gives only $\ge$; the ordering $L \le U$ makes the two failure events disjoint, upgrading the bound to equality.

**Solution.** $P_\theta(L \le \theta \le U) = 1 - P_\theta(\{L(X) > \theta\} \cup \{U(X) < \theta\})$. Now $P_\theta(L(X) > \theta) = \alpha_1$ and $P_\theta(U(X) < \theta) = \alpha_2$. Since $L(x) \le U(x)$ for every $x$, $\{L(X) > \theta\} \cap \{U(X) < \theta\} = \varnothing$ (it would require $U(X) < \theta < L(X) \le U(X)$). Hence the union has probability exactly $\alpha_1 + \alpha_2$, and the coverage is $1 - \alpha_1 - \alpha_2$.
