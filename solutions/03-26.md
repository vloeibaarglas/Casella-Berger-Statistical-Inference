**Central idea.** $h_T = f_T/(1-F_T)$; for each family compute $1-F_T$ and simplify.

**Solution.** (a) $f=\frac1\beta e^{-t/\beta}$, $1-F=e^{-t/\beta}$: $h_T=1/\beta$ (memoryless $\iff$ constant hazard). (b) $f=\frac\gamma\beta t^{\gamma-1}e^{-t^\gamma/\beta}$, $1-F=e^{-t^\gamma/\beta}$: $h_T=(\gamma/\beta)t^{\gamma-1}$. (c) $f_T=F_T'=\frac{e^{-(t-\mu)/\beta}}{\beta(1+e^{-(t-\mu)/\beta})^2}$, $1-F_T=\frac{e^{-(t-\mu)/\beta}}{1+e^{-(t-\mu)/\beta}}$: $h_T=\frac{f_T}{1-F_T}=\frac{1}{\beta(1+e^{-(t-\mu)/\beta})}=\frac1\beta F_T(t)$.
