**Central idea.** $\mathrm{SST} = \mathrm{SSB} + \mathrm{SSW}$ via the cross term vanishing; the diet means differ enormously ($F \approx 110$); the $F$ statistic is the average of the six ordered pairwise $t^2$ values.

**Solution.** (a) Cell means: Low 3.508, Medium 9.274, High 24.926; grand mean 12.569. $\mathrm{SSB} = 1228.27$, $\mathrm{SSW} = 66.74$, $\mathrm{SST} = 1295.01$.

| Source | df | SS | MS | $F$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Diet (between) | 2 | 1228.27 | 614.14 | 110.43 |
| Error (within) | 12 | 66.74 | 5.561 | |
| Total | 14 | 1295.01 | | |

The $F$ of 110.43 is overwhelming evidence that protein level affects weight gain.
(b) Write $y_{ij} - \bar{\bar{y}} = (y_{ij} - \bar{y}_{i\cdot}) + (\bar{y}_{i\cdot} - \bar{\bar{y}})$. Squaring and summing, the cross term is $2\sum_i(\bar{y}_{i\cdot}-\bar{\bar{y}})\sum_j(y_{ij}-\bar{y}_{i\cdot}) = 0$ since $\sum_j(y_{ij}-\bar{y}_{i\cdot}) = 0$ for each $i$. Hence $\sum_{i,j}(y_{ij}-\bar{\bar{y}})^2 = \sum_i n_i(\bar{y}_{i\cdot}-\bar{\bar{y}})^2 + \sum_{i,j}(y_{ij}-\bar{y}_{i\cdot})^2$.
(c) With $n = 5$, $S_p^2 = 5.561$: $t^2_{12} = 14.95$ (Low–Med), $t^2_{13} = 206.21$ (Low–High), $t^2_{23} = 110.13$ (Med–High). Summing over all $3\times3$ ordered pairs (diagonal terms 0, each pair counted twice): $\frac{1}{3\cdot2}\cdot 2(14.95+206.21+110.13) = 110.43 = F$. ✓
