**Central idea.** $\{X \le r-1\}$ = "fewer than $r$ successes in $n$ trials" = "the $r$th success comes after trial $n$" = $\{Y > n-r\}$, where $Y$ counts failures before the $r$th success.

**Solution.** $F_X(r-1) = P(X \le r-1)$, where $X$ is the number of successes in $n$ trials. $X \le r-1$ iff the $r$th success occurs after the $n$th trial, iff the number $Y$ of failures before the $r$th success exceeds $n-r$. Hence $P(X \le r-1) = P(Y > n-r) = 1-F_Y(n-r)$.
