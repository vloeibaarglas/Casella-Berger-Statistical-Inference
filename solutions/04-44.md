**Central idea.** Expand the squared sum of centered variables; diagonal terms give variances, off-diagonal terms pair up into covariances.

**Solution.** With $\mu_i = \mathrm{E}X_i$,
$$\mathrm{Var}\left(\sum_i X_i\right) = \mathrm{E}\left[\left(\sum_i (X_i-\mu_i)\right)^2\right] = \sum_i\mathrm{E}[(X_i-\mu_i)^2]+2\sum_{i<j}\mathrm{E}[(X_i-\mu_i)(X_j-\mu_j)],$$
which is $\sum_i\mathrm{Var}\,X_i+2\sum_{i<j}\mathrm{Cov}(X_i,X_j)$.
