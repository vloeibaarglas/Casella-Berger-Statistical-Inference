**Central idea.** An $r$th partial is a multiset of $r$ choices from $n$ variables — stars and bars.

**Solution.** (a) A fourth partial is determined by how many times each of the 3 variables is differentiated, $k_1+k_2+k_3 = 4$: $\binom{3+4-1}{4} = \binom{6}{4} = 15$. (b) An $r$th partial corresponds to $(k_1,\dots,k_n)$ with $k_i \geq 0$ and $\sum k_i = r$ (order irrelevant by continuity); the number of such multisets is $\binom{n+r-1}{r}$.
