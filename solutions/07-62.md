**Central idea.** $\delta^\pi=(1-\eta)\bar X+\eta\mu$ is of the form $a\bar X+b$; bias–variance plus $E_\pi[(\theta-\mu)^2]=\tau^2$ gives the Bayes risk.
**Solution.** (a) $E_\theta[\delta]-\theta=(a-1)\theta+b$, $\mathrm{Var}_\theta(\delta)=a^2\sigma^2/n$; risk $=$ variance $+$ squared bias.
(b) The posterior mean is $\delta^\pi=(1-\eta)\bar X+\eta\mu$ with $\eta=\frac{\sigma^2}{n\tau^2+\sigma^2}$ (since $\frac{\sigma^2/n}{\tau^2+\sigma^2/n}=\eta$); applying (a) with $a=1-\eta$, $b=\eta\mu$ gives the stated risk.
(c) $B(\pi,\delta^\pi)=E_\pi[R(\theta,\delta^\pi)]=(1-\eta)^2\frac{\sigma^2}{n}+\eta^2\tau^2=\tau^2\big[\frac{n\tau^2\sigma^2}{(n\tau^2+\sigma^2)^2}+\frac{\sigma^4}{(n\tau^2+\sigma^2)^2}\big]=\tau^2\frac{\sigma^2}{n\tau^2+\sigma^2}=\tau^2\eta$.
