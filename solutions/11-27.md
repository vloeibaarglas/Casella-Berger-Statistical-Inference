**Central idea.** Minimize $\sum c_i^2$ subject to the two unbiasedness constraints $\sum c_i = 1$, $\sum c_ix_i = 0$ (Lagrange); the solution is $\hat\alpha = \bar{Y} - \hat\beta\bar x$, the least squares intercept.

**Solution.** Seek $\sum c_iY_i$ with $E\sum c_iY_i = \alpha\sum c_i + \beta\sum c_ix_i \equiv \alpha$, i.e. $\sum c_i = 1$, $\sum c_ix_i = 0$, minimizing $\mathrm{Var} = \sigma^2\sum c_i^2$. Lagrange: $2c_i = \lambda_1 + \lambda_2x_i$, so $c_i = a + bx_i$. The constraints give $na + b\sum x_i = 1$ and $a\sum x_i + b\sum x_i^2 = 0$, i.e. $a + b\bar x = 1/n$ and, substituting, $bS_{xx} = -\bar x$. Hence $b = -\bar x/S_{xx}$, $a = 1/n + \bar x^2/S_{xx}$, and
$$c_i = \frac{1}{n} - \frac{\bar x(x_i-\bar x)}{S_{xx}}.$$
The BLUE is $\sum c_iY_i = \bar{Y} - \bar x\sum_i\frac{(x_i-\bar x)}{S_{xx}}Y_i = \bar{Y} - \hat\beta\bar x = \hat\alpha$, the least squares intercept (the Hessian/constrained-quadratic argument confirms the minimum).
