**Central idea.** MOM gives $2\bar X$ while the MLE is $X_{(n)}$; both are (essentially) unbiased, but the MLE has much smaller variance — it is sufficient, while $2\bar X$ is not.
**Solution.** MOM: $E_\theta X_1=\theta/2$, so $\tilde\theta=2\bar X$, with $E\tilde\theta=\theta$ and $\mathrm{Var}(\tilde\theta)=4\cdot\theta^2/(12n)=\theta^2/(3n)$. MLE: $L(\theta\mid\mathbf{x})=\theta^{-n}\mathbf{1}_{[\theta\ge x_{(n)}]}$, decreasing in $\theta$, so $\hat\theta=X_{(n)}$, with $E\hat\theta=n\theta/(n+1)$ and $\mathrm{Var}(\hat\theta)=n\theta^2/[(n+1)^2(n+2)]$. The MLE is preferred: it is sufficient and $\mathrm{MSE}(\hat\theta)=\mathrm{Var}(\hat\theta)+(E\hat\theta-\theta)^2=2\theta^2/[(n+1)(n+2)]\le\theta^2/(3n)=\mathrm{MSE}(\tilde\theta)$, with equality at $n=1,2$ and strict inequality for $n\ge3$ (and $\hat\theta$ is asymptotically unbiased).
