**Central idea.** A linear combination of independent normals is normal; means and variances add with squared coefficients.

**Solution.** The $\bar{Y}_{i\cdot}$ are independent with $\bar{Y}_{i\cdot} \sim n(\theta_i, \sigma^2/n_i)$. By Corollary 4.6.10, $\sum a_i\bar{Y}_{i\cdot}$ is normal with mean $\sum a_i E\bar{Y}_{i\cdot} = \sum a_i\theta_i$ and variance $\sum a_i^2\mathrm{Var}(\bar{Y}_{i\cdot}) = \sigma^2\sum a_i^2/n_i$.
