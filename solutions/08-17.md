**Central idea.** $-\log X_i \sim$ exponential$(\mu)$, so this is the two-sample exponential problem in disguise (cf. 8.6); $T$ is the beta-distributed fraction of the total "exponential mass" in the $X$ sample.

**Solution.** The beta$(\mu,1)$ pdf is $\mu x^{\mu-1}$ on $(0,1)$. Let $A = -\sum\log X_i > 0$, $B = -\sum\log Y_i > 0$.
(a) $L(\mu,\theta) = \mu^n\theta^m \exp(-(\mu-1)\sum\log X_i - (\theta-1)\sum\log Y_i)$. Unconstrained MLEs: $\hat\mu = n/A$, $\hat\theta = m/B$. Under $H_0$ ($\mu=\theta=\phi$): $\hat\phi = (n+m)/(A+B)$. The $\exp(\sum\log X_i + \sum\log Y_i)$ factors cancel, giving
$$\lambda = \frac{(n+m)^{n+m}}{n^n m^m}\cdot\frac{A^n B^m}{(A+B)^{n+m}}.$$
(b) With $T = \frac{\sum\log X_i}{\sum\log X_i+\sum\log Y_i} = \frac{A}{A+B} \in (0,1)$,
$$\lambda = \frac{(n+m)^{n+m}}{n^n m^m}\,T^n(1-T)^m,$$
and $T^n(1-T)^m$ is maximized at $T = n/(n+m)$, strictly monotone on each side. Hence the LRT rejects for $T \le c_1$ or $T \ge c_2$.
(c) Since $-\log X_i \sim$ exponential$(\mu)$ (mean $1/\mu$) and $-\log Y_i \sim$ exponential$(\theta)$, under $H_0$ ($\mu=\theta$) $A \sim \text{Gamma}(n,\mu)$, $B \sim \text{Gamma}(m,\mu)$ independently, so $T = A/(A+B) \sim \text{beta}(n,m)$, free of $\mu$. A size-$.10$ test: reject if $T < b_{n,m,.05}$ or $T > b_{n,m,.95}$, the lower/upper $5\%$ points of beta$(n,m)$ (any $c_1 < c_2$ with $P(T<c_1)+P(T>c_2)=.10$ works; equal tails is standard).
