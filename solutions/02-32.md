**Central idea.** Logarithmic differentiation turns the mgf's derivatives into the mean and the variance directly.

**Solution.** Assume $M_X$ exists in a neighborhood of 0. $S'(t)=M_X'(t)/M_X(t)$, so $S'(0)=M_X'(0)/M_X(0)=EX/1=EX$. $S''(t)=\frac{M_X''(t)M_X(t)-(M_X'(t))^2}{M_X(t)^2}$, so $S''(0)=EX^2-(EX)^2=\mathrm{Var}X$.
