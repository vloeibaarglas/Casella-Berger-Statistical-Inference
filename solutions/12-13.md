**Central idea.** The numerator of $r_\lambda(\beta)$ is (up to sign) the score quadratic, so it factors through its roots $\hat\beta$ and $-1/(\lambda\hat\beta)$; the denominator-minus-numerator collapses to $(1+\lambda\beta^2)^2(S_{xx}S_{yy}-S_{xy}^2)$, and the score quadratic evaluated at $\hat\beta$ converts the remaining constants into $\hat\sigma^2_{\hat\beta}$.

**Solution.** (a) Squaring (12.2.22), $(\beta-\hat\beta)^2\le t^2_{n-2,\alpha/2}\hat\sigma^2_{\hat\beta}/(n-2)$, and $t^2_{n-2,\alpha/2}=F_{1,n-2,\alpha}$.

(b) The numerator of $r_\lambda(\beta)$ is $\beta\lambda S_{yy}+(1-\beta^2\lambda)S_{xy}-\beta S_{xx}=-(\lambda S_{xy}\beta^2+(S_{xx}-\lambda S_{yy})\beta-S_{xy})=-\lambda S_{xy}(\beta-\hat\beta)(\beta+1/(\lambda\hat\beta))$ by Exercise 12.12(a). Writing $A=S_{xx}$, $B=S_{xy}$, $C=S_{yy}$, the denominator minus the squared numerator is
$$(\lambda^2\beta^2C+2\lambda\beta B+A)(C-2\beta B+\beta^2A)-(\beta\lambda C+(1-\beta^2\lambda)B-\beta A)^2$$
$$=(AC-B^2)(1+\lambda\beta^2)^2,$$
the $\beta^3$ and $\beta$ terms cancelling identically. Hence the stated identity for $r^2_\lambda/(1-r^2_\lambda)$ holds. Now
$$\frac{(n-2)r^2_\lambda}{1-r^2_\lambda}\le F\iff (n-2)\frac{\lambda^2S_{xy}^2(\beta-\hat\beta)^2(\beta+1/(\lambda\hat\beta))^2}{(1+\lambda\beta^2)^2(S_{xx}S_{yy}-S_{xy}^2)}\le F.$$
Since $\beta+1/(\lambda\hat\beta)=(1+\lambda\hat\beta\beta)/(\lambda\hat\beta)$, this equals $\frac{(\beta-\hat\beta)^2}{\hat\sigma^2_{\hat\beta}/(n-2)}\frac{(1+\lambda\hat\beta\beta)^2}{(1+\lambda\beta^2)^2}\le F$ provided $\hat\sigma^2_{\hat\beta}=\hat\beta^2(S_{xx}S_{yy}-S_{xy}^2)/S_{xy}^2$. Indeed, with $\hat\sigma^2_{\hat\beta}=(1+\lambda\hat\beta^2)^2(S_{xx}S_{yy}-S_{xy}^2)/((S_{xx}-\lambda S_{yy})^2+4\lambda S_{xy}^2)$, the requirement is $(1+\lambda\hat\beta^2)^2S_{xy}^2=\hat\beta^2[(S_{xx}-\lambda S_{yy})^2+4\lambda S_{xy}^2]$, which follows from the score equation: $(S_{xx}-\lambda S_{yy})\hat\beta=S_{xy}(1-\lambda\hat\beta^2)$, so $\hat\beta^2(S_{xx}-\lambda S_{yy})^2+4\lambda S_{xy}^2\hat\beta^2=S_{xy}^2(1-\lambda\hat\beta^2)^2+4\lambda S_{xy}^2\hat\beta^2=S_{xy}^2(1+\lambda\hat\beta^2)^2$. Finally $(1+\lambda\hat\beta\beta)^2/(1+\lambda\beta^2)^2\to_p 1$ since $\hat\beta\to_p\beta$, so $C_G$ is the Creasy–Williams set with that factor replaced by its probability limit.
