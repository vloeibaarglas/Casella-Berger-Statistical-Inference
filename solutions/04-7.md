**Central idea.** $(X,Y)$ is uniform on a $30\times 10$ rectangle; the favorable region $\{X+Y<60\}$ is a trapezoid whose area is a ratio away.

**Solution.** $X \sim$ uniform$(0,30)$ (minutes after 8 AM), $Y \sim$ uniform$(40,50)$, independent, so $(X,Y)$ is uniform on a rectangle of area $300$. She arrives before 9 iff $X+Y < 60$. For $0 \le x \le 10$ all $y \in [40,50]$ qualify (area $100$); for $10 \le x \le 20$, $y < 60-x$ gives area $\int_{10}^{20}(20-x)\,dx = 50$; for $x \ge 20$ none qualify. Favorable area $= 150$, so $P = 150/300 = 1/2$.
