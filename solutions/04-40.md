**Central idea.** Integrate $y$ out with $y=(1-x)t$ to get beta integrals twice (giving $C$ and the beta$(a,b+c)$ marginal); the conditional is read off as a scaled beta; $\mathrm{E}[XY] = \mathrm{E}[X(1-X)]\cdot b/(b+c)$.

**Solution.** The support is $x>0$, $y>0$, $x+y<1$.
(a) $\int_0^{1-x} y^{b-1}(1-x-y)^{c-1}dy = (1-x)^{b+c-1}B(b,c)$ (via $y=(1-x)t$), then $\int_0^1 x^{a-1}(1-x)^{b+c-1}dx = B(a,b+c)$; the product is $\frac{\Gamma(a)\Gamma(b)\Gamma(c)}{\Gamma(a+b+c)}$, so $C$ is its reciprocal.
(b) $f_X(x) = Cx^{a-1}(1-x)^{b+c-1}B(b,c) = \frac{\Gamma(a+b+c)}{\Gamma(a)\Gamma(b+c)}x^{a-1}(1-x)^{b+c-1}$: $X \sim \text{beta}(a,b+c)$; similarly $Y \sim \text{beta}(b,a+c)$.
(c) $f_{Y|X}(y|x) = \frac{y^{b-1}(1-x-y)^{c-1}}{(1-x)^{b+c-1}B(b,c)}$, $0<y<1-x$; with $t = y/(1-x)$ this is the beta$(b,c)$ density. So $Y/(1-x)|X=x \sim \text{beta}(b,c)$.
(d) $\mathrm{E}[XY] = \mathrm{E}[X\cdot\mathrm{E}(Y|X)] = \mathrm{E}[X(1-X)]\frac{b}{b+c}$. With $X \sim \text{beta}(a,b+c)$, $\mathrm{E}[X(1-X)] = \frac{a(b+c)}{(a+b+c)(a+b+c+1)}$, giving $\mathrm{E}[XY] = \frac{ab}{(a+b+c)(a+b+c+1)}$. Hence $\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y) = \frac{ab}{(a+b+c)(a+b+c+1)}-\frac{a}{a+b+c}\frac{b}{a+b+c} = -\frac{ab}{(a+b+c)^2(a+b+c+1)}$.
