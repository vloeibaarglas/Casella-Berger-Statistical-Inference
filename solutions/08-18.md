**Central idea.** Power is the two-tail normal probability $P(|Z+\delta| > c)$ with noncentrality $\delta = \sqrt n(\theta-\theta_0)/\sigma$; fixing $\alpha = .05$ gives $c = 1.96$, and $\beta = .25$ at one-$\sigma$ displacement solves to $n = 7$.

**Solution.** (a) Under $\theta$, $(\bar X-\theta_0)/(\sigma/\sqrt n) =_d Z + \delta$ with $Z \sim n(0,1)$ and $\delta = \sqrt n(\theta-\theta_0)/\sigma$. Hence
$$\gamma(\theta) = P_\theta(|Z+\delta| > c) = \bigl[1-\Phi(c-\delta)\bigr] + \Phi(-c-\delta), \qquad \delta = \frac{\sqrt n(\theta-\theta_0)}{\sigma}.$$
(b) Type I error $.05$ gives $c = z_{.025} = 1.96$. The Type II error $\beta(\theta) = 1-\gamma(\theta) = \Phi(c-\delta)-\Phi(-c-\delta)$ decreases as $|\theta-\theta_0|$ grows, so requiring $\beta \le .25$ for $|\theta-\theta_0| \ge \sigma$ amounts to $\beta(\theta_0+\sigma) = .25$. With $\delta = \sqrt n$,
$$\Phi(1.96-\sqrt n) - \Phi(-1.96-\sqrt n) = .25.$$
The second term is negligible, so $1.96-\sqrt n \approx \Phi^{-1}(.25) = -0.6745$, i.e. $\sqrt n \approx 2.63$, $n \approx 6.9$. Take $n = 7$ (with $n = 6$, $\beta \approx .31 > .25$; with $n=7$, $\beta \approx .246$). Answer: $c = 1.96$, $n = 7$.
