**Central idea.** *As printed this problem contains errata: with the usual unbiased $s_X^2, s_Y^2$ (as in 8.42), the factors $n_1(n_1-1)$ make the pooled quantity $n_1\chi^2_{n_1-1} + n_2\chi^2_{n_2-1}$ rather than $\chi^2_{n_1+n_2-2}$, so the displayed statistic is not Student $t$; the $F$ statement and the maximizer are likewise incorrect as stated. The corrected versions are proved below.*

**Solution.** Let $s_X^2, s_Y^2$ be the usual unbiased sample variances. Then $\frac{(\bar X-\bar Y)-(\mu_1-\mu_2)}{\sigma\sqrt{1/n_1+\rho^2/n_2}} \sim n(0,1)$, independent of $V := \frac{(n_1-1)s_X^2 + (n_2-1)s_Y^2/\rho^2}{\sigma^2} \sim \chi^2_{n_1+n_2-2}$ (since $(n_1-1)s_X^2/\sigma^2 \sim \chi^2_{n_1-1}$ and $(n_2-1)s_Y^2/(\rho^2\sigma^2) \sim \chi^2_{n_2-1}$, independently). Hence the (corrected) statistic
$$T := \frac{(\bar X - \bar Y) - (\mu_1 - \mu_2)}{\sqrt{\frac1{n_1}+\frac{\rho^2}{n_2}}\,\sqrt{\frac{(n_1-1)s_X^2+(n_2-1)s_Y^2/\rho^2}{\,n_1+n_2-2\,}}} \sim t_{n_1+n_2-2}.$$
(As printed, with $n_1(n_1-1)s_X^2$, the bracket over $\sigma^2$ equals $n_1\chi^2_{n_1-1} + n_2\chi^2_{n_2-1}$, not $\chi^2_{n_1+n_2-2}$, so the ratio is not Student $t$.) Moreover $\frac{s_Y^2/\rho^2}{s_X^2} = \frac{\chi^2_{n_2-1}/(n_2-1)}{\chi^2_{n_1-1}/(n_1-1)} \sim F_{n_2-1,n_1-1}$; as printed, $\frac{n_2s_Y^2}{\rho^2n_1s_X^2} = \frac{n_2}{n_1}F_{n_2-1,n_1-1}$, not $F_{n_1-1,n_2-1}$. Finally, for the corrected $T$, $|T|$ is maximized over $\rho^2 > 0$ by minimizing $\big(\frac1{n_1}+\frac{\rho^2}{n_2}\big)\big((n_1-1)s_X^2+(n_2-1)s_Y^2/\rho^2\big)$; differentiating gives $(\rho^2)^2 = \frac{n_2(n_2-1)s_Y^2}{n_1(n_1-1)s_X^2}$, i.e. $\rho^2 = \sqrt{\frac{n_2(n_2-1)}{n_1(n_1-1)}}\,\frac{s_Y}{s_X}$ (For the originally printed denominator with the extra $n_i$ factors, the optimizing variance ratio is instead $\frac{n_2\sqrt{n_2-1}s_Y}{n_1\sqrt{n_1-1}s_X}$. The printed proposal both reverses the dependence and uses variances where standard deviations are needed. Maximizing $T$ itself requires attention to the sign of its numerator; the denominator minimization maximizes $|T|$.)
