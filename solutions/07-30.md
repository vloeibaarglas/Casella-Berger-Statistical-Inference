**Central idea.** The component labels are the missing data: the E-step computes posterior membership probabilities (Bayes' rule), and the M-step averages them.
**Solution.** (a) By the hierarchical specification, $f(x_i,z_i\mid p)=[pf(x_i)]^{z_i}[(1-p)g(x_i)]^{1-z_i}$ for $z_i\in\{0,1\}$; independence across $i$ gives the product.
(b) $P(Z_i=1\mid x_i,p)=\frac{pf(x_i)}{pf(x_i)+(1-p)g(x_i)}$ by Bayes' rule (the denominator is the mixture marginal), so $Z_i\mid x_i,p\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}$ with that success probability.
(c) The complete-data log-likelihood is $\sum_i[z_i\log(pf(x_i))+(1-z_i)\log((1-p)g(x_i))]$. Its expectation under $Z_i\mid x_i,\hat p^{(r)}$ replaces $z_i$ by $\hat z_i^{(r)}=\hat p^{(r)}f(x_i)/[\hat p^{(r)}f(x_i)+(1-\hat p^{(r)})g(x_i)]$. Maximizing $\sum_i[\hat z_i^{(r)}\log p+(1-\hat z_i^{(r)})\log(1-p)]$ in $p$ gives $\hat p^{(r+1)}=\frac1n\sum_i\hat z_i^{(r)}$, as claimed.
