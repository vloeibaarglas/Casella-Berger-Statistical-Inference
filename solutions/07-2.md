**Central idea.** Profile the likelihood: substitute the closed-form $\hat\beta(\alpha)$ into $\log L$ and maximize the resulting univariate function of $\alpha$ (whose first-order condition is $\log\alpha-\psi(\alpha)=\log(\bar x/\tilde x)$).
**Solution.** The gamma pdf is $f(x\mid\alpha,\beta)=\Gamma(\alpha)^{-1}\beta^{-\alpha}x^{\alpha-1}e^{-x/\beta}$, $x>0$.
(a) $\log L=-n\log\Gamma(\alpha)-n\alpha\log\beta+(\alpha-1)\sum\log x_i-\sum x_i/\beta$. Then $\partial\log L/\partial\beta=-n\alpha/\beta+\sum x_i/\beta^2=0$ gives $\hat\beta=\bar x/\alpha$ (a maximum since $\partial^2\log L/\partial\beta^2<0$ at $\hat\beta$).
(b) Substituting $\hat\beta(\alpha)=\bar x/\alpha$ gives the profile log-likelihood
$$l_p(\alpha)=-n\log\Gamma(\alpha)-n\alpha\log(\bar x/\alpha)+(\alpha-1)\sum\log x_i-n\alpha,$$
maximized numerically. Equivalently $\hat\alpha$ solves $\log\alpha-\psi(\alpha)=\log(\bar x/\tilde x)$, where $\tilde x$ is the geometric mean and $\psi=\Gamma'/\Gamma$. For the 14 egg-length observations of Exercise 7.10(c) ($\bar x=23.114$, $\sum\log x_i=43.953$), this gives $\hat\alpha\approx 514.3$ and $\hat\beta=\bar x/\hat\alpha\approx 0.0449$.
