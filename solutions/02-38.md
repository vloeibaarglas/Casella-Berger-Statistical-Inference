**Central idea.** Scale the negative binomial so its mean stays fixed as $p\to0$; the mgf limit is read off termwise.

**Solution.** (a) From Exercise 2.30(d), $M_X(t)=\left(\frac{p}{1-(1-p)e^t}\right)^r$ for $t<-\log(1-p)$.
(b) $M_Y(t)=M_X(2pt)=\left(\frac{p}{1-(1-p)e^{2pt}}\right)^r=\left(\frac{1}{\frac{1-e^{2pt}}{p}+e^{2pt}}\right)^r$. As $p\downarrow0$, $\frac{1-e^{2pt}}{p}\to-2t$ and $e^{2pt}\to1$, so the bracket tends to $\frac{1}{1-2t}$ for $|t|<1/2$ (which also keeps $M_X(2pt)$ defined for small $p$). Hence $\lim_{p\to0}M_Y(t)=\left(\frac{1}{1-2t}\right)^r$, the mgf of $\chi^2_{2r}$.
