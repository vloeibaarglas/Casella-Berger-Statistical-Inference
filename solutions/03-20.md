**Central idea.** Folded normal = $|Z|$ for $Z\sim n(0,1)$; squaring it gives $Z^2\sim\chi^2_1 = \text{gamma}(1/2,2)$.

**Solution.** (a) $X\stackrel{d}{=}|Z|$: $EX = E|Z| = 2\int_0^\infty\frac{z}{\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-z^2/2}dz = \sqrt{2/\pi}$. $E[X^2]=E[Z^2]=1$, so Var$X = 1-2/\pi$. (b) Take $g(x)=x^2$: $Y=X^2\stackrel{d}{=}Z^2\sim\chi^2_1$, whose pdf $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}y^{-1/2}e^{-y/2} = \frac{1}{\Gamma(1/2)2^{1/2}}y^{-1/2}e^{-y/2}$ is gamma$(1/2,2)$. So $\alpha=1/2$, $\beta=2$.
