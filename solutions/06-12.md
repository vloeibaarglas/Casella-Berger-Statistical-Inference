**Central idea.** Condition on $N$: the joint pmf factors into a $\theta$-free part times $\theta^x(1-\theta)^{n-x}$, and the likelihood ratio pins down both coordinates.

**Solution.** (a) $P_\theta(X = x, N = n) = p_n\binom{n}{x}\theta^x(1-\theta)^{n-x}$. For $(x,n),(y,m)$,
$$\frac{P_\theta(x,n)}{P_\theta(y,m)} = \frac{p_n\binom{n}{x}}{p_m\binom{m}{y}}\;\theta^{\,x-y}(1-\theta)^{\,(n-x)-(m-y)},$$
constant in $\theta \in (0,1)$ iff $x = y$ and $n-x = m-y$, i.e. $(x,n) = (y,m)$. By Theorem 6.2.13, $(X,N)$ is minimal sufficient. $N$ has distribution $P(N = n) = p_n$, free of $\theta$: ancillary.
(b) $\mathrm{E}_\theta[X/N] = \mathrm{E}[\mathrm{E}[X/N\mid N]] = \mathrm{E}[(N\theta)/N] = \theta$. By the conditional variance formula, $\mathrm{Var}(X/N) = \mathrm{E}[\mathrm{Var}(X/N\mid N)] + \mathrm{Var}(\mathrm{E}[X/N\mid N]) = \mathrm{E}[(1/N^2)N\theta(1-\theta)] + \mathrm{Var}(\theta) = \theta(1-\theta)\mathrm{E}(1/N)$.
