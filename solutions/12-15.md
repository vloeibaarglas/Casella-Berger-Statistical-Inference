**Central idea.** Everything follows from $\pi(x)=e^{\alpha+\beta x}/(1+e^{\alpha+\beta x})$ and the chain rule; the logistic cdf satisfies $F'=F(1-F)$, which collapses the GLM score equations.

**Solution.** (a) $\pi(-\alpha/\beta)=e^0/(1+e^0)=1/2$. (b) $\pi(-\alpha/\beta+c)=e^{\beta c}/(1+e^{\beta c})$ and $\pi(-\alpha/\beta-c)=e^{-\beta c}/(1+e^{-\beta c})=1/(1+e^{\beta c})$; they sum to 1. (c) $d\pi/dx=\beta e^{\alpha+\beta x}/(1+e^{\alpha+\beta x})^2=\beta\pi(x)(1-\pi(x))$. (d) From (12.3.1), $\log\frac{\pi(x+1)}{1-\pi(x+1)}-\log\frac{\pi(x)}{1-\pi(x)}=\beta$; exponentiating gives (12.3.4). (e) Rearranged, this is $\frac{\pi(x+1)}{1-\pi(x+1)}=e^\beta\frac{\pi(x)}{1-\pi(x)}$. (f) With $F_i=F(\alpha+\beta x_i)$, $f_i=F'(\alpha+\beta x_i)$: $\frac{\partial}{\partial\alpha}\log(1-F_i)=-f_i/(1-F_i)=-F_if_i/(F_i(1-F_i))$, and $\frac{\partial}{\partial\alpha}\log\frac{F_i}{1-F_i}=\frac{f_i}{F_i}+\frac{f_i}{1-F_i}=\frac{f_i}{F_i(1-F_i)}$, which is (12.3.6); then $\frac{\partial\log L}{\partial\alpha}=\sum_i(y_i-F_i)\frac{f_i}{F_i(1-F_i)}$, i.e. (12.3.7). Since $\partial F_i/\partial\beta=x_i f_i$, the same calculation with an extra $x_i$ gives (12.3.8). (g) For $F(w)=e^w/(1+e^w)$, $f(w)=e^w/(1+e^w)^2=F(w)(1-F(w))$, so $f_i/(F_i(1-F_i))=1$ and the score equations simplify to $\sum_i(y_i-F_i)=0$, $\sum_i x_i(y_i-F_i)=0$.
