**Central idea.** Inversion swaps the roles: $P_\theta(\theta' \in C(X)) = P_\theta(X \in A(\theta'))$, so unbiasedness of the test ($\le 1-\alpha$ acceptance of false values) is exactly unbiasedness of the set ($\le 1-\alpha$ false coverage).

**Solution.** $P_\theta(\theta \in C(X)) = P_\theta(X \in A(\theta)) \ge 1-\alpha$: a $1-\alpha$ set. For $\theta' \ne \theta$: $P_\theta(\theta' \in C(X)) = P_\theta(X \in A(\theta')) \le 1-\alpha$ since $A(\theta')$ is unbiased level $\alpha$. Hence $C$ is unbiased.
