**Central idea.** Direct moment integrals/sums; (c) is symmetric about 1, so center there.

**Solution.** (a) $EX=\int_0^1ax^a\,dx=\frac{a}{a+1}$; $EX^2=\frac{a}{a+2}$; $\mathrm{Var}X=\frac{a}{a+2}-\frac{a^2}{(a+1)^2}=\frac{a}{(a+1)^2(a+2)}$.
(b) $EX=\frac1n\sum_{k=1}^nk=\frac{n+1}{2}$; $EX^2=\frac1n\sum_{k=1}^nk^2=\frac{(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}$; $\mathrm{Var}X=\frac{(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}-\frac{(n+1)^2}{4}=\frac{n^2-1}{12}$.
(c) With $u=x-1$, $f$ is symmetric about 1, so $EX=1$. $EX^2=\frac32\int_{-1}^1(u+1)^2u^2du=\frac32\big(\frac25+\frac23\big)=\frac85$; $\mathrm{Var}X=\frac85-1=\frac35$.
