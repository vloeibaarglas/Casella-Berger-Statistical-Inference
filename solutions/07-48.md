**Central idea.** The MLE is $\bar X$ (CRLB from 7.40); Rao–Blackwellizing $X_1X_2X_3X_4$ on the complete sufficient sum gives a hypergeometric probability.
**Solution.** (a) The MLE is $\bar X$, and $\mathrm{Var}_p(\bar X)=p(1-p)/n=1/[nI(p)]$ is the CRLB (Exercise 7.40).
(b) $E_p[X_1X_2X_3X_4]=p^4$. $S=\sum_{i=1}^nX_i$ is complete sufficient; by Lehmann–Scheffé the UMVUE is $E[X_1X_2X_3X_4\mid S]$. Given $S=s$, $(X_1,\dots,X_n)$ is uniform over binary $n$-tuples with $s$ ones, so $E[X_1X_2X_3X_4\mid S=s]=P(X_1=\cdots=X_4=1\mid S=s)=\binom{s}{4}/\binom{n}{4}$. Hence the best unbiased estimator of $p^4$ is $\dfrac{S(S-1)(S-2)(S-3)}{n(n-1)(n-2)(n-3)}$.
