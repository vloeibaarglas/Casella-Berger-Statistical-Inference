**Central idea.** A wins iff the first head lands on an odd toss — a geometric series over odd terms; the first-move advantage is strict for every $p$.

**Solution.** Let $E_i$ = {first head on toss $i$}, $P(E_i) = p(1-p)^{i-1}$. A wins on odd tosses:
$$P(\text{A wins}) = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} p(1-p)^{2k} = \frac{p}{1-(1-p)^2} = \frac{1}{2-p}.$$
(a) $p = 1/2$: $\frac{1/2}{1-1/4} = 2/3$. (b) As above. (c) $\frac{1}{2-p} > \frac{1}{2} \iff 2 > 2-p \iff p > 0$.
