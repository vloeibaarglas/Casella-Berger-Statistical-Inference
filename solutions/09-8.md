**Central idea.** Standardize by location and scale in different ways; any statistic computed from the standardized residuals $(X_i-\theta)/\sigma$ is free of $(\theta,\sigma)$.

**Solution.** With $\bar X$, $S$ the sample mean and sd: $\frac{\bar X-\theta}{\sigma/\sqrt{n}}$, $\frac{\bar X-\theta}{S/\sqrt{n}}$, $\frac{(n-1)S^2}{\sigma^2}$, $\frac{X_{(1)}-\theta}{\sigma}$, $\frac{X_{(n)}-\bar X}{S}$ (others: $\max_i\frac{|X_i-\bar X|}{S}$, $\sum_i(\frac{X_i-\bar X}{S})^3$, $\frac{X_{(n)}-X_{(1)}}{\sigma}$, …). Each is a function of the iid standardized variables $(X_i-\theta)/\sigma \sim f$, hence pivotal.
