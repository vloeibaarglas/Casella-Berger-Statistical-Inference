**Central idea.** The leave-one-out means combine to $\sum_{i\ne j}X_iX_j/[n(n-1)]$ — a $U$-statistic that is exactly unbiased here, and by completeness (Lehmann–Scheffé) the UMVUE.
**Solution.** (a) $E_\theta\bar X^2=\theta^2+\theta(1-\theta)/n\ne\theta^2$: biased (upward).
(b) With $T_n=\bar X^2$, $T_n^{(i)}=\big(\frac{n\bar X-X_i}{n-1}\big)^2$. Since $\sum_i(n\bar X-X_i)^2=\sum_iX_i^2+n^2(n-2)\bar X^2$,
$$\mathrm{JK}(T_n)=n\bar X^2-\frac{\sum_iX_i^2+n^2(n-2)\bar X^2}{n(n-1)}=\frac{\sum_{i\ne j}X_iX_j}{n(n-1)}=\frac{Y(Y-1)}{n(n-1)},\quad Y=\sum X_i$$
(using $X_i^2=X_i$).
(c) $E_\theta[Y(Y-1)]=n(n-1)\theta^2$, so $E_\theta[\mathrm{JK}(T_n)]=\theta^2$: unbiased — the jackknife removed the bias entirely.
(d) Yes: $\mathrm{JK}(T_n)$ is a function of the complete sufficient statistic $Y$ and is unbiased, hence by Lehmann–Scheffé it is the best (UMVUE) unbiased estimator of $\theta^2$.
