**Central idea.** Multiplication principle for a fixed pattern, addition principle across mutually exclusive patterns.

**Solution.** Each name position has 26 choices. (a) Surname $\times$ 2 given names: $26 \cdot 26 \cdot 26 = 26^3$. (b) Either the (a) pattern or surname $\times$ 1 given name: $26^3 + 26^2$. (c) Add the three-given-name pattern: $26^4 + 26^3 + 26^2$.
