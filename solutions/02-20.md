**Central idea.** The number of trials to the first success is geometric with $p=1/2$.

**Solution.** Let $X$ be the number of children up to and including the first daughter; $P(X=k)=(1/2)^k$, $k\ge1$, the geometric law with success probability $1/2$. Hence $EX=\sum_{k\ge1}k(1/2)^k=\frac{1/2}{(1-1/2)^2}=2$.
