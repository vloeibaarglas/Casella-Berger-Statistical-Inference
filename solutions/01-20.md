**Central idea.** Inclusion–exclusion over the 7 "day $i$ gets no calls" events (onto-function counting).

**Solution.** Let $A_i$ = {day $i$ gets no calls}. Then $P(\cap_i A_i^c) = \sum_{k=0}^{7}(-1)^k\binom{7}{k}\big(\frac{7-k}{7}\big)^{12} \approx 0.2285$: choose the $k$ empty days, and all 12 calls land in the remaining $7-k$ days.
