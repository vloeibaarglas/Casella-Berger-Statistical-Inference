**Central idea.** Wald $=(\hat\theta-\theta_0)/\mathrm{se}(\hat\theta)$: for $\mu$ the MLE is $\bar X$ with se $\sigma/\sqrt n$; for $\sigma$ (with $\mu$ known) the MLE is $\hat\sigma=\sqrt{\frac1n\sum(X_i-\mu)^2}$ with se $\hat\sigma/\sqrt{2n}$ from $I(\sigma)=2n/\sigma^2$.

**Solution.** (a) The MLE of $\mu$ is $\hat\mu=\bar X$ with $\mathrm{se}(\hat\mu)=\sigma/\sqrt n$ ($\sigma$ known), so the Wald statistic is $(\bar X-\mu_0)/(\sigma/\sqrt n)=\sqrt n(\bar X-\mu_0)/\sigma$.
(b) With $\mu$ known, the MLE of $\sigma$ is $\hat\sigma=\sqrt{\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\mu)^2}$. From $l(\sigma)=-n\log\sigma-\sum(X_i-\mu)^2/(2\sigma^2)$, $I(\sigma)=-E[l'']/n=2n/\sigma^2$, so $\mathrm{se}(\hat\sigma)=1/\sqrt{I(\hat\sigma)}=\hat\sigma/\sqrt{2n}$. The Wald statistic is
$$W = \frac{\hat\sigma-\sigma_0}{\hat\sigma/\sqrt{2n}} = \sqrt{2n}\,\frac{\hat\sigma-\sigma_0}{\hat\sigma}\;\xrightarrow{d}\;n(0,1)\ \text{under }H_0.$$
