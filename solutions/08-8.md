**Central idea.** In both families the nuisance mean $\theta$ profiles out in closed form ($\hat\theta = \bar x$ under $H_1$), leaving an LRT that is a function of the sufficient statistics $(\bar x, \sum(X_i-\bar x)^2)$ with only the null-constrained $\hat\theta_0$ differing between the two parametrizations.

**Solution.** (a) $L(a,\theta) = (2\pi a\theta)^{-n/2}\exp(-\sum(x_i-\theta)^2/(2a\theta))$, $\theta>0$, $a>0$. For fixed $\theta$, $\hat a(\theta) = \sum(x_i-\theta)^2/(n\theta)$, and the profile is $-\frac n2\log(\sum(x_i-\theta)^2/n) - \frac n2$ (up to constants), minimized at $\hat\theta = \bar x$. So $\sup_{H_1} L = (2\pi)^{-n/2}[\sum(x_i-\bar x)^2/n]^{-n/2}e^{-n/2}$. Under $H_0$ ($a=1$): $\ell(\theta) = -\frac n2\log\theta - \sum(x_i-\theta)^2/(2\theta)$; $\ell'(\theta) = 0$ gives $\theta^2+\theta-\frac1n\sum x_i^2 = 0$, i.e. $\hat\theta_0 = \frac12(-1+\sqrt{1+4\sum x_i^2/n})$. The LRT rejects for small
$$\lambda(\mathbf x) = \frac{(2\pi\hat\theta_0)^{-n/2}\exp(-\sum(x_i-\hat\theta_0)^2/(2\hat\theta_0))}{(2\pi)^{-n/2}[\sum(x_i-\bar x)^2/n]^{-n/2}e^{-n/2}},$$
a function of $(\bar x, \sum(x_i-\bar x)^2)$.
(b) Now $L(a,\theta) = (2\pi a\theta^2)^{-n/2}\exp(-\sum(x_i-\theta)^2/(2a\theta^2))$. For fixed $\theta$, $\hat a(\theta) = \sum(x_i-\theta)^2/(n\theta^2)$; profiling again gives $-\frac n2\log(\sum(x_i-\theta)^2/n)-\frac n2$, minimized at $\hat\theta=\bar x$, so $\sup_{H_1}L$ is the same expression as in (a). Under $H_0$ ($a=1$): $\ell(\theta) = -n\log\theta - \sum(x_i-\theta)^2/(2\theta^2)$; $\ell'(\theta)=0$ gives $-n\theta^2 + \sum(x_i-\theta)^2 + \theta\sum(x_i-\theta) = 0$, i.e. $\theta^2+\bar x\,\theta-\frac1n\sum x_i^2 = 0$, so $\hat\theta_0 = \frac12(-\bar x+\sqrt{\bar x^2+4\sum x_i^2/n})$. The LRT rejects for small $\lambda(\mathbf x) = L(1,\hat\theta_0)/\sup_{H_1}L$, again a function of $(\bar x,\sum(x_i-\bar x)^2)$.
