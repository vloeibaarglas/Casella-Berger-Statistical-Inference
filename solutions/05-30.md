**Central idea.** $\bar{X}_1 - \bar{X}_2 \;\dot\sim\; n(0, 2\sigma^2/n)$ by the CLT; invert $P(|Z| < \sqrt{n/50}) = 0.99$.

**Solution.** $E[\bar{X}_1-\bar{X}_2] = 0$, $\mathrm{Var}(\bar{X}_1-\bar{X}_2) = 2\sigma^2/n$, and by the CLT $\frac{\bar{X}_1-\bar{X}_2}{\sigma\sqrt{2/n}} \;\dot\sim\; n(0,1)$. Hence
$$P\left(|\bar{X}_1-\bar{X}_2| < \frac{\sigma}{5}\right) \approx P\left(|Z| < \frac{\sigma/5}{\sigma\sqrt{2/n}}\right) = P\left(|Z| < \sqrt{\frac{n}{50}}\right) \stackrel{!}{=} 0.99.$$
$P(|Z| < z) = 0.99$ gives $z = 2.5758$, so $n = 50(2.5758)^2 \approx 331.7$; take $n = 332$. (Uses CLT for the difference of means and independence of the samples.)
