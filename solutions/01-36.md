**Central idea.** Binomial model; use complements ($1 - P(0) - P(1)$) and the definition of conditional probability.

**Solution.** Let $X \sim \text{Binomial}(10, 1/5)$. $P(X = 0) = (4/5)^{10}$, $P(X = 1) = 10(1/5)(4/5)^9$. Then
$$P(X \geq 2) = 1 - (4/5)^{10} - 10(1/5)(4/5)^9 \approx 0.624,$$
and since $\{X \geq 2\} \subset \{X \geq 1\}$,
$$P(X \geq 2 \mid X \geq 1) = \frac{P(X \geq 2)}{P(X \geq 1)} = \frac{P(X \geq 2)}{1-(4/5)^{10}} \approx \frac{0.624}{0.893} \approx 0.699.$$
