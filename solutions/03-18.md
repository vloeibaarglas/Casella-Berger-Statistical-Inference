**Central idea.** $M_{pY}(t) = M_Y(pt)$; as $p\to0$, $e^{pt} = 1+pt+o(p)$ collapses the NB mgf to $(1-t)^{-r}$.

**Solution.** $M_{pY}(t) = E[e^{tpY}] = M_Y(pt) = \left(\frac{p}{1-(1-p)e^{pt}}\right)^r$. Now $1-(1-p)e^{pt} = 1-(1-p)(1+pt+o(p)) = p(1-t)+o(p)$, so $\frac{p}{p(1-t)+o(p)}\to\frac{1}{1-t}$ (for $t<1$), and $M_{pY}(t)\to(1-t)^{-r}$, the mgf of gamma$(r,1)$.
