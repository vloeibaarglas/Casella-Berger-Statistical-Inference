**Central idea.** For a uniform distribution, probabilities are area ratios; use symmetry (the square is centrally symmetric) and note that the complement of (c) is just two corner points.

**Solution.** (a) The region $\{X^2+Y^2<1\}$ is the unit disk, area $\pi$, inside the square of area $4$: $P = \pi/4$. (b) The line $Y=2X$ passes through the center of the square, so it bisects it: $P = 1/2$. (c) On $[-1,1]^2$, $|X+Y| \le 2$ always, with equality only at $(1,1)$ and $(-1,-1)$ — a set of area zero. Hence $P(|X+Y|<2) = 1$.
