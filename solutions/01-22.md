**Central idea.** Hypergeometric counting: (a) 15 days from each month; (b) all 30 from the 336 non-September days.

**Solution.** (a) "Evenly distributed" means 15 days from each of the 12 months. If month $m$ has $d_m$ days, favorable $= \prod_{m=1}^{12}\binom{d_m}{15}$; total $= \binom{366}{180}$. The ratio is $\approx 0.167 \times 10^{-8}$. (b) September has 30 days, leaving 336; $P = \binom{336}{30}/\binom{366}{30}$.
