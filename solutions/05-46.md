**Central idea.** $P(\text{Bin}(c,p) \geq 0.75c) \approx 1 - \Phi\left(\frac{0.75c - cp}{\sqrt{cp(1-p)}}\right)$; with $0.75c$ dozens of standard deviations above the mean, this is $1 - \Phi(\text{huge}) = 0$, agreeing with 5.45.

**Solution.** With $p$ from 5.45: (a) $1-\Phi((225-300p)/\sqrt{300p(1-p)}) \approx 1-\Phi(4.9\times10^4) = 0$; (b) $1-\Phi((75-100p)/\sqrt{100p(1-p)}) \approx 1-\Phi(50.6) = 0$; (c) $1-\Phi((75-100p)/\sqrt{100p(1-p)}) \approx 1-\Phi(42.4) = 0$. The normal approximation agrees with the exact $\approx 0$ in all three cases — here the event is so far in the tail that both methods just say "essentially impossible."
