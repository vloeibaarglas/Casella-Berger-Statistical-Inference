**Central idea.** All three follow from the bivariate normal mgf $M(s,t) = \exp(\mu_Xs+\mu_Yt+\frac12(\sigma_X^2s^2+2\rho\sigma_X\sigma_Yst+\sigma_Y^2t^2))$: set $t=0$ for (a), divide joint by marginal (completing the square) for (b), and evaluate at $(at,bt)$ for (c).

**Solution.** (a) $M_X(s) = M(s,0) = \exp(\mu_Xs+\sigma_X^2s^2/2)$: $X \sim n(\mu_X,\sigma_X^2)$; similarly for $Y$. (b) $f_{Y|X}(y|x) = f(x,y)/f_X(x)$; completing the square in $y$ in the joint exponent gives the $n(\mu_Y+\rho(\sigma_Y/\sigma_X)(x-\mu_X),\sigma_Y^2(1-\rho^2))$ density. (c) $M_{aX+bY}(t) = M(at,bt) = \exp((a\mu_X+b\mu_Y)t+\frac12(a^2\sigma_X^2+2ab\rho\sigma_X\sigma_Y+b^2\sigma_Y^2)t^2)$, the mgf of the stated normal.
