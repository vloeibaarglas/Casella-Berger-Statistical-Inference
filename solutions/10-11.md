**Central idea.** For the gamma mean with known scale, $I_1(\mu)=\psi'(\mu/\beta)/\beta^2$ gives $\mathrm{ARE}(\bar X,\hat\mu)=\alpha\psi'(\alpha)$ with $\alpha=\mu/\beta$; with $\beta$ unknown the MLE of $\mu$ collapses to $\bar X$.

**Solution.** With $f(x\mid\mu,\beta)=\Gamma(\mu/\beta)^{-1}\beta^{-\mu/\beta}x^{\mu/\beta-1}e^{-x/\beta}$, $\partial^2\log f/\partial\mu^2 = -\psi'(\alpha)/\beta^2$ ($\alpha=\mu/\beta$), so $I_1(\mu)=\psi'(\alpha)/\beta^2$. Since $\mathrm{Var}(\bar X)=\beta\mu/n$,
$$\mathrm{ARE}(\bar X,\hat\mu)=\frac{\beta\mu}{1/I_1(\mu)} = \frac{\mu}{\beta}\psi'\!\left(\frac{\mu}{\beta}\right)=\alpha\psi'(\alpha).$$
(a) Plotting $\alpha\psi'(\alpha)$ against $\mu$ for $\beta\in\{1,3,5,10\}$ reproduces Figure 10.1.1 (higher $\beta$ = higher curve; all curves $\downarrow 1$ as $\mu\to\infty$). E.g. for $\beta=5$: $\mu=1,2,\ldots,10$ gives $5.25348, 2.91014, 2.18173, 1.83958, 1.64493, 1.52085, 1.43550, 1.37349, 1.32655, 1.28987$, matching Example 12.6.7's Mathematica output.

(b) *As stated this is incorrect.* With $\beta$ unknown, the score equation $\partial l/\partial\beta = -n\alpha/\beta+\sum x_i/\beta^2=0$ gives $\hat\alpha\hat\beta=\bar X$ at the joint MLE, i.e. $\hat\mu_{\mathrm{MLE}}=\bar X$ exactly — the MOM and ML estimators of $\mu$ coincide, so $\mathrm{ARE}(\bar X,\hat\mu)=1$ trivially, NOT $\alpha\psi'(\alpha)$. The "sameness" claim fails: knowing $\beta$ genuinely helps (the $(\mu,\beta)$ information matrix is not block-diagonal), so the curve in (a) is specific to known $\beta$. (Book erratum.)
(c) *As printed this is incorrect.* With $\mu$ known, the MOM estimator is $\tilde\beta = (m_2-\mu^2)/\mu$ (from $E[X^2]=\mu^2+\mu\beta$), while the MLE solves the digamma equation $\psi(\mu/\hat\beta)+\log\hat\beta = 1+\overline{\log x}-\bar x/\mu$ — a function of $(\bar x,\overline{\log x})$, not of $m_2$. They are different statistics and different estimators: e.g. on $x=(2.1,4.5,6.3,8.8,3.2,7.7,5.5,9.1,1.2,6.6)$ with $\mu=6$ known, $\tilde\beta=0.156$ but $\hat\beta=1.867$. This is a book erratum.
(d) With $\mu$ unknown, $\tilde\beta = (m_2-\bar X^2)/\bar X$ (MOM; replacing its variance numerator by unbiased $S^2$ is only asymptotically equivalent) vs.\ $\hat\beta=\bar X/\hat\alpha$ (MLE). Delta method on $(m_1,m_2)$ gives asymptotic variance of $\tilde\beta$ as $\beta^2(2\alpha+3)/(\alpha n)$; from the Fisher information, $\hat\beta$ has asymptotic variance $\beta^2\psi'(\alpha)/[(\alpha\psi'(\alpha)-1)n]$. Hence (book convention)
$$\mathrm{ARE}(\tilde\beta,\hat\beta)=\frac{(2\alpha+3)(\alpha\psi'(\alpha)-1)}{\alpha\psi'(\alpha)},$$
a function of $\alpha=\mu/\beta$ only, decreasing to 1 as $\alpha\to\infty$ (e.g. $\alpha=1$: $1.96$; $\alpha=5$: $1.25$; $\alpha=10$: $1.13$).
