**Central idea.** Two-stage experiment: law of total probability for (a), then Bayes' theorem to invert the conditioning for (b).

**Solution.** Let $L_i$ = {litter $i$ chosen}, $P(L_1) = P(L_2) = 1/2$; $P(\text{brown} \mid L_1) = 2/3$, $P(\text{brown} \mid L_2) = 3/5$.
(a) $P(\text{brown}) = \tfrac12\cdot\tfrac23 + \tfrac12\cdot\tfrac35 = \tfrac13 + \tfrac{3}{10} = \tfrac{19}{30}$.
(b) $P(L_1 \mid \text{brown}) = \frac{P(\text{brown}\mid L_1)P(L_1)}{P(\text{brown})} = \frac{1/3}{19/30} = \frac{10}{19}$.
