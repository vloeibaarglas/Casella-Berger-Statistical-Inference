**Central idea.** Inverse-variance weighting: minimizing $\sum a_i^2\sigma_i^2$ under $\sum a_i=1$ puts weight proportional to precision.
**Solution.** (a) Unbiasedness needs $\sum a_i=1$; $\mathrm{Var}(\sum a_iW_i)=\sum a_i^2\sigma_i^2$. Lagrange: $2a_i\sigma_i^2=\lambda$, so $a_i\propto1/\sigma_i^2$; normalizing gives $a_i^*=(1/\sigma_i^2)/\sum_j(1/\sigma_j^2)$, i.e. $W^*$.
(b) $\mathrm{Var}(W^*)=\sum(a_i^*)^2\sigma_i^2=\sum(1/\sigma_i^2)/\big(\sum_j1/\sigma_j^2\big)^2=1/\sum(1/\sigma_i^2)$.
