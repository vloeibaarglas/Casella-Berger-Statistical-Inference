**Central idea.** (a) is one line from (3.4.4)/(3.4.5) with $w(p)=\log\frac{p}{1-p}$; (b) gets gamma moments from the identities and transfers them to beta via $U/(U+V)$.

**Solution.** (a) $f(x|p)=\binom{n}{x}(1-p)^n\exp(x\log\frac{p}{1-p})$: $w_1'(p)=\frac{1}{p(1-p)}$, $-c'/c=\frac{n}{1-p}$. (3.4.4) gives $EX=np$. (3.4.5): $w_1''(p)EX+(w_1'(p))^2\text{Var}\,X=-(\log c)''(p)=\frac{n}{(1-p)^2}$; with $w_1''(p)=-\frac{1}{p^2}+\frac{1}{(1-p)^2}$ this yields Var$X=np(1-p)$.
(b) The identities give log-moments of beta directly; for $EX$ and Var$X$ use the gamma bridge. The identities applied to gamma$(a,1)$ give $EU=a$, Var$U=a$ (cf. 3.32(b)). With independent $U\sim\text{gamma}(a,1)$, $V\sim\text{gamma}(b,1)$: $X\stackrel{d}{=}U/(U+V)\sim\text{beta}(a,b)$, independent of $S=U+V\sim\text{gamma}(a+b,1)$ (Jacobian factorization). Then $a=EU=E[XS]=EX\cdot ES=EX(a+b)$, so $EX=\frac{a}{a+b}$; and $E[U^2]=E[X^2]E[S^2]$ gives $E[X^2]=\frac{a(a+1)}{(a+b)(a+b+1)}$, so Var$X=\frac{ab}{(a+b)^2(a+b+1)}$.
