**Central idea.** $F_T(T\mid\theta_0) \sim \text{Uniform}(0,1)$ by the probability integral transform, so the acceptance probability is exactly the length of $[\alpha_1, 1-\alpha_2]$.

**Solution.** Since $T$ is continuous, $U = F_T(T\mid\theta_0) \sim U(0,1)$, and $P_{\theta_0}(\alpha_1 \le U \le 1-\alpha_2) = 1 - \alpha_2 - \alpha_1 = 1-\alpha$: an $\alpha$-level acceptance region. Inverting, $C(t) = \{\theta_0 : \alpha_1 \le F_T(t\mid\theta_0) \le 1-\alpha_2\}$ satisfies $P_\theta(\theta \in C(T)) = P_\theta(\alpha_1 \le F_T(T\mid\theta) \le 1-\alpha_2) = 1-\alpha$.
