**Central idea.** Mutual exclusivity forces $P(A \cap B) = 0$ while independence (with positive probabilities) forces $P(A \cap B) > 0$ — direct contradiction.

**Solution.** (a) Mutually exclusive $\Rightarrow P(A \cap B) = 0$, but independence would give $P(A \cap B) = P(A)P(B) > 0$. Contradiction. (b) Contrapositive of (a): independence gives $P(A \cap B) = P(A)P(B) > 0 \neq 0$, so they cannot be mutually exclusive.
