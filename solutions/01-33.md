**Central idea.** Bayes' theorem with equal priors — the likelihood ratio $0.05/0.0025 = 20$ dominates.

**Solution.** Let $M$ = male, $C$ = color-blind. $P(C \mid M) = 0.05$, $P(C \mid M^c) = 0.0025$, $P(M) = 1/2$. Then
$$P(M \mid C) = \frac{0.05 \cdot \frac12}{0.05\cdot\frac12 + 0.0025\cdot\frac12} = \frac{0.05}{0.0525} = \frac{20}{21} \approx 0.952.$$
