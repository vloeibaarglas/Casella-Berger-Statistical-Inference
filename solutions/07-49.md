**Central idea.** Here exponential$(\lambda)$ uses the textbook mean/scale convention: $f(x\mid\lambda)=\lambda^{-1}e^{-x/\lambda}$. The previous impossibility claim incorrectly treated $\lambda$ as a rate.

**Solution.** (a) $Y=\min_iX_i$ has survival $P_\lambda(Y>y)=e^{-ny/\lambda}$, so $EY=\lambda/n$ and $nY$ is unbiased for $\lambda$.
(b) $\bar X$ is also unbiased, and for $n>1$,
$$\operatorname{Var}(\bar X)=\lambda^2/n<\lambda^2=\operatorname{Var}(nY).$$
It is the UMVUE because $\sum X_i$ is complete sufficient. In fact, symmetry of the exponential sample conditional on its sum gives $E(nY\mid\sum X_i)=\bar X$, so this is a Rao–Blackwell improvement. For $n=1$ the estimators coincide.
(c) $n=12$, $Y=50.1$, and $\sum x_i=1497.9$. The estimates are $nY=601.2$ hours and $\bar X=124.825$ hours.
