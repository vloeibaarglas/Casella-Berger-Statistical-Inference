**Central idea.** For Bernoulli, $I(p)=1/[p(1-p)]$ and $\mathrm{Var}(\bar X)=p(1-p)/n$ — exactly the CRLB.
**Solution.** $\log f(x\mid p)=x\log p+(1-x)\log(1-p)$; $\frac{\partial^2}{\partial p^2}\log f=-\frac{x}{p^2}-\frac{1-x}{(1-p)^2}$, so $I(p)=\frac{1}{p(1-p)}$. The CRLB for unbiased estimators of $p$ is $\frac{1}{nI(p)}=\frac{p(1-p)}{n}=\mathrm{Var}_p(\bar X)$. Hence $\bar X$ attains the bound and is the best (UMVUE) unbiased estimator of $p$.
