**Central idea.** The score is a sum of iid mean-zero terms (regularity lets the derivative pass under the integral); the CLT and WLLN do the rest.

**Solution.** (a) $l'(\theta_0\mid X) = \sum_i \frac{f'(X_i\mid\theta_0)}{f(X_i\mid\theta_0)} = \sum_i W_i$, so $\frac{1}{\sqrt n}l'(\theta_0\mid X) = \sqrt n\cdot\frac1n\sum_i W_i$. Now $E_{\theta_0}W_1 = \int \frac{f'(x\mid\theta_0)}{f(x\mid\theta_0)}f(x\mid\theta_0)\,dx = \frac{d}{d\theta}\int f(x\mid\theta)\,dx\big|_{\theta_0} = 0$ by regularity, and $\mathrm{Var}(W_1) = E_{\theta_0}W_1^2 = I(\theta_0)$. By the CLT, $\frac{1}{\sqrt n}\sum_i W_i \xrightarrow{d} n[0,I(\theta_0)]$.
(b) Differentiating $l'(\theta\mid X) = \sum_i f'(X_i\mid\theta)/f(X_i\mid\theta)$,
$$l''(\theta_0\mid X) = \sum_i\left[\frac{f''(X_i\mid\theta_0)}{f(X_i\mid\theta_0)} - \left(\frac{f'(X_i\mid\theta_0)}{f(X_i\mid\theta_0)}\right)^2\right] = \sum_i\frac{f''(X_i\mid\theta_0)}{f(X_i\mid\theta_0)} - \sum_i W_i^2,$$
so $-\frac1n l''(\theta_0\mid X) = \frac1n\sum_i W_i^2 - \frac1n\sum_i f''(X_i\mid\theta_0)/f(X_i\mid\theta_0)$. Moreover $E_{\theta_0}W_1^2 = I(\theta_0)$ and $E_{\theta_0}[f''(X_1\mid\theta_0)/f(X_1\mid\theta_0)] = \frac{d^2}{d\theta^2}\int f(x\mid\theta)\,dx\big|_{\theta_0} = 0$. By the WLLN the two averages converge in probability to $I(\theta_0)$ and $0$, so $-\frac1n l''(\theta_0\mid X)\xrightarrow{p} I(\theta_0)$.
