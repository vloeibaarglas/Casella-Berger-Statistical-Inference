**Central idea.** Total variance on the mixture indicator: average of the component variances plus the variance of the component means.

**Solution.** Let $Y_i\in\{0,1\}$ indicate contamination. $E[\mathrm{Var}(X_i\mid Y_i)] = (1-\delta)\sigma^2+\delta\tau^2$ and $E[X_i\mid Y_i]$ equals $\theta$ or $\mu$, so $\mathrm{Var}(E[X_i\mid Y_i]) = (\theta-\mu)^2\delta(1-\delta)$. Hence $\mathrm{Var}(X_i) = (1-\delta)\sigma^2+\delta\tau^2+\delta(1-\delta)(\theta-\mu)^2$, and $\mathrm{Var}(\bar X)=\mathrm{Var}(X_1)/n$ gives the formula. For Cauchy contamination $\tau^2=\infty$, so $\mathrm{Var}(\bar X)=\infty$ for any $\delta>0$.
