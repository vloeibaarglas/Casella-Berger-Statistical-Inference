**Central idea.** Each is a smooth monotone transform of a gamma/exponential variable: push the pdf through the change of variables, then read off mean/variance from gamma integrals.

**Solution.** (a) $x=y^\gamma$: $f_Y(y)=\frac{1}{\beta}e^{-y^\gamma/\beta}\gamma y^{\gamma-1}$, $y>0$ (integrates to 1 via $u=y^\gamma/\beta$). $EY=\beta^{1/\gamma}\Gamma(1+1/\gamma)$, Var$Y=\beta^{2/\gamma}\big[\Gamma(1+2/\gamma)-\Gamma(1+1/\gamma)^2\big]$. (b) $x=\beta y^2/2$: $f_Y(y)=\frac{1}{\beta}e^{-y^2/2}\beta y = ye^{-y^2/2}$, $y>0$ (Rayleigh; note $\beta$ cancels). $EY=\sqrt{\pi/2}$, $EY^2=2$, Var$Y=2-\pi/2$. (c) $x=1/y$: $f_Y(y)=\frac{1}{\Gamma(a)b^a y^{a+1}}e^{-1/(by)}$, $y>0$ (integrates to 1 via $u=1/(by)$). $EY=\frac{1}{b(a-1)}$ ($a>1$), Var$Y=\frac{1}{b^2(a-1)^2(a-2)}$ ($a>2$). (d) $x=\beta y^2$: $f_Y(y)=\frac{4}{\sqrt\pi}y^2e^{-y^2}$, $y>0$ (Maxwell; $\beta$ cancels). $EY=2/\sqrt\pi$, $EY^2=3/2$, Var$Y=3/2-4/\pi$. (e) $x=e^{(\alpha-y)/\gamma}$: $f_Y(y)=\frac{1}{\gamma}\exp\!\left(-\frac{y-\alpha}{\gamma}-e^{-(y-\alpha)/\gamma}\right)$, $y\in\mathbb{R}$ (Gumbel). $EY=\alpha+\gamma\eta$ ($\eta\approx0.5772$ Euler–Mascheroni, since $E\log X=-\eta$ for Exp$(1)$), Var$Y=\pi^2\gamma^2/6$.
