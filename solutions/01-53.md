**Central idea.** Differentiate for the pdf; for (c), push the transformation through the cdf: $F_Z(z) = F_Y(1+z/10)$.

**Solution.** (a) With $F_Y(y) = 0$ for $y < 1$: $F_Y$ is continuous, $F_Y'(y) = 2/y^3 > 0$ on $[1,\infty)$ so nondecreasing, $\lim_{y\downarrow1}F_Y(y) = 0$, $\lim_{y\to\infty}F_Y(y) = 1$. (b) $f_Y(y) = F_Y'(y) = 2/y^3$, $y \geq 1$ (0 otherwise). (c) For $z \geq 0$,
$$F_Z(z) = P(10(Y-1) \leq z) = P\left(Y \leq 1+\frac{z}{10}\right) = 1-\frac{1}{(1+z/10)^2} = 1-\frac{100}{(10+z)^2},$$
and $F_Z(z) = 0$ for $z < 0$.
