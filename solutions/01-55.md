**Central idea.** $V$ is mixed: an atom at $5$ (when $T<3$) plus a continuous tail (when $T \geq 3$); build the cdf piecewise from $T$'s exponential law.

**Solution.** $T \sim \text{Exp}(\text{mean }1.5)$, $F_T(t) = 1-e^{-t/1.5}$. $P(V = 5) = P(T < 3) = 1-e^{-2}$. For $v < 5$: $F_V(v) = 0$. At $v = 5$ the cdf jumps to $1-e^{-2}$. For $5 \le v < 6$ there is no mass ($V = 2T \geq 6$ when $T \geq 3$), so $F_V(v) = 1-e^{-2}$. For $v \geq 6$: $\{V \leq v\} = \{T \leq v/2\}$, so $F_V(v) = 1-e^{-(v/2)/1.5} = 1-e^{-v/3}$. In summary,
$$F_V(v) = \begin{cases} 0 & v < 5, \\ 1-e^{-2} & 5 \leq v < 6, \\ 1-e^{-v/3} & v \geq 6. \end{cases}$$
