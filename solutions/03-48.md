**Central idea.** Each pmf's consecutive ratio $p(x+1)/p(x)$ is a rational function; that ratio *is* the recursion.

**Solution.** Binomial: $\frac{p(x+1)}{p(x)}=\frac{\binom{n}{x+1}}{\binom{n}{x}}\frac{p}{1-p}$, i.e. $p(x+1)=\frac{n-x}{x+1}\cdot\frac{p}{1-p}\,p(x)$. Negative binomial: $\frac{p(y+1)}{p(y)}=\frac{\binom{r+y}{y+1}}{\binom{r+y-1}{y}}(1-p)$, i.e. $p(y+1)=\frac{r+y}{y+1}(1-p)\,p(y)$. Hypergeometric: $\frac{p(x+1)}{p(x)}=\frac{M-x}{x+1}\cdot\frac{K-x}{N-M-K+x+1}$, i.e. $p(x+1)=\frac{(M-x)(K-x)}{(x+1)(N-M-K+x+1)}\,p(x)$.
