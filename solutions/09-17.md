**Central idea.** Extremes are the natural pivots for bounded-support families: $X_{(1)} - \theta$ for location, $X_{(n)}/\theta$ for scale.

**Solution.** (a) $Q = X_{(1)} - \theta$: $P(Q > q) = (1/2 - q)^n$, $-1/2 \le q \le 1/2$, a pivot. With $P(a \le Q \le b) = 1-\alpha$, e.g. equal-tail $a = \frac12 - (1-\alpha/2)^{1/n}$, $b = \frac12 - (\alpha/2)^{1/n}$: $[X_{(1)} - b,\ X_{(1)} - a]$.
(b) $P(X_{(n)} \le t) = (t/\theta)^{2n}$, so $Q = X_{(n)}/\theta$ has cdf $u^{2n}$ on $[0,1]$, a pivot. Shortest $1-\alpha$ interval (cf. 9.37): $P(\alpha^{1/(2n)} \le Q \le 1) = 1-\alpha$ gives $[y,\ y/\alpha^{1/(2n)}]$.
