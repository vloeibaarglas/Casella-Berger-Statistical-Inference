**Central idea.** Normalize by integrating over the rectangle; marginalize by integrating out $y$; for (d) use the cdf method on the monotone decreasing transform, differentiating via the chain rule.

**Solution.** (a) $1 = \int_0^2\int_0^1 C(x+2y)\,dy\,dx = C\int_0^2 (x+1)\,dx = 4C$, so $C = 1/4$.
(b) $f_X(x) = \int_0^1 \frac{x+2y}{4}\,dy = \frac{x+1}{4}$, $0 < x < 2$.
(c) For $0 < x < 2$, $0 < y < 1$: $F(x,y) = \int_0^x\int_0^y \frac{s+2t}{4}\,dt\,ds = \frac{x^2y}{8}+\frac{xy^2}{4}$. Piecing together: $F(x,y) = 0$ if $x \le 0$ or $y \le 0$; $F(x,y) = \frac{x^2y}{8}+\frac{xy^2}{4}$ on $(0,2)\times(0,1)$; $F(x,y) = \frac{x^2}{8}+\frac{x}{4}$ for $0<x<2$, $y \ge 1$; $F(x,y) = \frac{y+y^2}{2}$ for $x \ge 2$, $0<y<1$; $F(x,y)=1$ for $x \ge 2$, $y \ge 1$.
(d) $X \in (0,2)$ gives $Z \in (1,9)$, and $z = 9/(x+1)^2$ is strictly decreasing in $x$. For $1 < z < 9$: $F_Z(z) = P(X \ge 3/\sqrt{z}-1) = 1-F_X(3/\sqrt{z}-1)$, where $F_X(x) = (x^2+2x)/8$ on $(0,2)$. Differentiating, $f_Z(z) = f_X(3/\sqrt{z}-1)\cdot\frac{3}{2z^{3/2}} = \frac{3}{4\sqrt{z}}\cdot\frac{3}{2z^{3/2}} = \frac{9}{8z^2}$, $1 < z < 9$ (which integrates to $1$).
