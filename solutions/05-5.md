**Central idea.** This is just the change-of-variables formula for the linear map $Y = n\bar{X}$; no mgf is needed.

**Solution.** Let $Y = \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i = n\bar{X}$, so $\bar{X} = Y/n$. By the univariate transformation formula (Theorem 2.1.5), with $y = nx$,
$$f_{\bar{X}}(x) = f_Y(nx)\left|\frac{d}{dx}(nx)\right| = n\, f_{X_1+\cdots+X_n}(nx).$$
Equivalently via cdfs: $P(\bar{X} \leq x) = P(Y \leq nx)$, and differentiating gives the same result. No mgf is used.
