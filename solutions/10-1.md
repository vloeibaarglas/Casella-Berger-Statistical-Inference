**Central idea.** The mean is linear in $\theta$: $E_\theta X = \theta/3$, so $3\bar X$ is unbiased for $\theta$ and consistent by the WLLN.

**Solution.** $E_\theta X_1 = \int_{-1}^{1}x\cdot\tfrac12(1+\theta x)\,dx = \tfrac12\left(0+\theta\cdot\tfrac23\right) = \theta/3$. Hence $\tilde\theta = 3\bar X$ satisfies $E_\theta\tilde\theta = \theta$. Moreover $E_\theta X_1^2 = \int_{-1}^1 x^2\cdot\tfrac12(1+\theta x)\,dx = 1/3 < \infty$, so $\mathrm{Var}_\theta(X_1) < \infty$ and the WLLN gives $\bar X \xrightarrow{p} \theta/3$. By continuity, $3\bar X \xrightarrow{p} \theta$; i.e., $3\bar X$ is consistent for $\theta$ (and unbiased).
