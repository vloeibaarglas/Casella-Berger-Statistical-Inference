**Central idea.** With a finite parameter space, the MLE is found by direct comparison: for the observed $x$, pick the $\theta$ maximizing that row of the table.
**Solution.** For each observed $x$, $\hat\theta(x)=\arg\max_{\theta\in\{1,2,3\}}f(x\mid\theta)$:
- $x=0$: values $(1/3,1/4,0)$, max at $\theta=1$.
- $x=1$: values $(1/3,1/4,0)$, max at $\theta=1$.
- $x=2$: values $(0,1/4,1/4)$, max (tied) at $\theta=2$ or $3$.
- $x=3$: values $(1/6,1/4,1/2)$, max at $\theta=3$.
- $x=4$: values $(1/6,0,1/4)$, max at $\theta=3$.

Hence $\hat\theta(0)=\hat\theta(1)=1$, $\hat\theta(3)=\hat\theta(4)=3$, and $\hat\theta(2)$ is not unique ($2$ or $3$ both maximize the likelihood).
