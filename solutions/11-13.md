**Central idea.** The LRT statistic reduces to $(1 + \mathrm{SSB}/\mathrm{SSW})^{-N/2}$, monotone decreasing in the $F$ ratio.

**Solution.** Unrestricted MLEs: $\hat\theta_i = \bar{Y}_{i\cdot}$, $\hat\sigma^2 = \sum_{i,j}(Y_{ij}-\bar{Y}_{i\cdot})^2/N = \mathrm{SSW}/N$. Under $H_0$ ($\theta_i \equiv \theta$): $\hat\theta = \bar{\bar{Y}}$, $\hat\sigma_0^2 = \sum_{i,j}(Y_{ij}-\bar{\bar{Y}})^2/N = \mathrm{SST}/N = (\mathrm{SSB}+\mathrm{SSW})/N$. The likelihood ratio is
$$\lambda = \left(\frac{\hat\sigma_0^2}{\hat\sigma^2}\right)^{-N/2} = \left(\frac{\mathrm{SSB}+\mathrm{SSW}}{\mathrm{SSW}}\right)^{-N/2} = \left(1 + \frac{\mathrm{SSB}}{\mathrm{SSW}}\right)^{-N/2},$$
which is strictly decreasing in $\mathrm{SSB}/\mathrm{SSW}$, hence strictly decreasing in $F = \frac{\mathrm{SSB}/(k-1)}{\mathrm{SSW}/(N-k)}$. So $\{\lambda < c\} = \{F > c'\}$: the LRT rejects for large $F$, the $F$ test of (11.2.14).
