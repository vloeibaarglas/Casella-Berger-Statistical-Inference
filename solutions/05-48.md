**Central idea.** $F_{m,n} = (\chi^2_m/m)/(\chi^2_n/n)$, and for even df a chi-square is a sum of $-2\log U_i$ terms by (5.6.5).

**Solution.** Generate $U_1, \ldots, U_{m/2}, V_1, \ldots, V_{n/2}$ iid uniform$(0,1)$. Put
$$A = -2\sum_{i=1}^{m/2}\log U_i \sim \chi^2_m, \qquad B = -2\sum_{i=1}^{n/2}\log V_i \sim \chi^2_n,$$
independent by (5.6.5). Then
$$F = \frac{A/m}{B/n} \sim F_{m,n}.$$
(Even degrees of freedom are exactly what make the $-2\log U$ construction give the right df.)
