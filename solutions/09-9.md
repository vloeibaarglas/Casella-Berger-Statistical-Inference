**Central idea.** Write $X_i = \mu + \sigma Z_i$ with $Z_i$ iid $\sim f$; in each family the parameters cancel from the stated quantity.

**Solution.** In the location family $f(x-\mu)$ ($\sigma$ known $=1$), $\bar X - \mu = \bar Z$ is free of $\mu$. In the scale family $\frac1\sigma f(x/\sigma)$ ($\mu = 0$), $\bar X/\sigma = \bar Z$ is free of $\sigma$. In the location–scale family, $\frac{\bar X-\mu}{S} = \frac{\bar Z}{S_Z}$ is free of both $\mu$ and $\sigma$.
