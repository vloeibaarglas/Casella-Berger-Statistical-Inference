**Central idea.** The $n$ different support constraints $X_i \ge i\theta$ collapse into the single constraint $\theta \le \min_i(X_i/i)$; everything else in the likelihood is $\theta$-free.

**Solution.** The joint density is
$$\prod_{i=1}^n e^{i\theta-x_i}I(x_i \ge i\theta) = \exp\!\left(\theta\sum_{i=1}^n i - \sum_{i=1}^n x_i\right) I\!\left(\theta \le \min_i \frac{x_i}{i}\right) = g(T(\mathbf{x})\mid\theta)\,h(\mathbf{x}),$$
with $T(\mathbf{x}) = \min_i(x_i/i)$, $g(t\mid\theta) = e^{\theta n(n+1)/2}I(t \ge \theta)$, $h(\mathbf{x}) = e^{-\sum x_i}$. By the Factorization Theorem, $T$ is sufficient.
