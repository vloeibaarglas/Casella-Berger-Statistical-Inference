**Central idea.** The natural parameter is $\eta=w(\theta)$; its space is the image $w(\Theta)$ (which here coincides with the set where $\int h\,e^{\eta\cdot t}<\infty$).

**Solution.** (a) Normal: $\mu$ unknown: $\eta=\mu/\sigma^2\in\mathbb{R}$; $\sigma$ unknown: $\eta=-1/(2\sigma^2)\in(-\infty,0)$. (b) Gamma: both unknown: $(\eta_1,\eta_2)=(\alpha,-1/\beta)\in(0,\infty)\times(-\infty,0)$; $\alpha$ known: $\eta=-1/\beta\in(-\infty,0)$; $\beta$ known: $\eta=\alpha\in(0,\infty)$. (c) Beta: both unknown: $(\alpha,\beta)\in(0,\infty)^2$; one known: the other ranges over $(0,\infty)$. (d) Poisson: $\eta=\log\lambda\in\mathbb{R}$. (e) Negative binomial ($r$ known): $\eta=\log(1-p)\in(-\infty,0)$.
