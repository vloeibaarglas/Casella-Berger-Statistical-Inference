**Central idea.** Expand $\mathrm{E}[\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y|Z)] = \mathrm{E}[XY]-\mathrm{E}[\mathrm{E}(X|Z)\mathrm{E}(Y|Z)]$ by iterated expectation; the subtracted term is exactly what $\mathrm{Cov}(\mathrm{E}(X|Z),\mathrm{E}(Y|Z))$ adds back, leaving $\mathrm{E}[XY]-\mathrm{E}X\,\mathrm{E}Y$.

**Solution.** $\mathrm{E}[\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y|Z)] = \mathrm{E}[\mathrm{E}(XY|Z)-\mathrm{E}(X|Z)\mathrm{E}(Y|Z)] = \mathrm{E}[XY]-\mathrm{E}[\mathrm{E}(X|Z)\mathrm{E}(Y|Z)]$. And $\mathrm{Cov}(\mathrm{E}(X|Z),\mathrm{E}(Y|Z)) = \mathrm{E}[\mathrm{E}(X|Z)\mathrm{E}(Y|Z)]-\mathrm{E}[\mathrm{E}(X|Z)]\mathrm{E}[\mathrm{E}(Y|Z)] = \mathrm{E}[\mathrm{E}(X|Z)\mathrm{E}(Y|Z)]-\mathrm{E}X\,\mathrm{E}Y$. Adding gives $\mathrm{E}[XY]-\mathrm{E}X\,\mathrm{E}Y = \mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)$.
