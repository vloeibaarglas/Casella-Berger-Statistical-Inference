**Central idea.** Shift $\hat p$ by $\pm 1/(2n)$ before solving the score quadratic: each endpoint moves outward, widening the interval; the widening lifts the coverage dips above $1-\alpha$.

**Solution.** The corrected endpoints solve $\frac{|(\hat p\pm 1/(2n))-p|}{\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}}\le z_{\alpha/2}$: the upper endpoint is the larger root of $(\hat p+\frac{1}{2n}-p)^2 = z_{\alpha/2}^2\frac{p(1-p)}{n}$, i.e.
$$p:\ \left(1+\frac{z_{\alpha/2}^2}{n}\right)p^2-\left(2\hat p+\frac{1}{n}+\frac{z_{\alpha/2}^2}{n}\right)p+\left(\hat p+\frac{1}{2n}\right)^2\le 0\quad\text{(larger root)},$$
and the lower endpoint is the smaller root of the same quadratic with $\hat p-\frac{1}{2n}$. Wider: at the uncorrected upper root $p_U$, $|(\hat p+\frac{1}{2n})-p_U| = z_{\alpha/2}\sqrt{p_U(1-p_U)/n}+\frac{1}{2n} > z_{\alpha/2}\sqrt{p_U(1-p_U)/n}$, so $p_U$ lies strictly inside the corrected interval — the corrected upper (lower) endpoint is larger (smaller). E.g. $n=20$, $y=10$: uncorrected $(0.299,0.701)$, corrected $(0.279,0.722)$. Coverage: over a grid of $p\in[0.02,0.98]$ ($n=20$, 95% nominal), the uncorrected score interval's minimum coverage is $0.927$ (dips below $0.95$), while the corrected interval's minimum is $0.959$ — uniformly higher, maintaining $\ge 1-\alpha$ throughout.
