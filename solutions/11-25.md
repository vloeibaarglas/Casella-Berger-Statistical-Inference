**Central idea.** Setting the gradient of the quadratic $\mathrm{RSS}(c,d) = \sum(y_i-c-dx_i)^2$ to zero gives the normal equations; the Hessian $2\left(\begin{smallmatrix}n & \sum x_i\\ \sum x_i & \sum x_i^2\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ is positive definite ($nS_{xx} > 0$), so the critical point is the global minimum.

**Solution.** (a) $\frac{\partial\mathrm{RSS}}{\partial c} = -2\sum_{i}(y_i-c-dx_i) = 0$ gives $nc + d\sum x_i = \sum y_i$; $\frac{\partial\mathrm{RSS}}{\partial d} = -2\sum_i x_i(y_i-c-dx_i) = 0$ gives $c\sum x_i + d\sum x_i^2 = \sum x_iy_i$.
(b) With $a = \bar y - b\bar x$, $b = S_{xy}/S_{xx}$: $na + b\sum x_i = n\bar y - bn\bar x + bn\bar x = \sum y_i$ ✓; and $a\sum x_i + b\sum x_i^2 = n\bar x\bar y - bn\bar x^2 + b\sum x_i^2 = n\bar x\bar y + bS_{xx} = n\bar x\bar y + S_{xy} = \sum x_iy_i$ ✓ (since $S_{xy} = \sum x_iy_i - n\bar x\bar y$).
(c) The Hessian is $2\begin{pmatrix} n & \sum x_i \\ \sum x_i & \sum x_i^2 \end{pmatrix}$ with leading minor $2n > 0$ and determinant $4(n\sum x_i^2 - (\sum x_i)^2) = 4nS_{xx} > 0$ (unless all $x_i$ equal), hence positive definite: $(a,b)$ is the unique global minimizer.
