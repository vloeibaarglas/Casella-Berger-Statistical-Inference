**Central idea.** Chain the conditional probabilities along the nested sequence $C \supset B \supset (A \cap B)$: $P(A \cap B \cap C) = P(C)\,P(B \mid C)\,P(A \mid B \cap C)$.

**Solution.** (a) $A \cap B \cap C$ is the event that a U.S. birth results in identical twin females (all three conditions hold at once). (b) $P(C) = 1/90$. Given twins, $P(B \mid C) = 1/3$. Given identical twins, $P(A \mid B \cap C) = 1/2$ (same sex, equally likely). Hence
$$P(A \cap B \cap C) = \frac{1}{90}\cdot\frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{540}.$$
