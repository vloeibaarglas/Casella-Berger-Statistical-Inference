**Central idea.** The ratio is monotone in $x^2$ but $x^2$ is U-shaped in $x$ — folding via $|X|$ restores monotonicity on $(0,\infty)$, and symmetry makes the sign ancillary.

**Solution.** (a) For $\theta_2>\theta_1$, $\frac{f(x\mid\theta_2)}{f(x\mid\theta_1)}=\frac{\theta_2}{\theta_1}\cdot\frac{\theta_1^2+x^2}{\theta_2^2+x^2}$, which as a function of $x$ strictly decreases on $(-\infty,0)$ and strictly increases on $(0,\infty)$ (it is increasing in $x^2$): not monotone in $x$. No MLR.
(b) $f(x\mid\theta)$ depends on $x$ only through $x^2=|x|^2$, and by symmetry $P_\theta(X>0\mid|X|=t)=1/2$ for all $\theta$: the conditional distribution of $X$ given $|X|$ is free of $\theta$, so $|X|$ is sufficient (factorization with $h\equiv1$ on the sign). $Y=|X|$ has pdf $f_Y(y\mid\theta)=\frac{2\theta}{\pi(\theta^2+y^2)}$, $y>0$, and
$$\frac{f_Y(y\mid\theta_2)}{f_Y(y\mid\theta_1)}=\frac{\theta_2}{\theta_1}\cdot\frac{\theta_1^2+y^2}{\theta_2^2+y^2}, \qquad \frac{d}{d(y^2)}\log(\cdot)=\frac{\theta_2^2-\theta_1^2}{(\theta_1^2+y^2)(\theta_2^2+y^2)}>0,$$
strictly increasing in $y$ on $(0,\infty)$: the family of $|X|$ has MLR.
