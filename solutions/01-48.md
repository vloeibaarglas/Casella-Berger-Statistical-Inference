**Central idea.** Theorem 1.5.3 characterizes cdfs (nondecreasing, right-continuous, limits $0$/$1$); each property follows from the corresponding set relation plus continuity of probability.

**Solution.** Let $F(x) = P(X \leq x)$. Nondecreasing: $x_1 < x_2 \Rightarrow \{X \leq x_1\} \subset \{X \leq x_2\}$. Right continuity: if $x_n \downarrow x$ then $\{X \leq x_n\} \downarrow \{X \leq x\}$, so $F(x_n) \to F(x)$ by continuity of $P$. Limits: $\{X \leq -n\} \downarrow \emptyset$ gives $F(x) \to 0$ as $x \to -\infty$; $\{X \leq n\} \uparrow S$ gives $F(x) \to 1$ as $x \to \infty$.
