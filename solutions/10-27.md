**Central idea.** The contaminated cdf $F_\delta=(1-\delta)F+\delta\Delta_x$ shifts the median; implicit differentiation of $F(a_\delta)=1/[2(1-\delta)]$ gives the influence $\pm 1/[2f(m)]$ — bounded, unlike the mean's.

**Solution.** (a) $T(F_\delta)=(1-\delta)\mu+\delta x$, so $IF(\bar X,x)=\lim_{\delta\to0}[T(F_\delta)-T(F)]/\delta = x-\mu$.
(b) $F_\delta = (1-\delta)F+\delta\Delta_x$ gives $P_{F_\delta}(X\le a) = (1-\delta)F(a)+\delta\mathbf{1}\{x\le a\}$, i.e. the two cases shown. The median solves $(1-\delta)F(m)+\delta\mathbf{1}\{x\le m\}=1/2$: if $x>m$, $F(m)=1/[2(1-\delta)]$; if $x\le m$, $F(m)=(1/2-\delta)/(1-\delta)$, giving the two cases (each consistent on its stated region).
(c) Put $a_\delta = F^{-1}(1/[2(1-\delta)])$, so $F(a_\delta)=1/[2(1-\delta)]$. Differentiating in $\delta$: $f(a_\delta)a'_\delta = 1/[2(1-\delta)^2]$, and at $\delta=0$, $a_0=m=F^{-1}(1/2)$, so $a'_0 = 1/[2f(m)]$ — the displayed limit. For $x<m$, with $b_\delta=F^{-1}((1/2-\delta)/(1-\delta))$: $f(b_\delta)b'_\delta = \frac{d}{d\delta}\frac{1/2-\delta}{1-\delta} = \frac{-1/2}{(1-\delta)^2}$, so $b'_0=-1/[2f(m)]$. Hence
$$IF(M,x) = \begin{cases} \frac{1}{2f(m)} & x>m,\\ -\frac{1}{2f(m)} & x<m,\end{cases}$$
bounded in $x$, in contrast to $IF(\bar X,x)=x-\mu$.
