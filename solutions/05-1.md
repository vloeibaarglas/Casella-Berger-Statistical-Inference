**Central idea.** Work with the complement: $P(\text{at least one}) = 1 - P(\text{none}) = 1 - 0.99^n$.

**Solution.** With replacement, the number of color-blind people in the sample is Binomial$(n, 0.01)$. We need $1 - 0.99^n \geq 0.95$, i.e., $0.99^n \leq 0.05$, so $n \geq \log(0.05)/\log(0.99) \approx 298.07$. Hence $n = 299$ (the smallest integer doing the job).
