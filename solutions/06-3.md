**Central idea.** The support constraint involves only the minimum, and the exponent involves only the sum — the two pieces separate cleanly.

**Solution.** The joint pdf is
$$\sigma^{-n}\exp\!\left(-\frac{1}{\sigma}\sum_{i=1}^n(x_i-\mu)\right) I(x_{(1)} > \mu) = \sigma^{-n}\exp\!\left(-\frac{n(\bar{x}-\mu)}{\sigma}\right) I(x_{(1)} \ge \mu),$$
which is $g(\bar{x},x_{(1)}\mid\mu,\sigma)\cdot 1$. Hence $(\bar{X}, X_{(1)})$ — equivalently $(\sum X_i, \min_i X_i)$ — is a two-dimensional sufficient statistic for $(\mu,\sigma)$.
