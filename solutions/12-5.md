**Central idea.** After concentrating out $\alpha,\beta,\xi_1,\dots,\xi_n$, the profile likelihood (12.2.17) is a one-parameter scaled-$\chi^2$-type likelihood in $\sigma^2_\delta$.

**Solution.** From (12.2.17), with $A=\frac{\lambda}{1+\lambda\hat\beta^2}\sum_{i=1}^n(y_i-(\hat\alpha+\hat\beta x_i))^2$,
$$\log L = \frac{n}{2}\log\lambda-n\log(2\pi)-n\log\sigma^2_\delta-\frac{A}{2\sigma^2_\delta}.$$
Differentiating in $\sigma^2_\delta$: $-n/\sigma^2_\delta+A/(2(\sigma^2_\delta)^2)=0$, so $\hat\sigma^2_\delta=A/(2n)$, i.e.
$$\hat\sigma^2_\delta=\frac{1}{2n}\frac{\lambda}{1+\lambda\hat\beta^2}\sum_{i=1}^n\big(y_i-(\hat\alpha+\hat\beta x_i)\big)^2,$$
which is (12.2.18). It is a maximum since the second derivative is negative there.
