**Central idea.** $g(x)=x/(x+1)$ is strictly increasing on $[0,\infty)$, so $Y$ is just a relabeling of the mass points: $f_Y(y)=f_X(g^{-1}(y))$ on the image values.

**Solution.** $y = x/(x+1) \iff x = y/(1-y)$, giving the values $y\in\{0,\frac12,\frac23,\frac34,\dots\}=\{\frac{k}{k+1}:k=0,1,2,\dots\}$. Hence
$$f_Y(y) = \frac13\left(\frac23\right)^{y/(1-y)},\qquad y=\frac{k}{k+1}\ (k=0,1,2,\dots),$$
and $f_Y(y)=0$ otherwise. This sums to 1 since it is $f_X$ composed with the inverse bijection.
