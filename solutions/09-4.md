**Central idea.** With known zero means, $\sum X_i^2/\sigma_X^2 \sim \chi^2_n$; the log-LRT is a U-shaped function $h(w)$ of $w = W/\lambda_0$ where $W = (\sum Y_j^2/m)/(\sum X_i^2/n)$, so the LRT rejects for $w$ too small or too large.

**Solution.** (a) Unconstrained MLEs: $\hat\sigma_X^2 = \sum X_i^2/n$, $\hat\sigma_Y^2 = \sum Y_j^2/m$; constrained MLE $\tilde\sigma_X^2 = (\sum X_i^2 + \sum Y_j^2/\lambda_0)/(n+m)$. Writing $w = W/\lambda_0$,
$$-2\log\lambda = (n+m)\log\frac{n+mw}{n+m} - m\log w \equiv h(w),$$
with $h(1) = 0$, $h$ decreasing on $(0,1)$ and increasing on $(1,\infty)$. Reject $H_0$ for $h(W/\lambda_0) > c_\alpha$.
(b) Under $H_0$, $W/\lambda_0 = \frac{\sum Y_j^2/(m\sigma_Y^2)}{\sum X_i^2/(n\sigma_X^2)} \sim F_{m,n}$. The rejection region is $\{W/\lambda_0 < w_L\} \cup \{W/\lambda_0 > w_U\}$ with $h(w_L) = h(w_U) = c_\alpha$ and $P_{H_0}(\text{RR}) = \alpha$.
(c) Inverting, $C = \{\lambda_0 : w_L \le W/\lambda_0 \le w_U\} = [W/w_U,\ W/w_L]$, a $1-\alpha$ interval for $\lambda$ (equal-tail version: $[W/F_{m,n,\alpha/2},\ W/F_{m,n,1-\alpha/2}]$).
