**Central idea.** Substitute $z=(x-\mu)/\sigma$; symmetry of $f$ about 0 makes the lower half-mass exactly $1/2$.

**Solution.** $P(X\le\mu)=\int_{-\infty}^\mu\frac1\sigma f(\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma})dx=\int_{-\infty}^0f(z)dz$. By symmetry $f(z)=f(-z)$, so $\int_{-\infty}^0f=\int_0^\infty f=1/2$; likewise $P(X\ge\mu)=1/2$. Hence $\mu$ is a median.
