**Central idea.** Condition on the sum: the ratio of the joint pmf to the Poisson$(\theta+\lambda)$ pmf of the sum collapses to a binomial pmf.

**Solution.** For $0 \le k \le n$,
$$P(X=k|X+Y=n) = \frac{e^{-\theta}\theta^k/k!\cdot e^{-\lambda}\lambda^{n-k}/(n-k)!}{e^{-(\theta+\lambda)}(\theta+\lambda)^n/n!} = \binom{n}{k}\left(\frac{\theta}{\theta+\lambda}\right)^k\left(\frac{\lambda}{\theta+\lambda}\right)^{n-k},$$
so $X|X+Y=n \sim \text{binomial}(n,\theta/(\theta+\lambda))$. By symmetry, $Y|X+Y=n \sim \text{binomial}(n,\lambda/(\theta+\lambda))$.
