**Central idea.** $-\log\prod X_i = \sum(-\log X_i) \sim \text{gamma}(n,1)$; the cdf is the Poisson–gamma duality sum, whose derivative telescopes to $(-\log t)^{n-1}/(n-1)!$.

**Solution.** Let $P = \prod_{i=1}^n X_i$ and $G = -\log P = \sum_{i=1}^n(-\log X_i) \sim \text{gamma}(n,1)$ (rate $1$). For $0<t<1$, $F_P(t) = P(G \ge -\log t) = \sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\frac{t(-\log t)^k}{k!}$. Differentiating (telescoping sum): $f_P(t) = \frac{(-\log t)^{\,n-1}}{(n-1)!}$, $0<t<1$.
