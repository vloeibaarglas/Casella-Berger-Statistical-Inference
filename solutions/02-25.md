**Central idea.** Evenness of the density is exactly invariance under $x\mapsto -x$, which passes to the mgf by the same change of variables.

**Solution.** (a) For continuous $X$, $f_{-X}(y)=f_X(-y)=f_X(y)$; identical pdfs give identical distributions. (b) $M_X(-t)=\int e^{-tx}f_X(x)dx$; with $u=-x$, $=\int e^{tu}f_X(-u)du=\int e^{tu}f_X(u)du=M_X(t)$.
