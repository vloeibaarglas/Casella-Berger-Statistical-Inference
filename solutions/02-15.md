**Central idea.** Pointwise, one of $X,Y$ is the max and the other the min, so their sum splits; linearity of expectation does the rest — no independence needed.

**Solution.** For every outcome, $\{X\vee Y,\,X\wedge Y\}=\{X,Y\}$ as multisets, so $X+Y=(X\vee Y)+(X\wedge Y)$ pointwise. Taking expectations (assumed to exist) and rearranging gives $E(X\vee Y)=EX+EY-E(X\wedge Y)$.
