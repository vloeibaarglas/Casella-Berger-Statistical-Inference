**Central idea.** $x^v$ times the gamma kernel is another gamma kernel; the normalizing constants give the ratio of gamma functions.

**Solution.** $EX^v = \frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha)\beta^\alpha}\int_0^\infty x^{\alpha+v-1}e^{-x/\beta}dx = \frac{\Gamma(\alpha+v)\beta^{\alpha+v}}{\Gamma(\alpha)\beta^\alpha} = \frac{\beta^v\Gamma(v+\alpha)}{\Gamma(\alpha)}$, valid for $v>-\alpha$ (in particular for $v>0$).
