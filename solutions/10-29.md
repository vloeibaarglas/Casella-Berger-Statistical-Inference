**Central idea.** (10.2.9) is Cauchy–Schwarz, with equality iff $\psi\propto l'$; for a location family the efficient choice is $\psi(u)=-\frac{d}{du}\log f(u)$.

**Solution.** (a) (10.2.9), $\mathrm{ARE}(\hat\theta_M,\hat\theta)=\frac{[E_\theta(\psi(X-\theta)l'(\theta\mid X))]^2}{E_\theta\psi^2\,E_\theta(l')^2}\le 1$, is Cauchy–Schwarz for the inner product $(g,h)\mapsto E_\theta[gh]$, with equality iff $\psi$ is a.s. proportional to $l'$. If $\psi=cl'$, the numerator is $c^2[E(l')^2]^2$ and the denominator $c^2E(l')^2\cdot E(l')^2$: ratio $1$.
(b) For location families $l'(\theta\mid x)=-\frac{f'(x-\theta)}{f(x-\theta)}$, so efficiency needs $\psi(u)\propto -\frac{d}{du}\log f(u)$. (i) $-\frac{d}{dx}(-x^2/2)=x=\psi$ ✓. (iii) $-\frac{d}{dx}[-\log(1+x^2)]=2x/(1+x^2)=\psi$ ✓. (iv) $-\frac{d}{dx}\log f = x$ ($|x|<c$), $c\,\mathrm{sgn}(x)$ ($|x|>c$) $=\max\{-c,\min(c,x)\}$ ✓. (ii) *As printed this is incorrect:* $-\frac{d}{dx}\log f = -\frac{d}{dx}[-x-2\log(1+e^{-x})] = \tanh(x/2)$, not $\tanh(x)$. The efficient $\psi$ for the stated logistic pdf is $\tanh(x/2)$; $\tanh(x)$ is not proportional to the score, so it is not asymptotically efficient. (Book erratum.)
