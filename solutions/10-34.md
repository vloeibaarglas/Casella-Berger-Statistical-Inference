**Central idea.** The Bernoulli LRT is a scaled Kullback–Leibler divergence $2n[\hat p\log(\hat p/p_0)+(1-\hat p)\log((1-\hat p)/(1-p_0))]$; simulation confirms the $\chi^2_1$ limit.

**Solution.** (a) $\lambda(x) = \frac{p_0^{S}(1-p_0)^{n-S}}{\hat p^{\,S}(1-\hat p)^{n-S}}$ with $S=\sum x_i$, $\hat p=S/n$. Hence
$$-2\log\lambda(x) = 2\left[S\log\frac{\hat p}{p_0}+(n-S)\log\frac{1-\hat p}{1-p_0}\right] = 2n\left[\hat p\log\frac{\hat p}{p_0}+(1-\hat p)\log\frac{1-\hat p}{1-p_0}\right].$$
(b) Simulating $M=20000$ samples with $n=20$, $p_0=0.5$: simulated mean $1.03$ (vs.\ $1$ for $\chi^2_1$), $P(-2\log\lambda>3.84)=0.043$ (vs.\ nominal $0.05$), and simulated $0.90/0.95/0.99$ quantiles $(3.29,3.29,7.71)$ vs.\ $\chi^2_1$ quantiles $(2.71,3.84,6.64)$ — reasonable agreement, slightly conservative in the tail, as expected for $n=20$.
