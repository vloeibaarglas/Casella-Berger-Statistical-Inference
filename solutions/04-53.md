**Central idea.** Real roots $\iff B^2 \ge 4AC$; condition on $A,C$ to get $\mathrm{E}[(1-2\sqrt{AC})_+]$, then $-\log A-\log C \sim \text{gamma}(2,1)$ reduces it to a one-dimensional integral.

**Solution.** Real roots iff the discriminant $B^2-4AC \ge 0$. Given $A,C$, $P(B \ge 2\sqrt{AC}|A,C) = (1-2\sqrt{AC})_+$. Put $S = -\log A-\log C \sim \text{gamma}(2,1)$ (rate $1$): $\sqrt{AC} = e^{-S/2}$, and
$$P = \int_{\log 4}^\infty (1-2e^{-s/2})se^{-s}\,ds = \frac{\log4+1}{4}-\left(\frac{\log4}{6}+\frac19\right) = \frac{\log4}{12}+\frac{5}{36} = \frac{5+\log64}{36} \approx 0.254.$$
