**Central idea.** Symmetry kills the odd moments on both sides; the even moments are matched by solving $EY^2=1$, $EY^4=3$ with a symmetric three-point law.

**Solution.** $EX^r=0$ for odd $r$ by symmetry; $EX^2=1$, $EX^4=3$. For $Y$: $EY^r=\frac16(\sqrt3)^r+\frac16(-\sqrt3)^r+\frac23\cdot0^r$, which is 0 for odd $r$; $EY^2=\frac16\cdot3+\frac16\cdot3=1$; $EY^4=\frac16\cdot9+\frac16\cdot9=3$. Hence $EX^r=EY^r$ for $r=1,\dots,5$.
