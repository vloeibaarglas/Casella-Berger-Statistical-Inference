**Central idea.** Fold at 0 as in Example 2.1.7; the odd part of $f_X$ cancels in the fold, and moments of $Y$ are even moments of $X$.

**Solution.** (a) For $0<y<1$,
$$f_Y(y)=\frac{1}{2\sqrt y}\big[f_X(\sqrt y)+f_X(-\sqrt y)\big]=\frac{1}{2\sqrt y}\cdot\tfrac12\big[(1+\sqrt y)+(1-\sqrt y)\big]=\frac{1}{2\sqrt y}.$$
(b) $EY=EX^2=\frac12\int_{-1}^1x^2(1+x)dx=\frac12\cdot\frac23=\frac13$ (the $x^3$ term integrates to 0). $EY^2=EX^4=\frac12\int_{-1}^1x^4dx=\frac15$. Thus $\mathrm{Var}Y=\frac15-\frac19=\frac{4}{45}$.
