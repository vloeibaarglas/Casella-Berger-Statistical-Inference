**Central idea.** Square both sides for the pointwise inequality, then take expectations and use monotonicity of $\mathrm{E}$.

**Solution.** (a) $|a+b|^2 = a^2+2ab+b^2 \le a^2+2|ab|+b^2 = (|a|+|b|)^2$; taking square roots gives $|a+b|\le|a|+|b|$.
(b) Pointwise $|X+Y|\le|X|+|Y|$; taking expectations and using Exercise 4.2(c): $\mathrm{E}|X+Y| \le \mathrm{E}[|X|+|Y|] = \mathrm{E}|X|+\mathrm{E}|Y|$.
