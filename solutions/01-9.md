**Central idea.** "Not in at least one" is "in none"; "not in all" is "missing from at least one" — element-chasing with quantifiers.

**Solution.** (a) $x \in (\cup_\alpha A_\alpha)^c \iff x \notin \cup_\alpha A_\alpha \iff$ there is no $\alpha$ with $x \in A_\alpha$ $\iff$ for every $\alpha$, $x \notin A_\alpha$ $\iff$ for every $\alpha$, $x \in A_\alpha^c$ $\iff x \in \cap_\alpha A_\alpha^c$. (b) $x \in (\cap_\alpha A_\alpha)^c \iff$ not (for every $\alpha$, $x \in A_\alpha$) $\iff$ there exists $\alpha$ with $x \notin A_\alpha$ $\iff x \in \cup_\alpha A_\alpha^c$.
