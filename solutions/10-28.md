**Central idea.** Check the joins at $x=\pm k$: $\rho$ matches ($k^2/2=k^2-k^2/2$) and $\rho'$ matches ($\pm k$ from both sides).

**Solution.** $\rho(x)=x^2/2$ for $|x|\le k$, $k|x|-k^2/2$ for $|x|\ge k$. At $x=k$: left value $k^2/2$, right value $k^2-k^2/2=k^2/2$; similarly at $-k$. So $\rho$ is continuous. $\rho'(x)=x$ for $|x|<k$ and $k\,\mathrm{sgn}(x)$ for $|x|>k$: at $x=k$ both one-sided limits are $k$; at $x=-k$ both are $-k$. So $\rho'$ is continuous (it is $\psi$ in (10.2.7)).
