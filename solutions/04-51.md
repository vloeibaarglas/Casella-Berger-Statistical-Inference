**Central idea.** Condition on the denominator: $P(X/Y\le t) = \int_0^1 P(X\le ty)\,dy$; for products use the cdf $P(XY\le t) = t-t\log t$ on $(0,1)$ and iterate.

**Solution.** (a) $P(X/Y\le t) = \int_0^1 \min(ty,1)\,dy$: for $0<t\le1$ this is $t/2$; for $t>1$ it is $\int_0^{1/t}ty\,dy+(1-1/t) = 1-\frac{1}{2t}$. $P(XY\le t) = \int_0^1\min(t/y,1)\,dy$: for $0<t<1$, $t+\int_t^1\frac{t}{y}dy = t-t\log t$ (and $1$ for $t\ge1$).
(b) $P(XY/Z\le t) = \mathrm{E}[P(XY\le tZ)]$. For $0<t<1$: $\int_0^1 tz(1-\log t-\log z)\,dz = \frac{t}{2}(1-\log t)+\frac{t}{4} = \frac{t(3-2\log t)}{4}$. For $t\ge1$: $\int_0^{1/t}tz(1-\log(tz))\,dz+(1-1/t) = \frac{3}{4t}+1-\frac1t = 1-\frac{1}{4t}$.
