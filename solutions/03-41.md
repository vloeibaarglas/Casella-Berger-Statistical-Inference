**Central idea.** "Stochastically increasing" = the cdf decreases pointwise in the parameter; for n$(\mu,\sigma^2)$ and the gamma scale this is a direct monotonicity check.

**Solution.** $\theta_1>\theta_2\Rightarrow F(x|\theta_1)\le F(x|\theta_2)$ for all $x$ is the requirement (stochastically greater means smaller cdf). (a) $F(x|\mu)=\Phi((x-\mu)/\sigma)$, which decreases in $\mu$ for fixed $x,\sigma$. (b) $F(x|\beta)=P(X\le x)=P(X/\beta\le x/\beta)=G(x/\beta)$, where $G$ is the gamma$(\alpha,1)$ cdf; for $x\ge0$, $x/\beta$ decreases in $\beta$ and $G$ is nondecreasing, so $F$ decreases in $\beta$ (for $x<0$, $F\equiv0$).
