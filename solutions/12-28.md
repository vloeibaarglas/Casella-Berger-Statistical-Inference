**Central idea.** Closed-form normal equation for the no-intercept model; the numerator is a CLT average and the denominator converges to $\sigma^2_x$ (with unit error variance as in Example 12.4.3).

**Solution.** Minimizing $\sum_i(y_i-\beta x_i)^2$ gives $-2\sum_ix_i(y_i-\beta x_i)=0$, i.e. $\hat\beta=\sum_ix_iy_i/\sum_ix_i^2$. Then
$$\sqrt n(\hat\beta-\beta)=\frac{\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_ix_i\epsilon_i}{\frac1n\sum_ix_i^2}.$$
The numerator $\to_d n(0,\sigma^2_\epsilon\sigma^2_x)$ by the CLT ($E[x_i\epsilon_i]=0$, $\mathrm{Var}= \sigma^2_\epsilon x_i^2$, $x_i$ bounded) and the denominator $\to_p\sigma^2_x$; with $\sigma^2_\epsilon=1$ as in Example 12.4.3, Slutsky's theorem gives $\sqrt n(\hat\beta-\beta)\to_d n(0,1/\sigma^2_x)$.
