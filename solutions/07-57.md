**Central idea.** Rao–Blackwellize the indicator on the complete sufficient total $S=\sum_{1}^{n+1}X_i$; given $S=s$ the vector is uniform over weight-$s$ binary strings.
**Solution.** (a) $E_p[T]=P_p(\sum_{1}^nX_i>X_{n+1})=h(p)$.
(b) $S=\sum_{i=1}^{n+1}X_i$ is complete sufficient; the UMVUE is $\varphi(s)=E[T\mid S=s]=P(\sum_{1}^nX_i>X_{n+1}\mid S=s)$. Given $S=s$: with probability $\binom{n}{s}/\binom{n+1}{s}=(n+1-s)/(n+1)$, $X_{n+1}=0$ (then $\sum_1^nX_i=s>0\iff s\ge1$); with probability $s/(n+1)$, $X_{n+1}=1$ (then $\sum_1^nX_i=s-1>1\iff s\ge3$). Hence $\varphi(0)=0$, $\varphi(s)=\frac{n+1-s}{n+1}$ for $s=1,2$, and $\varphi(s)=1$ for $s\ge3$ — the best unbiased estimator of $h(p)$.
