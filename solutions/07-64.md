**Central idea.** Independence plus product prior makes the posterior factor; the additive loss then separates into $n$ independent minimizations.
**Solution.** The posterior is $\pi(\theta\mid\mathbf{x})=\prod_{i=1}^n\pi_i(\theta_i\mid x_i)$ (independent likelihoods $\times$ product prior). The posterior expected loss of $\mathbf{a}=(a_1,\dots,a_n)$ is $E\big[\sum_iL(\theta_i,a_i)\mid\mathbf{x}\big]=\sum_iE[L(\theta_i,a_i)\mid x_i]$, minimized by minimizing each summand separately — i.e. $a_i=\delta_i^{\pi_i}(x_i)$. Hence the coordinate-wise rule is Bayes for the sum loss.
