**Central idea.** Minimize posterior expected loss pointwise: include $\theta$ iff its posterior density exceeds the length price $b$.

**Solution.** Posterior expected loss: $E[L(\theta,C)\mid x] = b\,\text{Len}(C) - P(\theta\in C\mid x) = \int_C [b - \pi(\theta\mid x)]\,d\theta$. This is minimized by including exactly those $\theta$ with $b - \pi(\theta\mid x) < 0$: $C^\pi = \{\theta : \pi(\theta\mid x) \ge b\}$ (the Neyman–Pearson argument).
