**Central idea.** Discretizing an exponential at the integers gives a (shifted) geometric law; conditioning on $\{Y \ge 5\} = \{X \ge 4\}$ and subtracting $4$ is exactly the memoryless property.

**Solution.** (a) $P(Y=i+1) = P(i \le X < i+1) = e^{-i}-e^{-(i+1)} = (1-e^{-1})(e^{-1})^i$, $i \ge 0$: $Y-1$ is geometric$(1-e^{-1})$ (failures), i.e. $Y$ is the trials-until-first-success version with success probability $1-e^{-1}$.
(b) $\{Y \ge 5\} = \{X \ge 4\}$, and by memorylessness $X-4|X \ge 4 \sim \text{exponential}(1)$. So $X-4|Y\ge5 \sim \text{exponential}(1)$.
