**Central idea.** A separable linear rescaling has constant Jacobian $1/(ac)$.

**Solution.** The transformation $(x,y) = ((u-b)/a,(v-d)/c)$ is one-to-one with Jacobian $\left|\frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(u,v)}\right| = \frac{1}{ac}$ (using $a,c>0$). By the transformation formula, $f_{U,V}(u,v) = f((u-b)/a,(v-d)/c)\cdot\frac{1}{ac}$.
