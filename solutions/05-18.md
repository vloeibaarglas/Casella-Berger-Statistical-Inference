**Central idea.** $X = Z/\sqrt{V/p}$ with $Z \sim n(0,1) \perp V \sim \chi^2_p$; as $p \to \infty$, $V/p \to 1$ in probability, so the $t$ collapses to the normal and $qF_{q,p} = U/(V/p) \to \chi^2_q$.

**Solution.** (a) $E[X] = E[Z]\,E[(V/p)^{-1/2}] = 0$ for $p > 1$ (for $p = 1$, Cauchy, no mean). $E[X^2] = E[Z^2]E[p/V] = p/(p-2)$ for $p > 2$, so $\mathrm{Var}\, X = p/(p-2)$ ($p > 2$).
(b) $X^2 = \dfrac{Z^2/1}{V/p} \sim F_{1,p}$ by definition of $F$ ($Z^2 \sim \chi^2_1 \perp V$).
(c) $f(x|p) = \frac{\Gamma((p+1)/2)}{\sqrt{p\pi}\,\Gamma(p/2)}\left(1+\frac{x^2}{p}\right)^{-(p+1)/2}$. By Stirling, $\frac{\Gamma(p/2+1/2)}{\Gamma(p/2)} \sim (p/2)^{1/2}$, so the constant $\to \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}}\cdot\frac{(p/2)^{1/2}}{\sqrt{p}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}$; and $(1+x^2/p)^{-(p+1)/2} \to e^{-x^2/2}$. Hence $f(x|p) \to \phi(x)$ pointwise.
(d) $X^2 \sim F_{1,p} = \frac{\chi^2_1/1}{\chi^2_p/p}$; since $\chi^2_p/p \to 1$ in probability (WLLN), Slutsky gives $X^2 \to_d \chi^2_1/1 = \chi^2_1$.
(e) $qF_{q,p} = \frac{\chi^2_q}{\chi^2_p/p} \to_d \chi^2_q$: conjecture $qF_{q,p} \to_d \chi^2_q$ as $p \to \infty$ (the $F$ test statistic becomes the chi-square statistic in large samples).
